# Rafael Ibarrondo

Head of the institute sequencing core. He chairs the [[sequencing-core-user-committee]], where Ines represents the computational and liquid biopsy users.

## Role

Rafael runs the core's instruments, queue and prices. He handled the QC of the 1,840-region targeted methylation panel for [[methylation-panel-design]] in 2025 and runs the cfDNA libraries that [[elias-moreau]] prepares. He sets the annual [[sequencing-core-price-list-2026]], reviewed by the committee.

## How We Work Together

Direct and practical. He prefers a sample forecast per quarter to ad hoc requests, and he will fit urgent runs in if the forecast was honest. [[priya-raman]] sends him the group forecast. Disagreements so far have been about price, not service: the committee pushed back on the 2026 increase for low-input libraries and he reduced it from 12% to 7%.

He manages the relationship with the instrument vendor, including [[ben-whitfield]]; Ines meets Ben only with Rafael or in committee settings.

## Current Threads

- Buffy coat deep sequencing at 180 EUR per sample, which matters for the [[clonal-hematopoiesis-filter]]; Ines wants a volume discount discussed at the next committee.
- Instrument replacement plan for 2027, on the agenda of [[sequencing-core-user-committee-2026-10-08]].

## History With The Group

Rafael ran the first cfDNA low-pass libraries for the group in 2024, before the [[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study]] started, and has been a reliable partner since. In 2025 the core absorbed the targeted methylation panel workflow, which needed two rounds of capture optimisation; his team caught a probe batch problem that would otherwise have cost a full run. The Halyard delivery change in September 2026 went smoothly because his team tested it with [[tobias-wren]] in August.

Where we differ: he sees the low-input surcharge as cost recovery for failed libraries, while Ines sees it as a price signal that penalises the very assays the institute says it wants. The June compromise holds for now. Keep this in mind when preparing the next price review in 2027.
