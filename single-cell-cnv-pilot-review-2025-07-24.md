# Single-Cell CNV Pilot Review 2025-07-24

Closing review of the [[single-cell-cnv-pilot]], one week before [[felix-achterberg]]'s last month. Present: Felix, Ines, [[amara-okonkwo]], [[tobias-wren]].

## Evidence

- Four tumours, 1,612 nuclei after QC.
- Major clades identical to bulk multi-region trees in all four.
- One extra minor subclone (about 4% of cells) in one tumour; no effect on the questions asked.
- About 11,500 EUR per tumour against about 1,900 EUR for one more bulk region ([[single-cell-cnv-cost-comparison]]).

## Decision

Do not add single-cell copy number to group studies. Reconsider if the cost falls below about 3,000 EUR per tumour, or if a study needs subclones below 5% that bulk and spatial data cannot provide.
