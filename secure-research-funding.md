# Secure Research Funding

Ines answers for keeping the group funded: writing applications, reporting on awarded grants and keeping a working relationship with programme officers. It has no end date because the group's salaries and sequencing budgets always run on two or three overlapping grants.

## Scope

- Grant applications where Ines is lead or co-applicant, from first call reading to submission and resubmission.
- Reporting and amendments for awarded grants, and the contact with programme officers at the [[rosa-lindgren|Aurora Cancer Foundation]] and the [[thabo-nkosi|Frontier Research Fund]].
- The internal route through the grants office with [[chloe-armitage]], including budget checks and director sign-off.
- Not here: the science itself. The studies a grant funds stay under [[tumour-evolution-research]]; a grant Project links to them rather than repeating them. Halo consortium money is handled under [[halo-consortium-work]].

## How Ines Works Here

Grant writing gets protected mornings, Tuesday and Thursday, from six weeks before a deadline. Every application of more than 500 k EUR goes through a mock panel; the reasons are in [[mock-panels-are-worth-the-time]]. Budgets are built by the grants office from a sheet Ines and Priya fill in, never the other way round, because the office knows the overhead and salary scale rules that change each January.
