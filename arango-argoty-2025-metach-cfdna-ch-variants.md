# An artificial intelligence-based model for prediction of clonal hematopoiesis variants in cell-free DNA samples.

## Summary

An open-source machine-learning framework that classifies variants found in plasma cfDNA as CH-derived or tumour-derived without matched white blood cell sequencing, reported to outperform earlier classification approaches.

## Why It Matters

Lucas read it with [[hannah-lindqvist]] because it bears on both uses of CH calls. For the [[clonal-hematopoiesis-filter]] it is a candidate alternative to buffy coat sequencing. For Northvale it raises the question whether plasma-only classification could flag CH contribution in the 1,230 case-control plasma samples, where we have no buffy coat sequencing outside the subcohort ([[can-ch-explain-part-of-the-northvale-methylation-signal]]).

## Caveats

Brief report; trained on variant data, not methylation.
