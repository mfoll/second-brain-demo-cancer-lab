# Guidelines for performing Mendelian randomization investigations: update for summer 2023.

Burgess S et al., Wellcome Open Research (first version 2019, updated 2023). Methods article.

## Summary

Community guidelines for doing and reporting Mendelian randomization, written for analysts and for the editors and reviewers who assess MR papers. Ten sections run from motivation and data sources through variant selection and harmonisation, primary and robust sensitivity analyses, extensions, presentation and interpretation. The authors commit to updating it at least every two years.

## Why It Matters

This is our checklist. [[hiroshi-tanabe]] put it first on his list ([[mr-reading-list-hiroshi-tanabe]]) and [[lucas-brandt]] is structuring the draft analysis plan for a CH MR around its sections. Ines also used it when declining an MR review for [[lena-vogt]]: she checked whether she could assess the paper against these sections and concluded she could not do the robust-methods part justice.

## Key Details

- Harmonisation of effect alleles between exposure and outcome datasets gets its own section; palindromic variants are a known trap.
- Robust methods are presented as a set to be reported together, not a menu to pick the most favourable from.
- Interpretation section warns against reading MR estimates as the effect of a short-term intervention.

## Caveats

Guidelines, not evidence. Several recommendations assume large two-sample summary statistics; a one-sample analysis in Northvale would need adaptation.
