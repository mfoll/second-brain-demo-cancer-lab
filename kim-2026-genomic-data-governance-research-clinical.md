# Genomic Data Governance Challenges Between Research and Clinical Care: A Scoping Review.

Kim A, Merner B, Taylor M, et al. Health Expectations, 2026.

## Summary

A scoping review of 43 empirical, legal and commentary articles on how genomic data governance differs between research and clinical care. Communities and the professional workforce reported different consent priorities and different expectations of benefit; legal articles stressed how regulatory regimes shape practice; all commentary articles were written by researchers, none by community representatives. The authors conclude that meaningful community involvement is needed in governance frameworks.

## Why It Matters

Halo sits exactly on the research-clinical boundary: patient-tier samples come from routine care with research consent. [[eva-novak]] sent it to the site PIs ahead of the next steering committee, together with [[isabel-montes]]'s proposal to involve the patient advisory panel in the consortium paper. It also gives Ines a reference for the community-involvement point in [[data-access-policy-revision]] discussions.

## Caveats

Written to inform one national framework; we use only its general findings. No technical guidance on data transfer.
