# Frontier Interview 2025-10-14

Step-two interview for the [[frontier-starting-grant]], held online: ten-minute presentation, twenty minutes of questions from a panel of seven. [[thabo-nkosi]] attended as panel secretary.

## Questions Asked

- Why 40 metastases, and how many are paired before and after chemotherapy? Ines answered 40 patients, 28 with paired resections, with numbers from the tissue unit run by [[benedikt-kraus]].
- Is Visium HD resolution enough? Answered with the completed [[visium-hd-pilot]]: 8 µm bins recover the main cell populations, not rare immune subsets.
- How will low-count spots be handled? Ines described the [[deconvolution-benchmark]] started on 2025-09-01, which was the third reviewer's main concern in the [[frontier-reviewer-reports-2025]].
- Data release: asked twice. The answer (controlled access after publication, a browser later) was weaker than it should have been.

## Impressions

The panel was engaged and mostly friendly; one member pushed hard on whether a computational group can secure tissue access. Practice with [[jonas-eriksen]] and [[amara-okonkwo]] the week before covered every question except data release. Slides: [[frontier-interview-slides-2025]].
