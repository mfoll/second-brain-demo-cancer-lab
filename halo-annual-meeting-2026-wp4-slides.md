# Halo Annual Meeting 2026 WP4 Slides

Ines's WP4 opening talk on day two of the [[halo-annual-meeting-2026-06-10]], 24 slides, followed by a joint fragmentomics session by [[dmitri-halvorsen]] and [[fatima-benali]] (14 slides, same file).

## Summary

Three messages. First, the pilot panel worked as a dry run: all six sites returned data within five weeks and the hub format held. Second, both fragmentomics assays misbehave on sheared genomic DNA, so contrived material cannot score them; slide 11 shows the size distributions of nucleosomal reference, sheared DNA and three patient plasma samples overlaid. Third, the patient tier is where the clinical answer is, and it depends on every site's data rules being in place by the time the data arrive.

## Key Details

- Slide 6: pilot detection table, 12 samples by 6 assays.
- Slide 11: size profiles; the sheared DNA has no 167 bp peak.
- Slide 15: proposed rule, fragmentomics assays scored only on patient and donor tiers. Adopted.
- Slide 19: data-flow diagram from site to hub to WP4, with Corvane's box marked "policy pending". This slide is the first time the block was shown to the whole consortium.
- Slide 22: timeline to the consortium paper, now outdated.

## Caveats

Pilot data only, 12 samples; numbers on slide 6 were superseded by [[halo-interim-benchmark-report-2026-10]].
