# Halo Steering Committee Minutes 2025-11-20

Approved minutes of the third Halo steering committee, circulated by [[maximilian-auer]] on 2025-12-03. Eight pages.

## Key Details

- Item 3, truth set: draft plan (contrived only) not approved, five votes to one. Recorded reason: "no clinical endpoint". WP3 and WP4 to return with a three-tier design by end of January.
- Item 4, fragment features: Corvane and Thornvale to deliver a joint definition document by March.
- Item 5, annual meeting 2026: Corvane to host, June.
- Item 6, budget: WP3 reference-material budget raised from 18,000 to 26,000 EUR to allow four replicates per level.
- Item 7, any other business: [[eva-novak]] reminded partners that article 9 of the agreement requires internal rules permitting multilateral access. Corvane noted that its policy was under informal review. This is the first written mention of the issue.
