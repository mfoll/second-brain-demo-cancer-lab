# MRD Interim Analysis Meeting 2025-12-03

Monthly MRD study meeting with the first full interim results on all extracted samples. Present: Ines, Dmitri, Sofia, Hannah, Elias, [[samuel-okoye|Samuel Okoye]] (video), [[maja-kovac|Maja Kovac]] as statistical adviser.

## Results Presented

- Landmark day-28 call (variants, methylation and gradient-boosting fragmentomics combined): 71% of recurrences detected, 92% of non-recurrent patients negative, on the data then available.
- Serial monitoring: median lead time over imaging 4.3 months.
- Fragmentomics added most for the 23 patients without tumour-informed variants.

## Discussion

- Samuel: a 4-month lead time matters only if it changes management, for example earlier second-line treatment or eligibility for a trial. That became [[what-lead-time-over-imaging-is-clinically-meaningful]].
- Maja: lead time against three-monthly imaging is interval-censored; a naive median overstates it. She proposed a sensitivity analysis. It went into the analysis plan ([[mrd-statistical-analysis-plan-v2]]) but not into the submitted main analysis, and Reviewer 3 later asked for exactly this.
- Data freeze set for 2025-12-31; writing to start in February.

## Decisions

- Write the manuscript on the 2025-12-31 freeze ([[mrd-manuscript]]).
- Combine all three signal layers in the main MRD call ([[should-the-mrd-call-combine-methylation-and-fragmentomics]]).
- Report CA19-9 in a secondary table only ([[should-we-report-ca19-9-alongside-cfdna]]).
