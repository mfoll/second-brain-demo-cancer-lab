# MR Reading List From Hiroshi Tanabe

Annotated list of about 25 papers that [[hiroshi-tanabe]] emailed to [[lucas-brandt]] and Ines on 2025-11-28, the day after the first call. Ordered as he suggested reading them.

## Structure

1. Foundations and reporting: [[burgess-2019-guidelines-mendelian-randomization]] first.
2. Bias from selection, survival and stratification: [[gkatzionis-2019-selection-bias-mendelian-randomization]], [[smit-2019-survival-bias-mendelian-randomization]], [[coscia-2022-collider-bias-stratified-mendelian-randomization]].
3. Pleiotropy-robust methods: [[grant-2024-bayesian-mendelian-randomization-correlated-pleiotropy]] and two others; later he added [[tang-2025-pleiotropic-clustering-mendelian-randomization]] and [[van-der-graaf-2025-mr-link-2]].
4. Applied examples in cancer, chosen to show pitfalls rather than results.

## His Annotations Worth Keeping

- "Write the analysis plan before you look at any estimate."
- "Report all robust methods together; disagreement between them is information."
- For CH specifically: start from instrument biology, not from p-values.
