# How Should Research Engineering Time Be Funded?

## Question

[[tobias-wren]]'s position is on institute core funding until 2027-12. Grants fund projects, not pipeline maintenance. How do we keep at least one full-time engineer for the group after 2027?

## Options

- Costed engineering line in the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]]: about 0.5 FTE for five years. Aurora allows it if tied to programme deliverables.
- [[nikolai-petrov]]'s proposed shared RSE pool, funded by a levy on institute grants, with guaranteed time per group. Not yet approved by the director.
- Charging Halo for pipeline support for the cross-site work. Legally awkward; [[eva-novak]] doubts the consortium agreement allows it.
- Splitting across several grants at 0.2 FTE each. Rejected by Tobias and Ines: fragmentation was the problem in 2024.

## Current Answer

Pursue the first two in parallel. The Aurora budget has a 0.5 FTE engineering line in the current draft.
