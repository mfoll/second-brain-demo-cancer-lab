# Halo Data Sharing Agreement Template

## Summary

The multi-party data sharing agreement template for the Halo consortium, version 1.3, maintained by [[eva-novak]]. Four of the six sites have signed it; Corvane cannot until its own policy allows consortium transfer ([[data-access-policy-revision]]).

## Key Details

- One agreement for all members, with a schedule per use (purpose, dataset, recipients, duration).
- Data hosted in a secure analysis environment at Brightwater University Hospital, coordinated by [[maximilian-auer]].
- Each schedule needs approval by the contributing site's own committee. This is compatible with the per-use approval in [[data-access-policy-draft-3]].
- No onward transfer; deletion at the end of each schedule; annual access audit.
- Clause 7 allows a site to contribute derived features instead of reads.

## Why It Matters

Eva confirmed on 2026-07-21 that clause 2.4 can reference "each member institution's consortium transfer policy" once ours is adopted, so no renegotiation is needed. The first schedule Corvane would sign is the [[halo-cross-site-benchmark]] patient tier: Halo post-resection data and clinical follow-up coming to Corvane for the MRD external validation, plus the St Aldric contribution.

## Caveats

The template says nothing about registry-derived outcomes; that point has to be settled on our side with the registry.
