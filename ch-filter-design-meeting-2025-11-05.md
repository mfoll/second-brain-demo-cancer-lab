# CH Filter Design Meeting 2025-11-05

Design meeting for the [[clonal-hematopoiesis-filter|Clonal Hematopoiesis Filter]]. Present: Ines, [[hannah-lindqvist|Hannah Lindqvist]], [[dmitri-halvorsen|Dmitri Halvorsen]], [[lucas-brandt|Lucas Brandt]], [[rafael-ibarrondo|Rafael Ibarrondo]] for costs.

## Options Discussed

1. Matched buffy coat sequencing for all 212 patients: about 180 EUR per sample at the panel depth we need, so about 38,000 EUR ([[buffy-coat-sequencing-cost-estimate-2025-11]]). Reference standard, but no budget line for it.
2. Panel of normals only, built from the Northvale CH calls Lucas was starting to produce for [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]]. Nearly free, but untested at the VAFs we care about.
3. A pilot: matched buffy coat for 60 patients, chosen to include all patients with any plasma variant below 1% VAF at day 28, and use it to measure what the panel of normals misses.

## Decisions

- Option 3. Cost about 10,800 EUR from the study's consumables budget.
- One CH caller for both projects, developed here and reused for the Northvale subcohort calls (later frozen as [[ch-subcohort-variant-calls]]). Lucas and Hannah co-own it.
- Gene list agreed ([[ch-filter-gene-list]]), including PPM1D and TP53. Tumour-informed variants override the filter.
- Decision on the remaining 152 patients after the pilot.

## Dissent

Dmitri argued for option 1 straight away, on the grounds that a false molecular recurrence is worse than the cost ([[matched-normal-sequencing-is-cheaper-than-false-recurrence]]). Ines preferred to measure first. The question stayed open: [[do-we-still-need-matched-buffy-coat-sequencing]].
