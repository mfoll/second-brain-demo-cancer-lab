# Corvane Conflict Of Interest Policy

The institute policy on declaring and managing interests, 2024 version. Consulted on 2026-07-03 when Ines declared the contact with [[ravi-vellanki]].

## Key Details

- Staff declare financial interests, advisory roles and ongoing commercial discussions that could affect institute decisions, on the online register, within 30 days.
- Committee members must also declare at the start of each relevant item and do not vote on it.
- Gifts and hospitality above a nominal value are declined or declared; sponsored travel needs the director's prior approval.
- Commercial discussions about institute intellectual property go through the technology transfer office.
