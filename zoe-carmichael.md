# Zoe Carmichael

Clinical scientist in molecular diagnostics at a hospital genomics laboratory; guest lecturer on the [[cancer-genomics-course-2026]]. External contact, no affiliation hub in this vault.

## Role

Zoe gave the guest lecture on reporting genomic results to clinicians on 2026-02-18, in the liquid biopsy week ([[course-week-liquid-biopsy-2026-02-16]]). She showed real (anonymised) report formats and walked through how a variant of uncertain significance is worded for an oncologist.

## Lecture Content

Her session covered three things students rarely see: how a clinical report is structured so that the actionable finding is on the first line, how laboratories word uncertain or low-level findings, and why a research cfDNA result cannot simply be passed to a treating clinician. The last point led to a useful discussion of the difference between research MRD calls in the [[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study]] and an accredited clinical test.
