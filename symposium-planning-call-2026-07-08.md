# Symposium Planning Call 2026-07-08

Abstract selection call for the [[tumour-evolution-symposium-2026]]. Ines, [[oluwaseun-adebayo]] and the two external scorers; 90 minutes.

## Decisions

- From 112 abstracts, 16 short talks and 58 posters selected using the scores in [[symposium-abstract-scoring-sheet]]. 9 of the 16 talks are early-career, above the target of 8.
- New session on clonal expansions in normal tissue, because six strong CH and somatic mosaicism abstracts would otherwise have been split across sessions. Session 6 on methods added at the same time ([[symposium-programme-draft-v3]]).
- The four abstracts from Ines's group were scored by others; two became talks ([[sofia-reyes-calderon]], [[amara-okonkwo]]) and two posters ([[kenji-mori]], [[hannah-lindqvist]]). Ines left the call for that item.
- Registration fee confirmed at 80 EUR for non-students, free for students.
