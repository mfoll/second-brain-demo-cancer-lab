# New Candidate Reference Measurement Procedures for MET CNV Detection and Quantification Using Digital PCR.

Petiti J, Caria S, Revel L, et al. Biological Procedures Online, 2026.

## Summary

Describes a duplex droplet digital PCR assay for MET copy number against a diploid reference gene, optimised on synthetic constructs, cell lines and reference materials, and proposed as a candidate reference measurement procedure. Reported linearity was high and run-to-run variability low, with measurement uncertainty estimated in line with metrological practice. The motivation is that current copy-number methods lack traceability, which hampers comparison between laboratories.

## Why It Matters

[[sun-hee-park]] shared it while we drafted the [[halo-wp4-analysis-plan]] as an example of reporting measurement uncertainty explicitly, and as a reminder that ddPCR can anchor other assays. It shaped the idea, still under discussion, of using Kelstow's ddPCR results as the reference value for the mutation-dilution tier once they arrive.

## Caveats

Tissue and cell-line copy number, not cfDNA; we cite it for its reporting approach, not for cfDNA performance.
