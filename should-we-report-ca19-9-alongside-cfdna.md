# Should We Report CA19-9 Alongside cfDNA?

Should the [[mrd-manuscript|MRD Manuscript]] compare the cfDNA call with serum CA19-9, the standard pancreatic tumour marker?

## Current Answer

Yes, as a secondary comparison, not as a co-primary endpoint. Decided at the [[mrd-interim-analysis-meeting-2025-12-03|2025-12-03 interim meeting]].

## Reasoning

- [[samuel-okoye|Samuel Okoye]] argued that clinicians will ask whether cfDNA adds anything over CA19-9, which is already measured.
- CA19-9 is missing or uninformative for about 18% of St Aldric patients (non-secretors or missing values; see [[st-aldric-data-dictionary-v3]]), so a head-to-head primary comparison would lose patients.
- Reviews of pancreatic ctDNA note that serial ctDNA improves recurrence prediction beyond CA19-9 in several series ([[ojha-2025-ctdna-pancreatic-critical-appraisal]]); a secondary table lets readers judge it in our data.

In the submitted version, cfDNA preceded a CA19-9 rise in 41 of the 77 recurrent patients whose CA19-9 was informative and rose before or at recurrence.
