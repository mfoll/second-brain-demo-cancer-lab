# Serial Cell-Free DNA Detects Pancreatic Cancer Recurrence Months Before Imaging After Resection

Halvorsen D, Reyes Calderon S, Lindqvist H, Moreau E, Kovac M, Okoye S, Varga I. Preprint posted 2026-03-03, same version as submitted to Tumour Evolution Reports on 2026-02-27.

## Summary

Serial post-operative plasma from 212 resected pancreatic cancer patients, analysed with tumour-informed variants, a 1,840-region methylation panel and a gradient-boosting fragmentomics classifier. Day-28 combined call: 71% sensitivity for recurrence, 92% specificity. Serial monitoring: median lead time 4.3 months over three-monthly CT.

- Lead time not corrected for interval censoring.
