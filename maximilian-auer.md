# Maximilian Auer

Halo coordinator, based at Brightwater University Hospital, which is the consortium's coordinating centre. He runs Work Package 1 (coordination and management), holds the grant budget, and is the person Ines goes to when a work package needs a decision before the next steering committee.

## Role

- Scientific coordinator of Halo since the kick-off on 2025-03-18; signatory of the consortium agreement for Brightwater ([[halo-consortium-agreement-summary]]).
- Sets the steering committee agenda with [[henrik-dahl]], who chairs it.
- Oversees the coordinating-centre team: [[ngozi-adeyemi]] (data manager) and [[eva-novak]] (legal and ethics).
- Clinically he is a gastroenterologist; Brightwater contributed 36 of the 60 patient samples in the [[halo-truth-set]].

## How We Work Together

Short calls rather than long emails; he prefers a one-page summary with a recommended option before any steering committee decision. He is pragmatic about deadlines but firm about the consortium agreement: he will not let one site get a different data deal from the others. That is why the Corvane policy issue matters to him. He has been patient with it, but at the [[halo-steering-committee-2026-09-17]] he asked for a written date by which Corvane expects the revised policy, which we could not give.

He co-planned the [[halo-annual-meeting-2026]] with Ines and opened it on 2026-06-10.

## Current Threads

- Patient-tier analysis waiting on the Corvane policy ([[halo-data-flow-and-transfer-constraints]]).
- The federated proposal: he is supportive in principle but wants it costed and wants WP1 budget kept out of it until the steering committee agrees ([[federated-cfdna-learning]]).
- Chasing Kelstow's late truth-set data with [[sun-hee-park]].
- Consortium paper authorship rules, still in draft.

## History With The Group

Ines first met Maximilian when Brightwater and Corvane wrote the Halo proposal together in 2024; he asked her to lead WP4 because Corvane was the only partner with both a fragmentomics assay and a statistics group. He visited the cfDNA wet lab during the annual meeting and has since suggested Brightwater as a second MRD site for future grants ([[should-aurora-include-brightwater-as-second-mrd-site]]).
