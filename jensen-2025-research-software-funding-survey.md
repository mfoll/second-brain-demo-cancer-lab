# Evolving funding strategies for research software: Insights from an international survey of research funders.

Jensen EA, Katz DS. PLOS ONE, 2025.

## Summary

Survey of international research funders on how they support research software, what problems their programmes target, and how successful they think they are. Funding covers a wide range, from open-source projects and open-science tools to infrastructure, data science and AI tools. Funders report working research software into their formal definitions of research and their funding models.

## Why It Matters

Read in 2026-06 when the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]] budget was first drafted. Ines wanted to know whether asking for a share of a research software engineer is normal. [[chloe-armitage]] confirmed the Aurora Cancer Foundation allows it as a staff cost but has no dedicated software line. The idea of a post shared with [[nikolai-petrov]]'s team came from this.

## Caveats

Funder self-reports; no information on our two funders specifically.
