# Friday 31 October 2025

## Registry linkage agreement signed

Margarethe signed the institute-level agreement with the [[northern-cancer-registry|Northern Cancer Registry]], which closes the [[registry-linkage-agreement|Registry Linkage Agreement]] project after six and a half months. What it unlocks is recorded in the [[registry-linkage-agreement-signed-2025-10-31|signature note]] and the terms in the [[registry-linkage-agreement-summary|agreement summary]].

The practical change for us is that outcome follow-up for Northvale and for the St Aldric MRD patients now runs under one agreement, with refreshes on request instead of one application per study. That matters for the [[northvale-early-detection-study|Northvale study]], the [[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study|MRD study]] survival analyses and the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis|CH risk analysis]].

## Working rhythm with the registry

Daniel Asante and I agreed to talk before each refresh request to fix the variable list and the cut-off date. Informal, but it should save a round of corrections each time.

## Onward transfer clause

We accepted the clause on onward transfer of registry-derived variables as written. Fine for now; it may need revisiting if we ever share linked outcomes beyond Corvane.
