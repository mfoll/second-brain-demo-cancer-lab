# Halo Pilot Panel Results 2026-04

WP4 report on the dry-run pilot panel: 12 contrived samples shipped on 2026-03-23 to all six sites and sequenced in April. Written by Ines and [[sun-hee-park]], dated 2026-04-30.

## Summary

The logistics worked; the analytics raised one real problem. All six sites returned data by 2026-04-27 in the hub format. Mutation assays detected the 1% and 0.5% samples; the two fragmentomics assays produced tumour-fraction estimates on the sheared genomic DNA samples that were unrelated to the nominal VAF.

## Key Details

- Panel: 4 nucleosomal dilutions (0%, 0.1%, 0.5%, 1%), 4 sheared genomic DNA samples (0%, 1%, two each), 4 methylation fractions.
- Corvane's shallow-WGS fragmentomics scores: nucleosomal 1% sample scored low but plausible; both sheared samples scored as high as a typical recurrence patient, regardless of mutant fraction, because their size profile has no nucleosomal peak and a long tail.
- Thornvale's size selection removed most of the sheared DNA, leaving too little library for two samples.
- Methylation fractions recovered within 2 percentage points by Hollinmoor.

## Why It Matters

This is the evidence behind [[commutability-of-reference-materials]] as a reported finding and behind the rule, adopted at the [[halo-annual-meeting-2026-06-10]], that fragmentomics assays are scored only on plasma tiers.
