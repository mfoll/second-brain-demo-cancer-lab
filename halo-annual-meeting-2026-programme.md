# Halo Annual Meeting 2026 Programme

Final programme for the Halo annual meeting at Corvane, 2026-06-10 to 2026-06-12, version of 2026-05-27, prepared by [[priya-raman]] with [[maximilian-auer]] and [[henrik-dahl]].

## Key Details

- Day 1 (Wednesday): welcome by [[margarethe-sell]]; coordinator's report; six site updates of 15 minutes; truth-set shipment debrief by [[ngozi-adeyemi]]; lab tour of the cfDNA wet lab with [[elias-moreau]].
- Day 2 (Thursday): WP4 day. Pilot-panel results, fragmentomics joint session, breakout groups on endpoints (led by [[isabel-montes]]) and on statistics (led by [[sun-hee-park]]); consortium dinner.
- Day 3 (Friday morning): WP5 data governance and site data rules ([[eva-novak]]); consortium paper planning; closed steering committee session.
- Two patient-advisory observers attended days 1 and 2.

## Why It Matters

The programme shows where decisions were expected to fall, which helps reading the minutes in [[halo-annual-meeting-2026-06-10]]. The data-rules slot on day 3 was moved earlier from the original draft after Ngozi's warning about Corvane's credentials.
