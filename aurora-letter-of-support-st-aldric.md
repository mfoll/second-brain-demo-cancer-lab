# Aurora Letter Of Support St Aldric

## Summary

Draft letter of support from [[samuel-okoye]], pancreatic surgeon and clinical lead of the MRD study at St Aldric University Hospital, for aim 2 of the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]]. Ines sent a draft on 2026-10-21; Samuel will edit and sign on hospital letterhead.

- Commits to continued recruitment of post-surgery patients for a prospective extension of 150, at about 60 resections a year.
- Confirms two 10 mL plasma draws per visit at 1, 3, 6, 12 and 18 months, plus imaging per standard follow-up.
- Mentions access to the Northern Cancer Registry outcome linkage under the existing institute agreement ([[registry-linkage-agreement]]).
