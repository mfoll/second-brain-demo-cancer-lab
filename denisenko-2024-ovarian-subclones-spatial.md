# Spatial transcriptomics reveals discrete tumour microenvironments and autocrine loops within ovarian cancer subclones.

Denisenko E et al., Nature Communications, 2024.

## Summary

Uses Visium on high-grade serous ovarian carcinoma to identify several tumour subclones with different copy-number alterations within single sections, then shows that subclones express different ligands and receptors and associate with different stromal and immune populations. Single-molecule imaging on one sample supports subclone-specific neighbourhoods and autocrine loops.

## Why It Matters

Proof of principle for what the atlas does: subclones defined from expression-inferred copy number, then linked to their neighbourhoods. It is the reference [[amara-okonkwo]] uses when explaining why we do not need single-cell DNA for the atlas ([[can-spatial-cnv-replace-single-cell-dna]]).

## Caveats

Standard Visium spots and a genomically unstable tumour with large copy-number events; colorectal metastases may have fewer distinguishing events.
