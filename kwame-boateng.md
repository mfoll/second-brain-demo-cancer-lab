# Kwame Boateng

Computational pathology researcher in the digital pathology unit at Brightwater University Hospital. Builds and evaluates deep learning models on routine H&E slides, mostly colorectal and gastric. External contact, no affiliation hub in the vault.

## How We Met

[[benedikt-kraus]] invited him to give the institute pathology seminar on 2026-01-28, on what slide foundation models can and cannot do in routine diagnostics. Ines and [[amara-okonkwo]] went; the [[pathology-foundation-models]] cluster was created the next day with his slides as the first Source ([[kwame-boateng-seminar-slides-2026-01]]).

## What He Brings

- Practical experience running several public foundation models on hospital slides, including the failure modes: scanner differences, pen marks, folded tissue, and slides from before a stain protocol change.
- A healthy scepticism about vendor benchmarks. He recommended [[campanella-2025-clinical-benchmark-pathology-foundation-models]] as the most honest comparison.
- Access to a colorectal slide set with matched molecular data at Brightwater. We have not asked for it and would need a formal agreement.
