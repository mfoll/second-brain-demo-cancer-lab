# Mei-Lin Zhou

Epidemiologist and principal investigator of the [[northvale-health-cohort]]. She is the cohort's scientific owner and our main external collaborator on the [[northvale-early-detection-study]] and the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]]. She is not at Corvane, so she has no affiliation hub in this vault.

## Role in our work

Mei-Lin approves every use of Northvale data and samples. Under the [[northvale-collaboration-agreement]] she is a co-author on both studies, signs off analysis plans, and must approve any outward communication of results. She co-signed the [[northvale-statistical-analysis-plan]] on 2026-02-26 and the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis-plan]] in December 2025.

She knows the cohort's history better than any document: which recruitment centre changed its blood-processing protocol in 2006, why the 2005 questionnaire has two versions of the smoking question ([[northvale-smoking-history-derivation]]), and which freezer moves happened when.

## Positions she holds

- Reverse causation: she prefers a 6-month exclusion window rather than the 1-year exclusion the group adopted on 2026-06-24. Her argument is that pancreatic cancer progresses fast enough that a year throws away informative cases, and that the window analysis already shows the gradient. The group kept 1 year for the main model and reports her 6-month model as a named sensitivity analysis. See [[northvale-analysis-meeting-2026-06-24]] and [[how-do-we-handle-reverse-causation-in-the-northvale-lead-time-analysis]].
- New cases from the 2025 registry extract: she would rather spend them on an independent validation set than enlarge the current study.
- She is cautious about language. Drafts must not say "predicts cancer" where the data show an association in stored samples ([[prediagnostic-detection-is-not-screening-evidence]] is partly her influence).

## How we work together

- Monthly Northvale analysis meeting by video; she joins for the first 45 minutes.
- She replies to email within a week and prefers a one-page summary with the decision asked of her at the top.
- Requests to the [[corvane-biobank]] go out under her name with [[maja-kovac]] as contact ([[requesting-northvale-samples]]).
