# Clonal hematopoiesis of indeterminate potential and the risk of breast cancer: a UK Biobank cohort study.

## Summary

A cohort analysis of about half a million biobank participants with exome sequencing, using logistic and Cox models to relate CHIP to breast cancer. CHIP was associated with a modestly higher risk (odds ratio around 1.2), and the association persisted after adjustment for lifestyle, inflammation, health status and genetic factors.

## Why It Matters

Sets expectations for effect size in the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]]: an effect around 1.2 for a common cancer is plausible (the paper reports odds ratios), so our subcohort of 6,000 will be underpowered for single sites and the plan reports any solid cancer as the primary outcome.

## Caveats

Very large sample; small relative effects may reflect residual confounding by age.
