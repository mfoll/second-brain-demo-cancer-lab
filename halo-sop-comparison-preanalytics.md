# Halo SOP Comparison Pre-Analytics

A WP2 table comparing blood collection and plasma processing at the six sites, compiled from each site's SOP in January and February 2026. Circulated before the [[halo-steering-committee-2026-02-26]].

## Key Details

- Tubes: three sites used cfDNA preservative tubes, three used EDTA.
- Time to plasma: from under 2 hours (Corvane, Hollinmoor) to up to 24 hours at room temperature for EDTA at one site.
- Centrifugation: all double spin, but second-spin speed varied from 1,600 g to 16,000 g.
- Storage: plasma at -80 °C everywhere; extracted DNA at -20 °C at two sites.
- Extraction: four different kits.

## Why It Matters

Processing delay in EDTA releases white-cell DNA and dilutes tumour signal, and it shifts fragment-size profiles, which hurts fragmentomics most. This table is why the steering committee chose one harmonised protocol for the truth set and a statistical adjustment for legacy samples ([[should-halo-harmonise-preanalytics-or-correct-statistically]]). [[dohmen-2026-preanalytical-cfdna-evdna]], read later, gives comparable evidence on delay and tube type.
