# Foundational Competencies and Responsibilities of a Research Software Engineer: Current State and Suggestions for Future Directions.

Goth F et al., F1000Research, 2024.

## Summary

Community paper defining what a research software engineer (RSE) is, the range of roles from researcher-like to industry-like, and a list of foundational competencies and values that describe the general profile.

## Why It Matters

[[nikolai-petrov]] sent it when Ines asked how a shared RSE post would be described in a grant. It also helped frame [[tobias-wren]]'s 2026 appraisal: his role sits near the engineering end, and the paper gave language for recognising that work as a career path rather than support.

## Caveats

Descriptive; competency lists do not settle grading or pay, which is the institute's question.
