# Multiregional profiling reveals THBS1-SPP1 monocyte-macrophage axis drives immunosuppression and outcome in colorectal liver metastases.

Bellomo G et al., Science Advances, 2026.

## Summary

Multiregional sampling of colorectal liver metastases defines immune zones: active cytotoxic T cells in normal liver, dysfunctional T cells in the tumour core, and a pre-exhausted zone at the margin rich in THBS1-positive monocytes. Trajectory analysis suggests these monocytes become SPP1-positive macrophages that accumulate in the core and suppress T cells; SPP1-positive macrophage abundance correlates with exhaustion and poor survival.

## Why It Matters

Gives the atlas a concrete, testable niche: does the margin-to-core monocyte-macrophage gradient change after chemotherapy? [[amara-okonkwo]] added THBS1 and SPP1 macrophage states to the list of niches scored per section. It also argues for keeping monocyte-derived macrophages separate from Kupffer cells in the reference ([[liver-metastasis-scrna-reference]]).

## Caveats

Not focused on treatment effects; zones defined by sampling regions rather than spatial transcriptomics at cell resolution.
