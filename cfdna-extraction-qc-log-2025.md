# cfDNA Extraction QC Log 2025

Elias's QC log for all cfDNA extractions in 2025, closed on 2025-12-19. Covers the St Aldric extractions from 2025-03 to 2025-11.

## Key Details

- 1,468 St Aldric plasma time points extracted in 64 batches; 1,402 passed QC.
- Failures: 41 low yield, 19 high-molecular-weight contamination above 20%, 6 control failures with whole batch re-extracted from spare plasma.
- Median yield 1.9 ng per ml plasma; median total 14 ng for post-operative samples, 22 ng for pre-operative.
- Kit lot A until 2025-06-13 (batches 1–27, about 640 samples); lot B from 2025-06-16 (batches 28–64). Entry for 2025-06-16 notes the lot change and that the first 24 lot B samples passed QC.

## Why It Matters

This log is how [[sofia-reyes-calderon|Sofia]] reconstructed the extraction batches for the [[classifier-batch-effect-analysis-2026-03|batch analysis]] in 2026-03. Read together with [[st-aldric-data-dictionary-v3]], which now carries the lot per sample.
