# Northvale Ethics Amendment 2025

## Summary

Approval of an amendment to the Northvale cohort's ethics approval, covering cfDNA methylation profiling of stored plasma and targeted sequencing of buffy coat DNA for clonal hematopoiesis. Approved by the ethics committee on 2025-05-28; [[helena-strom]] handled the submission.

## Key Details

- Covers both the [[northvale-early-detection-study]] and the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]].
- No return of individual results: CH findings are not reported back to participants. The committee asked for this to be stated explicitly and for a review if CH ever became clinically actionable for healthy adults.
- Data stay pseudonymised in governed storage; linkage keys held by the cohort team and registry only.

## Caveats

Return of results was the main discussion point; revisit if guidelines on CH in healthy people change.
