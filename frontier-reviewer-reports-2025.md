# Frontier Reviewer Reports 2025

## Summary

The three step-one reviews of the [[frontier-starting-grant]] proposal, released with the step-one result on 2025-07-11. Scores 4.5, 4.0 and 3.5 out of 5.

## Key Details

- Reviewer 1 (4.5): strong clinical question, credible tissue access; asked for clearer data release plans.
- Reviewer 2 (4.0): liked the paired design; doubted that 28 pairs give power for clone-level comparisons and suggested pooling with public data.
- Reviewer 3 (3.5): main concern that low-count spots in necrotic and fibrotic regions will be mis-assigned by deconvolution methods; asked how the group would know when a method fails.

## Why It Matters

Reviewer 3's point became the [[deconvolution-benchmark]], started on 2025-09-01, and later the stance in [[benchmarks-must-report-failure-modes]]. Reviewer 1's point on data release was not fixed in time for the [[frontier-interview-2025-10-14]].
