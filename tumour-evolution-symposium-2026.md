# Tumour Evolution Symposium 2026

A two-day symposium on how cancers arise, evolve and are monitored, held at Corvane on 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-20 for 180 attendees. Ines co-organises it with [[oluwaseun-adebayo]]; [[priya-raman]] runs logistics.

## Outcome

The symposium takes place on 2026-11-19/20 with the full programme delivered, 180 registered attendees, the budget closed within the Aurora and institute contributions, and speaker slides published where speakers agree. Done when the budget is closed and the feedback summary is filed (target 2026-12-18).

## Current State

- Registration closed on 2026-10-02 at the 180-seat cap of the Corvane auditorium, with 37 on the waiting list ([[symposium-registration-export]]).
- Programme at draft v3: keynote by [[katarina-vukovic]] on day 1, six sessions, 16 short talks selected from 112 abstracts, 58 posters ([[symposium-programme-draft-v3]]).
- Venue and catering contract signed by the institute on 2026-05-14 ([[symposium-venue-contract]]); final numbers due to catering by 2026-11-05.
- Budget forecast 41,800 EUR against 43,700 EUR committed ([[symposium-budget-sheet]]). The open risk is travel for three speakers whose institutions will not prepay.
- In person only, decided on 2026-02-18; the keynote alone is streamed ([[should-the-symposium-be-hybrid]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

In scope: programme, speakers, abstract selection, venue, budget, the poster session, and a short public evening talk organised with [[grace-tanaka]]. Out of scope: publishing proceedings (we decided against it on 2026-02-18) and any press beyond the evening talk; [[freya-holm]] has asked to attend as a journalist and that is handled through the communications office.

## Working Context

The format follows the planning calls. On [[symposium-planning-call-2026-02-18]] Ines and Oluwaseun fixed two days, in person, one keynote and a strong share of early-career talks: at least half of the 16 short talks go to PhD students and postdocs, and the selection in [[symposium-planning-call-2026-07-08]] kept that (9 of 16). Sessions run from pre-diagnostic signals through MRD to metastasis and spatial maps, which mirrors the group's own programme but deliberately puts outside speakers in every session chair seat except one.

Group members presenting: [[sofia-reyes-calderon]] (short talk, fragmentomics), [[amara-okonkwo]] (short talk, atlas), [[kenji-mori]] and [[hannah-lindqvist]] (posters). Ines chairs the MRD session but does not speak.

Reading done for the programme and session framing is filed as Sources under this Project, for example [[williams-2025-clonal-evolution-ovarian-cfdna]] and [[hosseini-2025-divergent-clonal-evolution-prostate-metastases]].
