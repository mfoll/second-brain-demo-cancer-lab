# Rosa Lindgren

Programme officer at the Aurora Cancer Foundation, responsible for the Programme Grants scheme. No affiliation hub in this vault; external contact.

## Role

Rosa runs the Programme Grant call: she answers eligibility questions, manages the reviewer pool and attends the panel as a non-voting officer. She cannot comment on the science of an application, only on fit and rules.

## How We Work Together

- Contact by email with a specific question; she answers within a week and confirms important points in writing.
- On 2026-07-08 she confirmed in writing that a programme with one lead applicant and a co-applicant at the same institute is eligible, that hospital letters of support count towards the feasibility criterion, and that proteomics is within the call's remit if it serves the cancer question rather than being a technology aim.
- She recommended reading the 2025 panel's published summary of common weaknesses; the top two were aims that depend on each other and budgets with unexplained equipment. Both shaped the Aurora application ([[dependent-aims-risk]]).

Keep the tone formal and short. Do not send her draft aims for comment; the Foundation does not pre-review.
