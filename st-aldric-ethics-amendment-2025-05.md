# St Aldric Ethics Amendment 2025-05

Approval letter and amended application from the St Aldric research ethics committee, approved 2025-05-21 and received 2025-05-26. Filed by [[samuel-okoye|Samuel Okoye]].

## What It Allows

- Corvane Institute as an analysis site for cfDNA, tumour and germline sequencing data from the [[st-aldric-mrd-cohort|St Aldric cohort]].
- Linkage with the Northern Cancer Registry for recurrence and death, implemented later under the institute's [[registry-linkage-agreement|registry linkage agreement]] signed 2025-10-31.
- Sharing of coded derived data with "academic collaborators under a data transfer agreement".

## What It Does Not Settle

Whether derived data may go to, or come from, a consortium with several parties (Halo). The Corvane ethics committee secretary, [[helena-strom|Helena Strom]], advised that the St Aldric approval probably allows it but that the institute policy must also allow consortium transfers, which brings the question back to the [[data-access-policy-revision|Data Access Policy Revision]].
