# Genetic and microenvironmental evolution of colorectal liver metastases under chemotherapy.

Shi M et al., Cell Reports Medicine, 2024.

## Summary

Multi-omics and response data from 115 samples of 49 patients with colorectal liver metastases treated with bevacizumab-based chemotherapy. Genomic alterations changed little in most cases, while transcriptomes changed markedly. Separating intrinsic from acquired resistance, the authors attribute part of acquired resistance to hepatocyte and myeloid infiltration, and link SMAD4 mutations and chr20q gain to intrinsic resistance, with functional experiments implicating STAT3 signalling.

## Why It Matters

The paper we cite most in atlas discussions. It was read two weeks after the [[atlas-kickoff-2025-03-03]] and supports the atlas premise: if genomes barely change but expression and microenvironment do, bulk genomics misses the action and spatial data should see it. Its hepatocyte-infiltration finding is also a warning for us, since hepatocyte proportions are exactly what the [[low-count-spot-collapse]] inflates.

## Caveats

Bulk multi-omics, not spatial; one regimen family. Our atlas includes mixed regimens ([[atlas-clinical-annotation-table]]).
