# Symposium Programme Draft v3

Current programme for the [[tumour-evolution-symposium-2026]], agreed on [[symposium-planning-call-2026-10-07]]. Replaces v2 (2026-03-25) and [[symposium-programme-draft-v1]].

## Structure

Day 1, 2026-11-19:

- 09:00 Welcome by [[margarethe-sell]].
- 09:15 Keynote, [[katarina-vukovic]], streamed.
- Session 1, Before diagnosis: pre-diagnostic signals in cohorts. Chair external.
- Session 2, Minimal residual disease. Chair Ines.
- 16:30 Poster session with drinks, 58 posters.
- 19:00 Public evening talk, organised with [[grace-tanaka]].

Day 2, 2026-11-20:

- Session 3, Clonal expansions in normal tissue, including clonal hematopoiesis.
- Session 4, Metastasis and treatment resistance. Chair [[oluwaseun-adebayo]].
- Session 5, Spatial maps of tumours.
- Session 6, Methods and benchmarks.
- 16:00 Poster prizes, close.

Each session: one invited 25-minute talk and two or three 12-minute short talks. 16 short talks in total, 9 by PhD students or postdocs.

## What Changed From v2

- Clonal expansions in normal tissue became its own session after six strong abstracts arrived on CH and somatic mosaicism.
- Session 6 added for methods; it absorbed two talks that would otherwise have competed in session 5.
- Group speakers: [[sofia-reyes-calderon]] in session 2, [[amara-okonkwo]] in session 5.

## Open Items

Two invited speakers in sessions 3 and 4 have not confirmed travel; see [[symposium-speaker-list]].
