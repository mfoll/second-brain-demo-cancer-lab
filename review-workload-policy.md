# Review Workload Policy

Ines's rules for accepting review requests, written in 2025-03 when she joined the Genome Methods Letters board and revised in 2026-01.

## Rules

1. At most 12 manuscript reviews a year outside board duties, and at most two open at once.
2. Accept only when she can run or meaningfully assess the methods. Decline Mendelian randomization methods and pure imaging AI papers and suggest a better reviewer.
3. Decline conflicts: Halo consortium authors, anything using St Aldric or Northvale data, co-authors from the last three years, and any paper handled by [[clara-hoffmann]] while the [[mrd-manuscript]] is in revision at Tumour Evolution Reports.
4. No grant panels for funders we apply to in the same year (Aurora Cancer Foundation, Frontier Research Fund).
5. Answer every request within three working days, with a name if declining.
6. Students may co-review with consent from the editor, credited in the report. [[kenji-mori]] co-reviewed once in 2026-04.

## Why

Each review takes four to six hours, more when code is checked. Twelve a year is roughly two working weeks, which Ines considers fair given how many reviews the group's own papers consume.

## Revision 2026-01

Added rule 3's handling-editor clause after the MRD manuscript went to Tumour Evolution Reports, and rule 6 after a request from Kenji. Counts are in [[peer-review-log]].
