# Sequencing Core Price List 2026

## Summary

Internal prices of the institute sequencing core, effective 2026-07-01 after review at [[sequencing-core-user-committee-2026-06-18]]. Prices are for institute groups and include library preparation, sequencing and primary QC, not analysis.

## Key Details

| Service | Price per sample (EUR) | Change from 2025 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cfDNA low-pass whole genome (about 5x) | 145 | +4% |
| Targeted methylation panel, 1,840 regions | 210 | +3% |
| Buffy coat deep targeted sequencing (CH panel) | 180 | +5% |
| Low-input library surcharge (under 5 ng) | 25 | +7% (proposed +12%) |
| Visium HD library, per capture area | 1,950 | unchanged |

Volume discount: 8% above 500 samples per quarter, agreed per project with [[rafael-ibarrondo]].

## Why It Matters

The buffy coat price is the number behind the question of whether the [[clonal-hematopoiesis-filter]] still needs matched buffy coat sequencing: 180 EUR for each of 212 St Aldric patients is about 38,000 EUR. The cfDNA and methylation prices feed the budgets of the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]].
