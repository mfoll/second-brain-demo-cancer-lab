# Halo Truth Set Shipment 2026-05-19

The full [[halo-truth-set]] left Brightwater on dry ice for all six sites: 120 samples per site, 720 aliquots in total, plus two spare sets kept at Brightwater. [[ngozi-adeyemi]] coordinated the courier; the manifest is in [[halo-truth-set-shipment-manifest]].

## What Happened

- Corvane's box arrived on 2026-05-20 at 09:40 with dry ice remaining and the temperature logger below -60 °C throughout. [[elias-moreau]] checked all 120 tubes against the manifest; two labels were smudged and confirmed by barcode.
- Kelstow's box was held for a day at a transfer hub; the logger stayed below -60 °C and the steering committee accepted the samples.
- Westmere and Thornvale received theirs the same day; Hollinmoor and Brightwater (as a site) next day.

## Consequences

Sequencing window opened: sites to return data to the hub by 2026-09-30. Corvane ran its 120 samples in two batches in June and July on the shallow-WGS assay and uploaded on 2026-08-04. Material transfer was covered by the [[halo-material-transfer-agreement]]; data transfer for the patient tier was not, which is the block described in [[halo-data-flow-and-transfer-constraints]].
