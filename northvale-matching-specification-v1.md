# Northvale Matching Specification v1

> [!warning]
> Superseded on 2025-05-21 by [[northvale-matching-specification-v2]]. Kept for provenance only; do not use for new selections.

## Summary

The first matching draft, circulated on 2025-03-17. It matched on sex, recruitment centre, age at blood draw within 2 years and blood-draw year within 12 months, with three controls per case.

## Why it was replaced

- Twelve months of draw-date tolerance left storage time and season unbalanced within matched sets; [[maja-kovac]] tightened it after reading [[enroth-2016-storage-time-biobank-plasma]].
- Three controls per case did not fit the assay budget once the panel price was known ([[northvale-assay-budget]]); the [[northvale-power-calculation]] showed two controls lost little power.
