# Visium HD Cost Model

Cost per section used for the pilot decision and the grant budget, from the vendor price list and the sequencing core quote ([[atlas-sequencing-quote-2025]]), in 2025 prices.

| Item | EUR per section |
| --- | --- |
| Slide and reagent kit share | 3,300 |
| Sequencing, 300 M read pairs | 1,600 |
| Histology, CytAssist run, QC | 350 |
| Compute and storage, 5 years | 150 |
| Total | about 5,400 |

## Totals

- Pilot: 7 sections, about 38,000 EUR including two repeats.
- Atlas: 80 sections, about 432,000 EUR, plus 10% contingency for repeats. Budgeted in the [[frontier-starting-grant]] ([[frontier-grant-atlas-workplan]]).

## Caveats

Biopsies use less sequencing but the same slide area. Reagent prices rose about 6% in 2026; batch 3 is still within contingency.
