# Symposium Keynote Confirmed 2026-04-21

[[katarina-vukovic]] confirmed by email that she will give the opening keynote of the [[tumour-evolution-symposium-2026]] on 2026-11-19.

## Background

Ines first wrote on 2026-02-23. Katarina could not make the original dates (2026-11-12/13) because of a teaching commitment. On 2026-03-25 Ines and [[oluwaseun-adebayo]] moved the symposium to 2026-11-19/20, which also suited the institute calendar, and re-invited her. She accepted on 2026-04-21.

## Terms Agreed

- 50-minute talk plus questions; streamed.
- Travel and two nights covered from the symposium budget ([[symposium-budget-sheet]]).
- Slides shared afterwards only with her figure-by-figure approval.
- Her request: a dinner with early-career speakers on 2026-11-18 instead of an organisers' dinner.

## Consequences

Programme v2 was rebuilt around a single keynote, and the second keynote slot went to two more short talks.
