# Halo Steering Committee 2026-09-17

Fifth steering committee, online, one week after the Corvane [[data-governance-board-2026-09-10]]. Minutes in [[halo-steering-committee-minutes-2026-09-17]].

## What Was Reported

Ines reported that the institute board discussed the consortium transfer clause in the [[data-access-policy-revision]] but did not adopt it, and that no adoption date could be given. Until then Corvane can neither upload pseudonymised patient-tier outputs linked to clinical data nor receive the unblinding key. She also explained that the same block delays the external validation of the [[mrd-manuscript]] that Reviewer 2 asked for.

## Decisions

- No site analyses the patient tier until its own rules allow it; no exceptions for Corvane ([[henrik-dahl]]).
- If Corvane cannot receive data by mid-2027, the patient-tier analysis moves to Brightwater with Ines leading it remotely.
- [[arjun-mehta]] proposed federated evaluation and training as a way to work within each site's rules. The committee asked Arjun and Ines for a costed concept note for the next meeting; no budget committed ([[federated-cfdna-learning]]).
- Contrived-tier analysis proceeds now; interim report due before the end of November.

## Note

[[maximilian-auer]] asked for a written estimate of the Corvane policy timeline. Ines took that to [[victor-lindahl]] and [[ruth-abernathy]] the following week; the answer was that draft 4 goes to the board on 2026-11-12, and that countersignature of the Halo data sharing agreement could follow by mid-December if it is adopted.
