# Personalizing Treatment for Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma: The Emerging Role of Minimal Residual Disease in Perioperative Decision-Making

Theocharopoulos C et al., Cancers, 2025.

## Summary

Review of MRD biology and detection in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma after curative-intent resection, focused on ctDNA. Argues that MRD status is a sensitive prognostic marker that identifies patients at high relapse risk and could guide perioperative and adjuvant treatment, and discusses how MRD assessment might enter clinical practice.

## Why It Matters

For the [[mrd-review-article|MRD Review Article]] and for [[what-lead-time-over-imaging-is-clinically-meaningful]]: it frames MRD as a tool for treatment decisions rather than earlier diagnosis of recurrence, which is the framing [[samuel-okoye|Samuel Okoye]] has been pushing for our own results.

## Caveats

Review with an advocacy tone; benefit from MRD-guided treatment remains to be shown in trials.
