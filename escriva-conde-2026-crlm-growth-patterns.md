# Spatial transcriptomics differences of histopathological growth patterns in colorectal cancer liver metastases.

Escriva Conde M et al., British Journal of Cancer, 2026.

## Summary

Uses in situ sequencing with two custom panels (150 and 175 genes) on resected, chemotherapy-naive liver metastases from 19 patients with desmoplastic or replacement growth patterns. Replacement growth associated with damaged hepatocytes and particular neoplastic and hybrid hepatocyte-cholangiocyte cells; desmoplastic growth with interferon-gamma signalling, cytotoxic T cells and a fibrotic capsule whose liver-facing and tumour-facing sides differ in cell composition.

## Why It Matters

[[benedikt-kraus]] sent it the week it appeared. Growth pattern is not yet in our [[atlas-pathology-annotations]]; he proposes scoring it on all resections, since the capsule zonation described here maps onto our fibrotic capsule annotations. Its damaged-hepatocyte finding supports the category we added in annotation schema v2. To be discussed at the November atlas meeting.

## Caveats

Chemotherapy-naive tissue and targeted panels; growth patterns after chemotherapy may be harder to score.
