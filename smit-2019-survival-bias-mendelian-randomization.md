# Survival Bias in Mendelian Randomization Studies: A Threat to Causal Inference.

Smit RAJ et al., Epidemiology, 2019.

## Summary

Simulations of instrumental variable estimators when the exposure affects survival up to study inclusion. Selecting on survival weakened the instrument and, for exposures that reduce survival, biased estimates downward; the bias grew with older age at inclusion and differed between one- and two-sample designs.

## Why It Matters

CH is associated with cardiovascular death, so people with large clones are under-represented among older survivors. Northvale recruited at 40 to 69, which limits this, but the CH GWAS we would draw instruments from include much older biobank participants. [[hiroshi-tanabe]] flagged this as a reason to prefer instruments discovered in younger samples ([[ch-instrument-shortlist]]).

## Caveats

Simple simulated causal structures; it shows direction and rough size, not a correction we can apply.
