# Effects of Long-Term Storage Time and Original Sampling Month on Biobank Plasma Protein Concentrations.

## Summary

In 380 biobank plasma samples from 106 women, freezer storage time affected 18 of 108 measured proteins, and season and month of collection affected others, with effect sizes comparable to chronological age. The authors argue that sample handling history, especially storage time, should be treated as seriously as other covariates.

## Why It Matters

The paper that made [[maja-kovac]] tighten the blood-draw date match from twelve months to three in May 2025 ([[northvale-matching-specification-v2]]). Its point about sampling month also argues for matching on date rather than year, which removes seasonal effects within matched sets. Cited in [[matching-on-blood-draw-date-is-worth-the-cost]].

## Caveats

Proteins, not DNA; a small number of individuals sampled repeatedly.
