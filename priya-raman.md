## Background

Priya joined in 2023 from the institute's grants office, which is why budget questions rarely surprise her. Her post is permanent and half-funded by the institute, the most stable position in the group after Ines's own.

## How We Work Together

Fortnightly one-to-one on Tuesdays, the week between lab meetings. Priya sends an agenda the day before with items marked "decide", "inform" or "sign". She prefers decisions in writing, even short ones, and will ask for one if a conversation ends without it. She has authority to approve purchases up to EUR 2,000 without asking.
