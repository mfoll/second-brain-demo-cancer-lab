# Frontier Award 2025-11-28

The Frontier Research Fund informed Ines that the Starting Grant was awarded: 1.48 M EUR, 2026-01-01 to 2030-12-31, funding the [[colorectal-metastasis-atlas]].

## What Changed

- The atlas moved from institute pilot money to a five-year funded programme. [[amara-okonkwo]]'s position was extended to 2030 and a second spatial postdoc was budgeted, which became [[postdoc-hiring-2026]].
- Reporting obligations started: the first interim report is due 2027-05-31 ([[frontier-interim-report]]).
- The award letter ([[frontier-award-letter-2025]]) carried one condition: a data management plan for the atlas within six months, delivered in May 2026 as an addendum to the [[lab-data-management-plan]].

Ines told the group at the next lab meeting and thanked [[chloe-armitage]] for the budget work. [[margarethe-sell]] announced it in the institute newsletter on 2025-12-04.
