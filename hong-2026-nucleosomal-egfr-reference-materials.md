# Development and characterization of nucleosomal DNA-based reference materials for the epidermal growth factor receptor gene liquid biopsy.

Hong SR, Park J, Lee SS, Kim DY, Cho YJ, Bae YK. Analytical and Bioanalytical Chemistry, 2026.

## Summary

A pilot batch of nucleosomal DNA reference materials carrying four EGFR mutations at nominal VAFs of 0%, 0.2%, 1% and 5%. Digital PCR confirmed specificity with limits of detection below 0.2% VAF; the batch was homogeneous across bottles and stable under simulated transport and after three years at -70 °C. Amplicon sequencing agreed closely with dPCR values. The authors argue that nucleosomal material is a more biologically representative standard for low-VAF assays than conventional DNA.

## Why It Matters

Supports the choice in the [[halo-truth-set-design-document]] to use nucleosomal material for the mutation-dilution tier, and the decision to add sheared genomic DNA as a deliberate comparison. Read by [[dmitri-halvorsen]] in May, just before the shipment.

## Caveats

Stability and homogeneity are shown for mutation detection; the paper does not test fragment-length or end-motif features, so it does not settle whether nucleosomal material is commutable for fragmentomics ([[are-contrived-reference-materials-commutable-for-fragmentomics]]).
