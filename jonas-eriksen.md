# Jonas Eriksen

Group leader of the organoids group at Corvane. Internal member of [[sofia-reyes-calderon]]'s thesis committee and a regular internal reviewer for the group's grants.

## Role

Jonas's group derives patient organoids from colorectal and pancreatic tumours. He has held two Aurora awards himself and sat on a Frontier panel in 2024, so he knows how both funders read an application.

## How We Work Together

- Ines and Jonas swap mock-panel duty: he reviewed the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]] at the [[aurora-mock-panel-2026-09-29]] and she will review his Frontier Consolidator draft in spring 2027.
- On the thesis committee he asks the practical questions: timelines, data access, what happens if a dependency slips.
- Possible future collaboration on the [[colorectal-metastasis-atlas]]: organoids from the same patients for functional follow-up. Not funded; mentioned as an outlook in the Frontier proposal.

## Current Threads

- [[sofia-thesis-committee]]: second-year committee on 2026-11-17.
- His mock-panel point that aim 3 depended on samples aims 1 and 2 also needed is the origin of [[dependent-aims-risk]].

## History

- Internal member on Sofia's first-year committee in November 2025 ([[sofia-first-year-committee-report-2025]]).

## What He Brings

Jonas reads applications as a reviewer first and a colleague second. His comments are short and concrete: which aim fails if a sample shipment is late, which budget line a panel will question. At the Aurora mock panel he scored approach lowest of the three panellists and was the one who noticed the Northvale aliquot conflict from the biobank's withdrawal rules ([[corvane-biobank]]). He also proposed a second recruitment site for aim 2, now an open question in [[should-aurora-include-brightwater-as-second-mrd-site]].

## Boundaries

He does not review applications where his group would be a partner, and he asked not to be listed as a collaborator on Aurora so that he could act as an independent mock reviewer.
