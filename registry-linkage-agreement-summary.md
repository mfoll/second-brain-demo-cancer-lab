# Registry Linkage Agreement Summary

## Summary

One-page summary, written by Ines and [[yara-castellanos]] on 2025-11-03, of the agreement between the Corvane Institute and the [[northern-cancer-registry]] signed on 2025-10-31 (contract NCR-2025-017). Use it instead of the 34-page contract for routine questions; check the contract for anything legal.

## Key Details

- Parties: Corvane Institute (signed by [[margarethe-sell]]) and the Northern Cancer Registry (signed by its director).
- Term: 2025-10-31 to 2030-10-31, renewable.
- Populations: consented Northvale Health Cohort participants and St Aldric MRD study patients. Adding a study requires a written variation, not a new agreement.
- Identifiers sent: national ID, date of birth, sex, postcode sector. Registry links in-house and deletes the linkage key 90 days after delivery.
- Variables returned: see [[northern-cancer-registry]].
- Refreshes: up to two per year, target delivery within three months.
- Use: research by named institute projects only, under the institute data access policy.
- Onward transfer: registry-derived variables may not be transferred outside the institute without the registry's written consent (clause 9.2). Aggregate results with cells under 5 suppressed may be published.
- Fees: fixed annual fee plus a per-refresh charge, paid from institute core funds for the first two years.

## Why It Matters

It made outcome follow-up routine for the [[northvale-early-detection-study]] and the [[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study]]. Clause 9.2 is the reason the board asked for the registry's written position before adopting the consortium clause ([[data-access-policy-draft-3]]).
