# Atlas Kickoff 2025-03-03

Kickoff of the [[colorectal-metastasis-atlas]] with [[amara-okonkwo]], [[benedikt-kraus]], [[rafael-ibarrondo]] from the sequencing core and Ines.

## Decisions

- Scope fixed at 40 colorectal liver metastases with a pre-chemotherapy core biopsy and a post-chemotherapy resection, from the Westmere Cancer Centre archive (2019 to 2024 resections).
- Platform not fixed: start the [[visium-hd-pilot]] the same day, with standard Visium as the fallback ([[standard-visium-atlas-plan]]).
- Benedikt to draw up block selection criteria; Amara to request the tissue agreement ([[atlas-tissue-access-agreement]]).
- Funding for the full atlas depends on the [[frontier-starting-grant]], submitted in January. Until the award, spend only on the pilot.

## Notes

Rafael warned that Visium HD slots on the core's sequencer would compete with cfDNA runs in the autumn; we agreed to book pilot sequencing early. The pilot stop rule was written in the meeting: median above 100 UMIs per 8 µm bin in resections and the invasive margin recoverable from expression ([[pilots-need-a-written-stop-rule]]).
