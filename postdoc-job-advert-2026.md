# Postdoc Job Advert 2026

The advert for a postdoctoral scientist in spatial omics, published on the institute careers page on 2026-05-18, closing 2026-06-30.

## Summary

Three-year position on the [[colorectal-metastasis-atlas]], funded by the [[frontier-starting-grant]]. The successful candidate would analyse spatial transcriptomics of paired colorectal liver metastasis samples taken before and after chemotherapy, track tumour clones between them, and help bring the spatial pipeline to release.

## Key Details

- Essential: PhD in a quantitative biology field; analysis of spatial or single-cell data; programming in Python or R; evidence of finishing and communicating a project.
- Desirable: copy-number or clonal inference from spatial or single-cell data; workflow managers such as Nextflow; experience with multi-site or batch-affected data; supervising students.
- Explicit statement that we value collaborative work and read CVs for contribution, not journal names ([[hire-for-collaboration-over-pedigree]]).
- Application: CV, two-page statement on one analysis the applicant is proud of and what went wrong in it, three referees.

## Why It Matters

The criteria became the rows of the [[postdoc-interview-scoring-sheet]]. The "what went wrong" statement was the most useful document for shortlisting.
