# Why human connection is the true metric of research success

Tan TLY, Kon MWR, Coumoul X, Fung FM. FEBS Open Bio, 2026.

## Summary

A perspective piece on postdoctoral supervision and research leadership. The authors draw on their own mentoring experience and a mentorship workshop with early-career researchers, and describe how mentees define good mentorship. Participants put everyday interactions ahead of output metrics: empathy, trust, humility, availability and clear communication. The authors connect this to education literature and argue that humane mentorship matters for research integrity and wellbeing.

## Why It Matters

Brought to the [[group-retreat-2026]] reading list by [[amara-okonkwo]], who asked how postdoc mentoring would work as the group grows with [[owen-mcallister]] joining. Ines found it close to [[leah-ostrander]]'s way of running a lab. It informed the retreat's discussion of one-to-ones for postdocs, not only students.

## Caveats

Perspective and workshop-based, not a systematic study; small and self-selected participant group.
