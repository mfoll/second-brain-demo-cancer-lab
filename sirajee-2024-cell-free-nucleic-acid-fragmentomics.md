# Cell-free nucleic acid fragmentomics: A non-invasive window into cellular epigenomes

Sirajee AS et al., Translational Oncology, 2024.

## Summary

Review of cfDNA fragmentomics: how fragment structure, topology and cleavage patterns reflect the source tissue and its epigenome, the computational methods used to extract these signals, and applications in diagnosis and monitoring. Ends with current challenges.

## Why It Matters

The orientation reading we give new people on the [[fragmentomics-classifier|fragmentomics]] work. Assigned to [[noor-haddad|Noor Haddad]] in 2026 and listed in [[cfdna-fragmentomics-reading-notes]]. Useful background for section 4 of the [[mrd-review-article|MRD Review Article]].

## Caveats

A broad review; no new data. Pre-analytical variation gets less attention than we think it deserves.
