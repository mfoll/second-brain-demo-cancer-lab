# Registry Recurrence Extract 2026-03

Linked extract from the [[northern-cancer-registry|Northern Cancer Registry]] for the 212 St Aldric patients, received 2026-03-18 under the institute's [[registry-linkage-agreement|registry linkage agreement]]. Prepared by [[daniel-asante|Daniel Asante]]'s team.

## Key Details

- Death dates and causes to 2025-12-31 for all 212 patients; 74 deaths, matching St Aldric records in 72 cases. The two extra deaths were patients lost to St Aldric follow-up.
- Recurrence or progression records for patients treated outside St Aldric: 5 recurrences not in the St Aldric export, all in patients who moved hospital. Added after adjudication, they bring the count from 116 to 121 in the 2025-12-31 freeze.
- Linkage by the registry's trusted third party; Corvane receives coded IDs only.

## Why It Matters

Without the linkage agreement signed on 2025-10-31, these five recurrences would have counted as recurrence-free, inflating specificity. A concrete example of service work enabling research.

## Caveats

Registry recurrence coding is less complete than for first diagnoses; dates may lag clinical recurrence by weeks.
