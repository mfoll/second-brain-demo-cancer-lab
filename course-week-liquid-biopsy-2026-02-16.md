# Course Week Liquid Biopsy 2026-02-16

First of two practical weeks in the [[cancer-genomics-course-2026]], 2026-02-16 to 2026-02-20. New in the third edition.

## Programme

- Monday and Tuesday: cfDNA biology, fragment length and end motifs, low-VAF calling. Taught by Ines and [[dmitri-halvorsen]].
- Wednesday: guest lecture by [[zoe-carmichael]] on reporting genomic results to clinicians.
- Thursday and Friday: practical on simulated cfDNA data. Students computed fragment-length profiles and built a simple classifier, with [[sofia-reyes-calderon]] and [[kenji-mori]] as teaching assistants.

## What Went Well

The simulated dataset meant no patient data left governed storage and students could take the notebooks home. Zoe's lecture was the best-rated session of the course.

## What Did Not

Kestrel queue times on Thursday left half the groups waiting over an hour for jobs; the practical overran by 90 minutes. Several students used chat assistants to write the classifier code and could not explain the cross-validation; this started [[how-should-the-course-handle-generative-ai-in-assessments]].
