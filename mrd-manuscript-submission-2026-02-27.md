# MRD Manuscript Submission 2026-02-27

The [[mrd-manuscript|MRD Manuscript]] was submitted to Tumour Evolution Reports on 2026-02-27, four weeks after writing started. The preprint went up on 2026-03-03 ([[mrd-preprint-2026]]).

## What Was Submitted

- 212 patients, data freeze 2025-12-31.
- Main MRD call combining tumour-informed variants, the methylation panel and a gradient-boosting fragmentomics classifier.
- Headline: 71% sensitivity for recurrence at day 28 with 92% specificity; median 4.3 months lead time over imaging.
- CH handling: matched buffy coat for 60 patients, panel of normals for the rest.

## Notes

- Suggested reviewers included two cfDNA methods groups and one pancreatic surgeon; we excluded Halo members because of the consortium relationship.
- Cover letter written by Ines; [[samuel-okoye|Samuel Okoye]] approved the clinical framing.
- Two weeks later the batch analysis showed that the fragmentomics component overfitted extraction batches ([[lab-meeting-2026-03-12]]). We decided not to withdraw: the effect on the combined call was expected to be modest, and we would correct it in revision. Ines informed the editor, [[clara-hoffmann|Clara Hoffmann]], on 2026-03-16 by a short note through the journal system.
