# Biobank Access Committee 2025-06-18

The [[corvane-biobank]] access committee approved the Northvale plasma request ([[northvale-biobank-sample-request-2025]]) with two conditions. [[maja-kovac]] attended to answer questions; [[paolo-ferrante]] presented the retrieval plan.

## Outcome

- Approved: 1,230 aliquots of 1 ml plasma plus up to 14 spares.
- Condition 1: spares retrieved with the shipment but returned unopened if not used.
- Condition 2: methylation data at region level returned to the cohort within 12 months of publication, consistent with the [[northvale-collaboration-agreement]].

## Discussion

A committee member asked why the study did not use 0.5 ml to save plasma for future projects. Maja showed the panel's sensitivity at 0.5 ml from the [[methylation-panel-design]] validation: library yield halved and region dropout doubled. The committee accepted 1 ml for this study and asked future requests to justify volume in the same way, which is now a step in [[requesting-northvale-samples]].

## Follow-up

Retrieval started on 2025-07-07; shipment on [[northvale-sample-shipment-2025-09-23]].
