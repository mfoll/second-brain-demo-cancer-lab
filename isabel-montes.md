# Isabel Montes

Halo clinical lead, a medical oncologist at Westmere Cancer Centre. She leads Work Package 6 (clinical validation) and is the person who decides whether a benchmark result means anything for patients.

## Role

- WP6 lead: defines the clinical endpoints of the benchmark (recurrence by imaging, time from blood draw to recurrence) and the follow-up schedule for the patient tier of the [[halo-truth-set]].
- Westmere contributed 24 of the 60 patient samples and all 20 healthy-donor samples.
- Westmere's own assay is a tumour-naive hybrid-capture panel with UMIs ([[halo-site-and-assay-overview]]).

## How We Work Together

Isabel brings the clinic into every analysis discussion. She asked at the [[halo-annual-meeting-2026-06-10]] that every sensitivity figure be shown alongside the lead time over imaging, because a test that is positive two weeks before a scan changes nothing. That request is now in the [[halo-wp4-analysis-plan]].

She knows [[samuel-okoye]] from surgical oncology meetings and supports using the truth set for our [[mrd-manuscript]] validation, provided Westmere's patients are reported as a separate stratum. She is impatient with the Corvane policy delay but understands it; her own hospital went through a similar revision in 2024.

## Current Threads

- Clinical follow-up update for the 2027-03-31 cut.
- Whether 30 pancreatic patients are enough to say anything clinical ([[is-the-halo-patient-tier-large-enough-for-validation]]).
- Wants a patient-advisory voice in the consortium paper; she invited [[anna-sorensen]] to the annual meeting.

## History With The Group

Isabel and Ines co-wrote the endpoint section of the [[halo-wp4-analysis-plan]]. She chaired the endpoints breakout at the annual meeting, where she also argued that healthy-donor specificity should be reported separately from specificity in non-recurrent patients, because the two answer different clinical questions. Westmere's donor programme supplied all 20 healthy-donor samples on her initiative.
