# Can We Send Pseudonymised cfDNA Data To Halo Sites Under The Current Policy?

## Why It Matters

The [[mrd-manuscript]] needs external validation (Reviewer 2, [[mrd-manuscript-decision-2026-07-14]]). The realistic route is the [[halo-cross-site-benchmark]], which pools pseudonymised fragment-level data and outcomes in an environment hosted at Brightwater. Validation needs two flows: Halo patient-tier data (the unblinding key and clinical follow-up held at Brightwater) coming to Corvane, and St Aldric data contributed to the pooled environment for a joint estimate. Whether that can move now, or only after the [[data-access-policy-revision]], decides the revision timeline.

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> Not in the form Halo needs. The current policy recognises only bilateral agreements with one named partner per project, so Corvane cannot sign the multi-party Halo agreement that governs both sending to and receiving from the pooled environment; and registry-derived outcomes cannot leave the institute without the registry's written consent in any case.

What is possible today:

- A bilateral agreement with Brightwater alone, for one project. Legally possible, but it would not let the other sites access the data, and the board has asked us not to create that precedent while the clause is pending.
- Sharing aggregate results (per-patient classifier scores are not aggregate; cohort-level performance tables are).
- Receiving Halo data at Corvane is also blocked: the Halo sites send only under the multi-party agreement, which Corvane cannot sign under the current policy.

## What Would Settle It

- Adoption of [[data-access-policy-draft-3]] or its successor at the board on 2026-11-12.
- Written consent from the [[northern-cancer-registry]] for the death and second-primary variables, or a decision to send only clinically recorded recurrence, which is not registry-derived.
