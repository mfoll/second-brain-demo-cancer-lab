# Multiple imputation of missing data in nested case-control and case-cohort studies.

## Summary

Keogh and colleagues adapt multiple imputation for missing covariates to nested case-control and case-cohort substudies. They compare imputation using full-cohort information with imputation based on substudy data only, including a matched-set approach that needs no full-cohort data, and show by simulation that full-cohort information can bring large efficiency gains when its assumptions hold.

## Why It Matters

Smoking pack-years are missing for part of the Northvale cohort ([[northvale-smoking-history-derivation]]). The [[northvale-statistical-analysis-plan]] uses multiple imputation with full-cohort questionnaire data as auxiliary information, following this paper; Maja chose it over complete-case analysis in January 2026.

## Caveats

Assumes data missing at random given the imputation model.
