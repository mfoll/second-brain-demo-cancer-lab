# Cycling on the Freeway: The perilous state of open-source neuroscience software.

Westner BU et al., Imaging Neuroscience, 2025.

## Summary

Maintainers of widely used open-source neuroscience tools argue that the ecosystem is fragile because the software is mostly written by working scientists on short, fixed-term contracts, not by professional developers, while the field increasingly depends on it. The paper describes the risks and calls for changes in how such software is funded and staffed.

## Why It Matters

Different field, same structure as ours. Ines quoted the title at the 2026-01 group meeting when explaining why the [[group-software-inventory]] records contract end dates next to each tool's maintainers. Two of our six core tools had a maintainer whose contract ended within 18 months.

## Caveats

A position paper by maintainers; it argues a case rather than measuring one.
