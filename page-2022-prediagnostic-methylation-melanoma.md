# Pre-diagnostic DNA methylation in blood leucocytes in cutaneous melanoma; a nested case-control study within the Norwegian Women and Cancer cohort.

## Summary

A nested case-control epigenome-wide study of 183 incident melanoma cases and 183 matched cancer-free controls, measuring DNA methylation in prediagnostic whole blood on a genome-wide array and analysing it with conditional logistic regression and false discovery rate control. The authors combine their results with a public dataset using a covariate-modulated FDR method.

## Why It Matters

A model for how to write up a prediagnostic methylation study in a matched design, which Maja used when structuring the results section plan in the [[northvale-statistical-analysis-plan]]. The important difference: this study measures leucocyte methylation (host signal), while Northvale measures plasma cfDNA, which mixes tumour-derived and blood-derived DNA. That distinction feeds [[can-ch-explain-part-of-the-northvale-methylation-signal]].

## Caveats

One-to-one matching, a different cancer, and whole blood rather than plasma.
