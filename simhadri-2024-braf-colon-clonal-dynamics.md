# Evolution of Rapid Clonal Dynamics and Non-Cross-Resistance in Response to Alternating Targeted Therapy and Chemotherapy in BRAF-V600E-Mutant Colon Cancer.

Simhadri S et al., JCO Precision Oncology, 2024.

## Summary

A case study of a young patient with metastatic BRAF-V600E, mismatch-repair-stable colon cancer, followed by serial cfDNA, with mouse-derived organoids modelling the response. Resistance to combined targeted therapy came with a MET-amplified clone, which cleared under subsequent chemotherapy; rechallenge with targeted therapy worked again, suggesting the two treatments are not cross-resistant.

## Why It Matters

A clear example that clonal composition can shift within months under alternating therapy, and that cfDNA can follow it. For the atlas, it is a reminder that our "after" sample is a snapshot at resection, possibly weeks after the last cycle. [[dmitri-halvorsen]] flagged it as a link between the atlas and the MRD work.

## Caveats

Single patient; targeted therapy, not the standard chemotherapy in our cohort.
