# DMP Approval Letter 2025

Letter from the data governance board, signed by its chair [[ruth-abernathy]] on 2025-05-27, approving the [[group-data-management-plan]] v1.0.

## Summary

The board approved the plan with two conditions: an annual review of who has access to each dataset, and a named steward for every dataset containing clinical-linked data.

## Key Details

- Both conditions accepted; [[yara-castellanos]] named steward for all clinical-linked data.
- The board noted the plan as an example for other groups and asked Yara to present it at the November 2025 data stewards' forum.
- First access review completed in December 2025; two former members' access removed.
