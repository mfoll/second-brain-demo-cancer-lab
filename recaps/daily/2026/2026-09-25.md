# Friday 25 September 2026

## Postdoc decision

The panel chose Owen McAllister for the spatial-omics post in [[postdoc-hiring-2026|Postdoc Hiring 2026]], with Mateo Silva as reserve. Scores, references and the formal conditions are in the [[owen-mcallister-offer-2026-09-25|offer decision note]].

The choice came down to what the [[colorectal-metastasis-atlas|atlas]] needs next year. Mateo is the stronger deconvolution methodologist, but Kenji's benchmark already covers that ground. Owen has tracked copy-number clones across matched spatial samples and has run analysis for a multi-site study, which is exactly the gap for the paired pre- and post-chemotherapy sections. Jonas made that argument most clearly, and Amara said she would rather supervise someone who fills the gap.

## Offer call

I called Owen in the afternoon. He asked for two weeks to decide. Mateo gets a call from me tomorrow.

Later note: Owen accepted on 9 October and starts on 11 January 2027.
