# Northvale Kick-off 2025-02-10

Kick-off of the [[northvale-early-detection-study]] with [[mei-lin-zhou]]. Present: Ines, [[maja-kovac]], [[dmitri-halvorsen]], Mei-Lin, and [[paolo-ferrante]] for the last half hour.

## Decisions

- Design: [[nested-case-control-design]] with incidence-density sampling, not a case-cohort design. A case-cohort would have let the random subcohort serve other outcomes, but every sample costs a full panel run, and the question is about two cancers only. The rejected option is kept in [[northvale-case-cohort-option]].
- Cancers: pancreatic and colorectal. Mei-Lin argued for colorectal (enough cases); Ines for pancreatic (the group's MRD expertise and the bigger need). Both kept.
- Assay: the methylation panel then in design ([[methylation-panel-design]]); no assay work on Northvale plasma until the panel passes QC.
- Mei-Lin's conditions: co-authorship, sign-off on outward communication, derived data returned to the cohort. Became the [[northvale-collaboration-agreement]].

## Actions

- Maja: matching draft (became [[northvale-matching-specification-v1]]) and power calculation.
- Paolo: estimate plasma availability for cases in the [[northern-cancer-registry-extract-2023]].
- Ines: ethics amendment with [[helena-strom]].

## Notes

Paolo's first estimate, that about half the cases would have no plasma left, turned out right and set the final size at 410.
