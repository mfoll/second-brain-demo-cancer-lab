# Halo Truth Set Blinding And Unblinding Rules

How the [[halo-truth-set]] is blinded and when each part is unblinded, as agreed in the [[halo-wp4-analysis-plan]] and held by [[ngozi-adeyemi]].

## Rules

1. Every aliquot carries a random consortium code; tier and nominal values are withheld from site teams. The contrived tier is recognisable by DNA mass; sites are asked not to exploit this.
2. Contrived tier, stage 1: the WP4 statisticians (Ines and [[sun-hee-park]]) receive the contrived key once a site has uploaded and locked its results, so interim analysis can start. Site teams stay blind.
3. Contrived tier, stage 2: the key is released to all sites once all six have locked their results. Kelstow has not, so this has not happened.
4. Patient and donor tiers: unblinding only after all six sites have locked results and only to sites whose rules permit receiving pseudonymised clinical data ([[halo-data-flow-and-transfer-constraints]]).
5. A site may not re-run samples after seeing any key. Re-runs before unblinding are logged.

## Consequences For Our Own Projects

The [[low-vaf-caller-benchmark]] needs contrived-tier truth at site level, which is stage 2: it stays paused until Kelstow locks its results, expected after mid-November. Ines's stage-1 access is for WP4 only and must not feed group projects.
