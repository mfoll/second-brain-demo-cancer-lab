# MRD Revision Planning 2026-07-21

Planning meeting for the [[mrd-manuscript|MRD Manuscript]] revision, one week after the decision ([[mrd-manuscript-decision-2026-07-14]]). Present: Ines, Dmitri, Sofia, Hannah, [[maja-kovac|Maja Kovac]], [[yara-castellanos|Yara Castellanos]]; [[samuel-okoye|Samuel Okoye]] for the first half hour.

## Critical Path

External validation for Reviewer 2. Yara explained that sharing derived St Aldric data with Halo, or bringing Halo-derived data in for a Corvane-led paper, falls outside the current institute data access policy; the consortium clause is in the draft of the [[data-access-policy-revision|Data Access Policy Revision]], due at the board in September. Without it, no transfer.

## Options

1. **Halo samples, central analysis**: request post-resection pancreatic plasma data from two Halo sites with outcomes ([[halo-cross-site-benchmark]]). Best answer to Reviewer 2. Needs the policy.
2. **Federated scoring**: Halo sites run our frozen container on their own data and return per-site metrics only. Possibly within current rules, but needs each site's agreement and compute; links to [[federated-cfdna-learning]].
3. **Temporal split in St Aldric**: rejected; Reviewer 2 ruled it out.

## Decisions
