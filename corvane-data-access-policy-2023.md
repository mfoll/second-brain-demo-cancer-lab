# Corvane Data Access Policy 2023 (Version 2)

## Summary

The institute data access policy in force since 2023-02-01, adopted by the data governance board and replacing [[corvane-data-access-policy-2018]]. It governs access by internal and external researchers to institute-held person-level data: cohort data, biobank-derived data, sequencing data and linked registry data.

## Why It Matters

This is the text the [[data-access-policy-revision]] changes. It works for one-to-one collaborations and for internal access, but it has no route for multi-party consortium sharing. Every transfer outside the institute needs a bilateral data transfer agreement naming one recipient and one project. For Halo that means fifteen agreements, no basis for signing the consortium's multi-party agreement, and therefore no route for receiving Halo patient-tier data or placing St Aldric data in the pooled environment hosted at Brightwater.

## Key Details

- Section 3: internal access by project, approved by the data steward for low-risk data and by the board for cohort and linked data.
- Section 4: incoming person-level data from external partners may be received only under an agreement approved under this policy; the policy recognises bilateral agreements only.
- Section 5: external transfer of pseudonymised data requires a bilateral agreement, a DPIA screening, and board approval. Onward transfer is prohibited.
- Section 6: anonymous aggregate outputs may be shared freely if cells under 5 are suppressed.
- Section 8: registry-derived variables follow the conditions of the relevant registry agreement.
- Review: due 2026-02-01, three years after adoption. The board's review on 2026-03-05 triggered the revision.

## Caveats

The policy uses "anonymised" loosely in section 6 for aggregate outputs that are, strictly, only aggregated; draft 3 fixes the wording ([[pseudonymisation-vs-anonymisation]]).
