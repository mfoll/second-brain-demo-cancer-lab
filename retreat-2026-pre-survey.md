# Retreat 2026 Pre-Survey

Anonymous survey run by [[priya-raman]] from 2026-07-20 to 2026-08-07 to set the [[group-retreat-2026]] agenda. 13 of 15 members responded. Results summarised by Priya on 2026-08-10; free-text answers are kept only in the survey tool.

## Summary

Two themes dominated: lab meetings run long without clear decisions, and pipeline knowledge is concentrated in one person. Satisfaction with supervision and with the science was high.

## Key Details

- "Lab meetings end with clear decisions": 4 of 13 agreed.
- "I could fix a failing pipeline run without asking Tobias": 3 of 13 agreed.
- "I know who to ask about data access": 11 of 13 agreed, credited to [[yara-castellanos]].
- Most requested change: separate operational items from science in lab meetings (8 mentions).
- Second: code review so that more people understand the pipelines (6 mentions).
- Third: clearer authorship conversations at project start (4 mentions).

## Why It Matters

Each of the top three requests became a retreat session and a practice change: [[lab-meeting-format]], [[code-review-guidelines]], [[authorship-and-credit-guidelines]].

## Caveats

Small group; percentages would mislead, so only counts are reported. Two non-responses were people on leave.
