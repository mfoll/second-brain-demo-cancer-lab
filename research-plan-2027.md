# Research Plan 2027

The group's plan for 2027, drafted at the [[group-retreat-2026]] and circulated by Ines on 2026-09-25. Accepted at [[lab-meeting-2026-10-08]].

## Summary

Four scientific priorities with owners and finish dates, two practice changes, and a short list of things we will deliberately not start in 2027.

## Key Details

Priorities:

1. [[mrd-manuscript]] revised with external validation and resubmitted. Owner [[dmitri-halvorsen]]. Depends on the [[data-access-policy-revision]] allowing Halo transfer.
2. Northvale lead-time paper submitted from [[lead-time-analysis]]. Owner [[maja-kovac]].
3. [[colorectal-metastasis-atlas]] cohort complete: all 40 pre- and post-chemotherapy pairs sequenced and clone tracking under way. Owners [[amara-okonkwo]] and [[owen-mcallister]].
4. [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]] submitted by 2026-12-04, then reporting on the [[frontier-starting-grant]].

Practice changes: review of every pipeline change ([[code-review-guidelines]]); lab meetings split into science and operations ([[lab-meeting-format]]).

Not in 2027: the [[atlas-data-portal]] beyond a design note; restarting the [[low-vaf-caller-benchmark]] before the Halo truth set exists; new wet-lab assays.
