# Conflict Of Interest Handling For Vendor Contacts

How Ines handles industry contacts given her seats on the [[data-governance-board-membership|data governance board]] and the [[sequencing-core-user-committee]]. Based on the [[corvane-conflict-of-interest-policy]].

No financial interest, advisory role or payment in either case.

## Working Rules

- Declare on the institute register within 30 days of a contact becoming more than routine.
- Restate the interest at the start of any relevant board or committee item; present if asked, do not vote.
- Meet vendor staff with the core head or in committee; commercial discussions with the technology transfer office present.
- No hospitality beyond meeting refreshments; sponsored travel declined (one offer in 2026).
- Share nothing unpublished: no St Aldric data, no Halo data, no classifier details beyond the preprint.
- Record recusals in the minutes, as at [[sequencing-core-user-committee-2026-10-08]].
