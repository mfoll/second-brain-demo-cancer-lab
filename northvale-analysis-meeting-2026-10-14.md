# Northvale Analysis Meeting 2026-10-14

Monthly Northvale meeting, focused on the consequences of the 2025 registry extract. Present: [[maja-kovac]], Ines, [[mei-lin-zhou]], [[daniel-asante]], [[lucas-brandt]].

## Discussion

Daniel walked through the three control replacements and the four corrected case dates. All three replaced controls were diagnosed in late 2023 and registered in 2024, a textbook case of [[registry-reporting-lag]]. Their blood had been drawn more than 14 years before those diagnoses, and their methylation scores were unremarkable, consistent with the null result beyond five years.

Mei-Lin raised the 97 new cases from 2024 and 2025. She prefers an independent validation set with a fresh biobank request. Ines worried about plasma availability and the 2025 data being provisional. Daniel advised waiting for the 2027 refresh before treating 2025 diagnoses as final.

## Decisions

- Re-run all lead-time models on the corrected control set (started 2026-10-15).
- No decision on the 97 cases; scope a validation proposal after the refresh, with [[paolo-ferrante]]'s availability estimate.
- Keep the symposium talk on interim numbers unless the re-run is frozen by 2026-11-12.
