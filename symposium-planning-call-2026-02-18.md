# Symposium Planning Call 2026-02-18

First planning call for the [[tumour-evolution-symposium-2026]] between Ines and [[oluwaseun-adebayo]], with [[priya-raman]] for the logistics half. 60 minutes, video.

## Decisions

- Two days in November at Corvane; first proposed dates 2026-11-12/13 ([[symposium-programme-draft-v1]]), later moved.
- In person, no hybrid participation; the keynote alone will be streamed. Oluwaseun argued for hybrid, Ines against on cost and on the quality of discussion; settled as recorded in [[should-the-symposium-be-hybrid]].
- At least half of the short talks to go to early-career researchers.
- No proceedings volume.
- Division of labour: Oluwaseun leads metastasis and treatment sessions and the abstract rubric; Ines leads pre-diagnostic and MRD sessions, venue and budget.
- Keynote shortlist of three names; [[katarina-vukovic]] first choice.
