# Wednesday 22 April 2026

## First Northvale window results

Maja showed the first [[time-to-diagnosis-windows|time-to-diagnosis window]] results for the [[lead-time-analysis|Lead-Time Analysis]]. The methylation signal is concentrated in cases diagnosed within two years of blood draw and fades beyond five years; pancreatic and colorectal cases have the same shape. The figures are in the [[northvale-analysis-meeting-2026-04-22|analysis meeting note]].

Nobody wanted a pooled estimate after seeing this. Mei-Lin put it simply: the gradient is the finding. How much of the early signal to exclude from the main model was left for a later meeting, once Maja had prepared the options and a storage-time check.
