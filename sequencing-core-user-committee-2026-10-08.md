# Sequencing Core User Committee 2026-10-08

Quarterly meeting. Main item: the 2027 instrument replacement plan.

## Discussion

- [[rafael-ibarrondo]] presented the replacement plan: one of the two high-throughput instruments reaches end of support in 2027. Two vendors will be invited to present; one of them is the current vendor.
- [[ben-whitfield]] presented the current vendor's roadmap for 20 minutes and left before the discussion, as agreed beforehand. Ines restated her declared contact with him at the start of the item and said she will not vote on the recommendation if his company is shortlisted. Rafael minuted it.
- Ines raised the buffy coat sequencing price (180 EUR per sample). Rafael offered a project discount if the group commits to more than 400 buffy coat samples in 2027; this depends on whether the [[clonal-hematopoiesis-filter]] still needs matched buffy coat.
- Turnaround recovered to 14 working days in Q3.

## Outcome

Vendor presentations scheduled for December. The buffy coat volume decision is now an input to the CH filter planning.
