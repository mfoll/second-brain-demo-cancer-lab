# St Aldric MRD Study Protocol v2

Study protocol for the analysis of the St Aldric post-resection collection at Corvane, version 2, approved with the [[st-aldric-ethics-amendment-2025-05|2025 ethics amendment]]. Authors: [[samuel-okoye|Samuel Okoye]] and Ines Varga.

## Key Details

- Objectives: primary, molecular lead time over radiological recurrence; secondary, landmark day-28 sensitivity and specificity, comparison with CA19-9, layer-specific performance.
- Sample flow: St Aldric biobank to Corvane cfDNA lab, coded IDs, linkage key held at St Aldric.
- Outcome ascertainment: St Aldric imaging plus registry linkage for recurrence and death outside St Aldric ([[registry-recurrence-extract-2026-03]]).
- Statistics: detailed separately in [[mrd-statistical-analysis-plan-v2]].

## Changes From v1

v1 (2025-01) had no registry linkage and did not name Corvane as an analysis site.
