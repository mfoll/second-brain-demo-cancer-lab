# Requesting Northvale Samples

How to request plasma or buffy coat DNA from the Northvale Health Cohort in the [[corvane-biobank]]. Written after the 2025 plasma request ([[northvale-biobank-sample-request-2025]]) and used for the CH subcohort release and the 2026 control replacements.

## When to use

Any new use of Northvale samples by the group: a new study, a second case set, or replacement samples within an approved study (steps 1, 5 and 6 only).

## Inputs

- An approved question from the Northvale steering group, or a minor variation under an existing approval ([[northvale-collaboration-agreement]]).
- Ethics coverage for the assay ([[northvale-ethics-amendment-2025]]); check with [[helena-strom]] if the assay type is new.
- A participant list with biobank sample identifiers, from the governed cohort release ([[northvale-health-cohort]]).

## Steps

1. **Talk to [[paolo-ferrante]] first.** Share the draft list and ask for an availability check: remaining aliquots per participant, last-aliquot status, freezer history. Expect one to two weeks.
2. **Fix the design against availability.** Apply the last-aliquot rule before matching, not after. If matching on collection date, agree tolerances with Paolo ([[matching-on-blood-draw-date-is-worth-the-cost]]).
3. **Justify volume.** The access committee asks for evidence that the requested volume is needed, for example library yield at lower input ([[biobank-access-committee-2025-06-18]]).
4. **Submit the request** under the cohort PI's name ([[mei-lin-zhou]]) with the group contact, the participant list sorted by freezer and rack position, the destination lab, and the return plan for unused material.
5. **Receive and reconcile.** Check temperature logs and barcodes on the day; reconcile the full manifest against the selection log within four weeks ([[northvale-aliquot-manifest-2025]]).
6. **Return unused material** within three months and log the return with Paolo.

## Guardrails

- Never request a participant's last aliquot.
- Never move Northvale material to another lab or site without a new approval.
- Identifiers stay pseudonymised; lists travel through governed storage, not email.

## Verification

The request is complete when every received aliquot is either assayed and linked in the data release or returned, and the reconciliation note is filed with the manifest.

## Timing

From first contact to shipment: about four months for 1,230 aliquots in 2025.
