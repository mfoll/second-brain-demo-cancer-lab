# Internal Grant Review And Sign-off

How an outgoing grant application moves from the group through internal review and the grants office to submission. Follows the institute rules in [[corvane-grants-office-internal-deadlines-policy]].

## When to Use

Every application where Ines is lead or co-applicant. Steps 2 and 3 are optional below 500 k EUR.

## Steps

1. **Notify the grants office** at least eight weeks before the funder deadline. Send the call link and a one-paragraph plan to [[chloe-armitage]]. She returns an eligibility note and a budget skeleton.
2. **Build the resource sheet.** Ines and [[priya-raman]] fill in staff, consumables and services in the grants office template (for Aurora: [[aurora-budget-sheet]]). Do not compute overhead; the office does.
3. **Mock panel** eight to ten weeks out, scored on the funder's criteria ([[mock-panels-are-worth-the-time]]). Record the outcome as an Event.
4. **Budget review** with the grants office after the aims are stable. The office freezes the sheet ten working days before its own internal deadline.
5. **Complete package to the grants office** ten working days before the funder deadline: case for support, budget justification, letters, CVs, data management section.
6. **Director sign-off.** Chloe routes the package to [[margarethe-sell]]; allow five working days.
7. **Submission.** The grants office submits through the funder portal unless the funder requires the applicant to submit. Ines checks the submitted PDF the same day.

## Verification

- The grants office confirmation email is saved in the grant Project's Source notes.
- The submitted version is tagged in the group's grants folder and linked from the Project.

## Pitfalls

- Letters of support take longer than expected; ask four weeks ahead.
- Funders change salary scales and overhead rules; never reuse last year's budget sheet.
- A co-applicant's group must sign off its own lines with its own department administrator before step 6.
