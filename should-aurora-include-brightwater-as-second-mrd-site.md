# Should Aurora Include Brightwater As Second MRD Site?

Should aim 2 of the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]] recruit post-surgery patients at Brightwater University Hospital as well as St Aldric?

## Why It Matters

[[jonas-eriksen]] raised it at the [[aurora-mock-panel-2026-09-29]]: a single-site MRD aim invites the same external-validation criticism that Reviewer 2 made of the [[mrd-manuscript]]. A second site answers it by design. St Aldric alone gives about 60 new resections a year, enough for the primary endpoint but not for a separate validation set.

## Arguments

- For: built-in external validation; Brightwater already sends samples to the Halo consortium, so pre-analytics are harmonised through the [[halo-cross-site-benchmark]]; [[maximilian-auer]] is supportive in principle.
- Against: about 90 k EUR recruitment costs, a second ethics approval, and a data sharing agreement that depends on the [[data-access-policy-revision]], which the board has not adopted. It also squeezes the proteomics budget ([[how-much-of-the-aurora-budget-goes-to-proteomics]]).

Ines to speak with [[samuel-okoye]] and Maximilian by 2026-11-04; decision by 2026-11-06.
