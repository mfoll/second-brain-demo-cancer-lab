# Corvane Data Access Policy 2018 (Version 1)

Superseded on 2023-02-01 by [[corvane-data-access-policy-2023]]. Kept because the Northvale access decisions from 2018 to 2022 were taken under it, and because two older collaboration agreements still cite its section numbers.

## Summary

The first institute-wide data access policy. It had no distinction between pseudonymised and aggregate outputs, routed every external request to the full board, and had no DPIA requirement. Captured in February 2025 while preparing the [[lab-data-management-plan]], to check which rules applied to legacy Northvale extracts held by the group.

## What Changed In Version 2

- Data steward approval for low-risk internal requests, which cut board load roughly by half.
- Mandatory DPIA screening for external transfers.
- Explicit section on registry-derived variables.
