# Comparing Xenium 5K and Visium HD data from identical tissue slide at a pathological perspective.

Long M et al., Journal of Experimental & Clinical Cancer Research, 2025 (letter).

## Summary

Compares a sequencing-based platform (Visium HD) and a targeted imaging platform (Xenium 5K) run on the same lung tissue slide, read alongside pathology annotations. Visium HD gives broader gene coverage and probably resolves more tumour subclones; Xenium gives comparable clustering with robust algorithms and better single-cell segmentation of irregular cells, but fluorescence-based detection struggles in pigmented tissue.

## Why It Matters

Read a few weeks before the [[visium-hd-pilot-decision-2025-08-28]], when we were asked by the sequencing core whether an imaging panel would be cheaper. It supported the argument that whole-transcriptome coverage matters for subclone detection, which the atlas needs.

## Caveats

One public dataset, lung tissue, a short letter. Liver has its own autofluorescence issues that this does not address.
