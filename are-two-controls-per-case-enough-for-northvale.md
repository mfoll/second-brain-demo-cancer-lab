# Are two controls per case enough for Northvale?

## Question

The first matching draft used three controls per case. Assay cost per sample is high. Is two enough?

## Current Answer

Yes, decided in April 2025. The [[northvale-power-calculation]] gave power of 0.83 for an odds ratio of 1.3 with two controls, against 0.88 with three, for 50% more control assays. The saving (410 assays) went into deeper sequencing of all samples instead ([[northvale-assay-budget]]).

## Caveats

Window-specific power is poor with any ratio; more controls would not have fixed that, more cases would. The study cannot get more cases from the 2023 follow-up, which is part of why the 97 new cases from the 2025 extract are being discussed ([[registry-linkage-update]]).
