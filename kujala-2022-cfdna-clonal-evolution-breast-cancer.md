# Circulating Cell-Free DNA Reflects the Clonal Evolution of Breast Cancer Tumors.

Kujala J et al., Cancers, 2022.

## Summary

Sequenced matched primary tumours, metastases and cfDNA from 18 breast cancer patients. cfDNA detected on average a little over half of the somatic variants found in either tumour sample, and despite high discordance it reflected clonal evolution and revealed driver variants absent from tumour DNA. Tumour-specific variants were seen in cfDNA at diagnosis and months before recurrence was detected.

## Why It Matters

An earlier, simpler counterpart to [[williams-2025-clonal-evolution-ovarian-cfdna]] that Ines paired with it for the MRD session introduction. The discordance point is useful for our own talk planning: [[sofia-reyes-calderon]]'s short talk will be asked why a fragmentomics signal is needed when variants exist, and this paper shows how incomplete variant detection in cfDNA can be.

## Caveats

Small cohort; tumour-informed variant panels have improved since.
