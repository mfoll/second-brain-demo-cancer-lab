# Pre-analytical optimization of cell-free DNA and extracellular vesicle-derived DNA for mutation detection in liquid biopsies.

Dohmen J, De Santis L, Stephens BM, et al. Molecular Oncology, 2026.

## Summary

A prospective study of four pre-analytical variables (processing delay, storage temperature, tube type, plasma volume) on cfDNA and extracellular-vesicle DNA from 244 cancer patients, with key findings checked in 723 archived samples. cfDNA concentration rose after 24 hours at room temperature in the prospective set and after 6 hours in the retrospective one. Short-term refrigeration preserved quality better than freezing whole samples; extracted DNA was stable at -80 °C. Two tube types performed similarly when processed promptly. Larger plasma volume helped vesicle DNA but not cfDNA for mutation detection.

## Why It Matters

Arrived a month after the [[halo-steering-committee-2026-02-26]] decision and supports it: processing delay is the variable that most needs harmonising, which is what the truth-set protocol did (plasma within 4 hours). The retrospective result is a warning for legacy samples at sites with long delays ([[should-halo-harmonise-preanalytics-or-correct-statistically]]). [[elias-moreau]] used it to check our own wet-lab SOP.

## Caveats

Mutation detection only; no fragment-size endpoints, which matter most to us. Not a multi-site study.
