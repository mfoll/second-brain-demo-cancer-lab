# Causal association between clonal hematopoiesis indeterminate potential and cancer: a Mendelian randomization study.

## Summary

A two-sample Mendelian randomization study of genetically predicted CHIP and 18 cancer types, using genetic instruments from a large genome-wide association study of CHIP and summary statistics from several large consortia. Overall and DNMT3A CHIP were associated with higher risk of several cancers, including lung, kidney and thyroid cancer, after multiple-testing correction, with estimates varying by histological subtype.

## Why It Matters

Lucas flagged it before the [[ch-analysis-meeting-2026-09-16]] as external support for expecting site-specific rather than uniform effects, and for reporting DNMT3A separately. It does not change the plan: our analysis is observational and Mendelian randomization stays out of scope for this paper.

## Caveats

Mendelian randomization instruments for CHIP are weak and the method answers a lifetime-exposure question.
