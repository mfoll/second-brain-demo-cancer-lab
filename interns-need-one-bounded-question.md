# Interns Need One Bounded Question

## Stance

An MSc intern project should be one question that fits in ten weeks, has a known answer shape (a curve, a table, a yes or no) and sits next to an ongoing Project that will absorb the result.

## Why I Think This

- 2026 worked: [[noor-haddad]]'s panel-of-normals comparison and [[kofi-mensah]]'s low-count simulation both produced a clear figure and were picked up by [[hannah-lindqvist]] and [[kenji-mori]] ([[intern-final-talks-2026-08-26]]).
- 2025 did not: an open-ended "explore the methylation data" project produced a lot of notebooks and no result anyone used.
- A daily supervisor who needs the answer is a better motivator than any amount of formal supervision.

## What Would Change My Mind

An intern who thrives on open exploration and produces something useful from it. Possible, but the group cannot plan around it.
