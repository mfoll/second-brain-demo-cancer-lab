# Extensive methylation analysis of circulating tumor DNA in plasma of patients with gastric cancer

Nagano S et al., Scientific Reports, 2024.

## Summary

Targeted bisulfite sequencing of 63 candidate genes in tumour, normal tissue and plasma of 16 patients treated for recurrent gastric cancer. Five genes (including SEPT9 and SDC2) were particularly hypermethylated in tumours, and high plasma methylation of these genes was associated with worse survival. In one patient sampled repeatedly, plasma methylation tracked clinical status.

## Why It Matters

Read during region selection for the [[methylation-panel-design|methylation panel]] in 2025-03. Two of its genes overlap regions we included for gastrointestinal tissue signal. Mainly useful as a small example of serial plasma methylation following disease, which we cited in the panel design rationale ([[methylation-panel-design-document-v4]]).

## Caveats

16 patients, gastric cancer, bisulfite chemistry; no bearing on pancreatic specificity.
