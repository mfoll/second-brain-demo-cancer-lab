# Lab Meetings Should End With Decisions

## Stance

A lab meeting that ends without a written list of decisions and owners was an update session, and updates can be read. The whole group's time is the most expensive resource I schedule; it should be spent on deciding and on science that benefits from many eyes.

## Why I Think This

- The best meeting of 2026 was [[lab-meeting-2026-03-12]]: one clear problem, evidence on the table, a decision and owners by the end.
- The worst were the 100-minute round-robins of 2025 under [[lab-meeting-format-2025]], where nobody remembered a week later what had been agreed.
- The [[retreat-2026-pre-survey]] said the same from the group's side.

## What Would Change My Mind

If the decision-first format squeezes out the open-ended science discussion that students value, or if people stop raising half-formed problems because they feel they must bring a proposal. I will ask about this specifically at the January review.

## Implications

- Every agenda item is marked decide, discuss or inform. Inform items go in the written notes, not the meeting.
- A discussion that cannot reach a decision ends with an owner and a date for one.
- The notes start with decisions, then actions, then everything else ([[lab-meeting-format]]).
