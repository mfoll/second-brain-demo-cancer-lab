# Aurora Cancer Foundation Programme Grants Call 2026

## Summary

The Aurora Cancer Foundation's 2026 call for Programme Grants, published in May 2026: five-year awards for established groups pursuing a coherent programme on cancer prevention, early detection or treatment. Full applications are due on 2026-12-04; panel interviews in April 2027; decisions in June 2027; earliest start 2027-10-01.

## Key Details

- Ceiling 2.5 M EUR over five years, including 20% overhead on staff and consumables; equipment carries no overhead and above 50 k EUR per item needs justification.
- One lead applicant; up to two co-applicants, at most one from the lead's own institute.
- Maximum two funded postdocs per applicant.
- Assessment criteria, each scored 1 to 5: significance, approach, feasibility, team, value for money.
- Technology development is in scope only where it serves a defined cancer question.
- Patient and public involvement is expected in the case for support; a letter from a patient organisation is encouraged.
