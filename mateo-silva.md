# Mateo Silva

Postdoc candidate interviewed in September 2026 for the spatial-omics position in [[postdoc-hiring-2026]]. Runner-up to [[owen-mcallister]]. External contact with no affiliation in this vault.

## Interview Context

- Interviewed on 2026-09-15. Strongest candidate on cell-type deconvolution methods: his talk compared reference-based methods on low-count spots and was the most rigorous methods talk of the five.
- Panel scored him slightly below Owen on clonal inference and on experience running multi-site analyses, which the atlas needs in 2027. The decision is recorded in [[owen-mcallister-offer-2026-09-25]].
- The team session rated him highly; [[kenji-mori]] in particular.

His application statement, on an analysis he was proud of and what went wrong in it, described a deconvolution result that did not replicate on a second platform and how he traced it to reference mismatch. The panel used it as an example of the kind of statement the advert was designed to elicit ([[postdoc-job-advert-2026]]).

## Since Then

- Ines called him on 2026-09-26 with feedback. He agreed to stay on the reserve list until 2026-12-31.
- He is interested in the [[deconvolution-benchmark]] and offered comments on the preprint. Kenji and Ines agreed to send it once it is posted; any collaboration should be offered as a contribution with credit, not as unpaid review.

## What We Learned From His Interview

His talk was the clearest account of how reference-based deconvolution fails on low-count spots that the panel heard all year, and it matched what [[kenji-mori]] is finding in the [[deconvolution-benchmark]]. He also asked the sharpest question of the team session: how the group decides which method goes into the production pipeline when the benchmark shows no single winner. Nobody had a written answer; the review rule in [[code-review-guidelines]] now covers part of it.

If a second spatial position opens, for example through the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]], he should be invited to apply. That would be a new recruitment with an open advert, not an offer from this reserve list.
