# Northvale Analysis Meeting 2026-04-22

First look at window results. Present: [[maja-kovac]], Ines, [[mei-lin-zhou]], [[lucas-brandt]].

## What we saw

- Methylation score clearly higher in cases diagnosed within two years of blood draw; odds ratios around 5 in the first year and 2 in the second.
- Little or no difference beyond five years.
- Pancreatic and colorectal cases show the same shape; pancreatic numbers are small in every window.

## Discussion

Everyone agreed the pooled estimate would mislead. Mei-Lin said the gradient was the finding. Lucas asked whether CH in blood cells could contribute to the score in older cases; nobody knew, and it became [[can-ch-explain-part-of-the-northvale-methylation-signal]].

## Decisions

- Maja prepares the exclusion options and a storage-time check before deciding anything ([[northvale-analysis-meeting-2026-05-20]], [[northvale-analysis-meeting-2026-06-24]]).
- No results leave the group before the decision.
