# Fatima Benali

Halo site PI at Thornvale University, where the consortium's second fragment assay runs: size-selected low-pass whole-genome sequencing with nucleosome-footprint features. She is [[leila-farouk]]'s PhD supervisor and the reason Leila is spending 2026 with us.

## Role

- Site PI, Thornvale: nucleosome footprint and fragment-end profiling from low-pass WGS, with a size-selection step that enriches short fragments ([[halo-site-and-assay-overview]]).
- Contributor to WP4 task 4.2 (harmonised fragmentomics features) with [[dmitri-halvorsen]].
- Member of the steering committee.

## How We Work Together

Thornvale and Corvane are the two fragmentomics sites in Halo, which makes Fatima both our closest ally and our most direct comparison. The relationship is good because it was set up as collaboration early: at the [[halo-steering-committee-2025-11-20]] she and Ines agreed to define fragment-length and end-motif features jointly ([[fragment-length-and-end-motif-features]]) rather than publish competing definitions.

She raised the commutability problem from the Thornvale side at the [[halo-annual-meeting-2026-06-10]]: her size selection discards much of the sheared genomic DNA in the contrived tier, so her assay underreports VAF there for reasons unrelated to sensitivity. That, together with our own results, made commutability a reported finding rather than a footnote ([[commutability-of-reference-materials]]).

## Current Threads

- Leila's visit, from 2026-04-13 to 2026-12-18: joint work on porting our end-motif features to the Thornvale assay.
- A joint fragmentomics note from the two sites on the truth set, once the patient tier can be analysed.
- She favours federated evaluation for her own reasons: Thornvale's ethics approval also forbids outgoing patient-level data.

## History With The Group

Fatima and Ines have known each other since a fragmentomics workshop in 2023; Thornvale joined Halo partly at Ines's suggestion. Thornvale has not yet signed the Halo data sharing agreement either, which makes Fatima an ally on the transfer question.
