# Halo Material Transfer Agreement

The multi-party material transfer agreement for the [[halo-truth-set]], drafted by [[eva-novak]] and signed by all six sites by 2026-04-22. For Corvane, signed by the director's office after review by the grants office and [[paolo-ferrante]] for the biobank.

## Key Details

- Covers physical aliquots only: receipt, storage at -80 °C, use for the benchmark, destruction or return of leftovers at consortium end.
- No re-identification attempts; no use outside the benchmark without steering committee approval.
- Explicitly excludes data. Data generated on the samples falls under article 9 of the consortium agreement and the data sharing agreement.

## Why It Matters

This is why the shipment could go ahead on 2026-05-19 while data transfer stayed blocked: samples and data travel under different instruments, and our institute rules allowed the first but not the second. The board confirmed on 2026-09-10 that the revised clause will cover data only ([[data-governance-board-2026-09-10]]).
