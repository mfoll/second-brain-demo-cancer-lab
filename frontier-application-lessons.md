# Frontier Application Lessons

What the successful [[frontier-starting-grant]] application taught us, written in December 2025 so it could be reused for the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]].

## Keep Doing

- One design figure on page one. All three reviewers in the [[frontier-reviewer-reports-2025]] referred to it.
- Name the collaborators who control access: [[benedikt-kraus]] for tissue, the biobank for plasma. Feasibility scores followed.
- Pilot data at real resolution, even if small. The [[visium-hd-pilot]] figure did more than a page of argument.
- Practise the interview with people who will be rude ([[frontier-interview-2025-10-14]]).

## Do Better

- Data release and sharing: thin in the proposal, weak at the interview. For Aurora, the data management section is drafted early with [[yara-castellanos]].
- The budget justification was written in the last two days and had a salary scale error that [[chloe-armitage]] caught. Start it with the resource sheet.
- Reviewer 3's concern about low-count spots was foreseeable; we knew about it from the pilot. Name known weaknesses and the plan for them in the proposal itself.
