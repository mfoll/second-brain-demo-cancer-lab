# Copy-number alterations in cell-free DNA can be transient or harbingers of clonal hematopoiesis

Tuveri S et al., NPJ Precision Oncology, 2025.

## Summary

Genome-wide plasma cfDNA screening of 1,002 healthy elderly people found 15 with copy-number alterations of unknown origin. Nine were followed for three to five years with questionnaires, whole-body MRI and repeated blood analyses: the alterations disappeared in two and persisted in seven, mostly linked to low-grade clonal mosaicism in blood.

## Why It Matters

[[lucas-brandt|Lucas Brandt]] circulated it in 2025-09. For the MRD study it is a reminder that clonal hematopoiesis is not only point mutations: copy-number signals in plasma, which tumour-naive MRD approaches use, can also come from blood clones. We do not currently use copy number in the main MRD call, but any future copy-number layer would need a CH check too ([[clonal-hematopoiesis]]).

## Caveats

Small follow-up subset; healthy elderly, not cancer patients.
