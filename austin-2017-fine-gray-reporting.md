# Practical recommendations for reporting Fine-Gray model analyses for competing risk data.

## Summary

A review of how the Fine-Gray subdistribution hazard model was used in medical papers published in 2015, finding frequent misinterpretation of subdistribution hazard ratios. The authors distinguish questions about the rate of events (cause-specific hazards) from questions about the probability of events over time (cumulative incidence) and give reporting recommendations.

## Why It Matters

The basis for the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis-plan]] choice: cause-specific hazard ratios answer the aetiological question (does CH change the rate of cancer), and cumulative incidence curves show absolute risk. A Fine-Gray model is reported only as a secondary description. The open part is in [[cause-specific-or-fine-gray-for-ch-cancer-risk]].

## Caveats

Reporting guidance, not new methods.
