# Characterization of fragment sizes, copy number aberrations and 4-mer end motifs in cell-free DNA of hepatocellular carcinoma for enhanced liquid biopsy-based cancer detection

Jin C et al., Molecular Oncology, 2021.

## Summary

Whole-genome cfDNA sequencing of 197 patients with hepatocellular carcinoma and 187 with hepatitis B infection. Selecting fragments shorter than 150 bp raised the estimated tumour fraction and strengthened copy-number signals. A few 4-mer end motifs (CCCA, CCTG, CCAG) were preferred in cancer samples, and many end motifs were associated with fragment size in both groups, suggesting the two features are mechanistically coupled.

## Why It Matters

Sofia used it in 2025-05 to define the first feature list of the [[fragmentomics-classifier|Fragmentomics Classifier]]: length bins with a 150 bp boundary and the 256 4-mer end motifs ([[fragment-length-and-end-motif-features]]). The coupling between size and motif is also a warning: if a batch shifts sizes, motifs may move too, which is why we tested motif stability separately in 2026.

## Caveats

Liver cancer with a hepatitis B comparison group, not pancreatic cancer after surgery; tumour fractions are much higher than in our post-operative samples.
