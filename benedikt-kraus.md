# Benedikt Kraus

Pathologist in the institute tissue unit. He is our pathology partner on the [[colorectal-metastasis-atlas]] and co-author of the atlas paper.

## Role

- Selects blocks from the Westmere Cancer Centre archive with the hepatobiliary team and checks that each pre-chemotherapy biopsy and its resection come from the same metastasis ([[atlas-tissue-manifest]]).
- Reviews H&E for every section before a Visium HD run and signs off section QC ([[tissue-section-qc]]).
- Annotates regions on each section (viable tumour, invasive margin, fibrotic capsule, necrosis, adjacent liver) and scores tumour regression grade on the resections ([[tumour-regression-grade]], [[atlas-pathology-annotations]]).

## How We Work Together

He works through the tissue unit's request system; requests go via [[amara-okonkwo]] with block lists, never ad hoc emails. Annotation sessions are booked half-days, about eight sections per session. He insists that clusters are interpreted against his annotations rather than the other way round, which the group has adopted ([[pathologist-annotations-anchor-spatial-clusters]]).

He is sceptical that a 1 mm core represents a metastasis and raised this first ([[are-pre-chemo-core-biopsies-representative]]).

## Background

Benedikt has run the institute tissue unit's hepatobiliary work for several years and knows the Westmere archive well, which saved weeks in block selection. He shaped the stop rule of the [[visium-hd-pilot]] by insisting it include recovery of the invasive margin, not only count thresholds. He is co-author on the atlas paper and has asked to be involved in the [[deconvolution-benchmark]] applied example, since its fibrotic-capsule figure depends on his annotations.

Recent reading he brought in: [[escriva-conde-2026-crlm-growth-patterns]], which may lead to adding growth-pattern scoring to the annotations.
