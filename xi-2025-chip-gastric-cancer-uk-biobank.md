# Clonal hematopoiesis of indeterminate potential is a risk factor of gastric cancer: A Prospective Cohort in UK Biobank study.

## Summary

A prospective cohort analysis in a large population biobank with whole-exome sequencing at baseline. CHIP was defined from mutations in 43 CHIP-related genes and examined as a risk factor for incident gastric cancer, with adjustment for age, sex, alcohol, smoking status and diabetes.

## Why It Matters

One of two recent single-site CH and cancer cohort analyses Lucas used to plan the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]], with [[lai-2025-chip-breast-cancer-uk-biobank]]. Both adjust for smoking status only, which is the gap our analysis tries to close with pack-years.

## Caveats

Exome-based CHIP calls with limited sensitivity at low VAF; few gastric cancers.
