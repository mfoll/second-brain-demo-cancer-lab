# Northvale Sample Shipment 2025-09-23

The [[corvane-biobank]] delivered the Northvale plasma aliquots to the cfDNA wet lab next to the sequencing core: 1,244 straw pairs in 16 boxes on dry ice, with the [[northvale-aliquot-manifest-2025]].

## What happened

- [[paolo-ferrante]] handed over; [[elias-moreau]] received, checked temperature logs (no excursions) and moved boxes to the lab's -80 freezer.
- Spot check of 64 barcodes against the manifest on the day: two position swaps found, both corrected by barcode.
- Extraction started on 2025-09-29 in randomised plate order, with case and control status hidden from the wet lab. Each plate held complete matched sets to avoid batch differences within sets.
- [[rafael-ibarrondo]]'s core scheduled sequencing in eight runs from mid-October to late November 2025.

## Why it matters

Plate layout by matched set is the reason batch effects do not bias the conditional analysis, and the reason the [[northvale-statistical-analysis-plan]] does not adjust for plate.
