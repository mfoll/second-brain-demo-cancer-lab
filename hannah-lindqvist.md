# Hannah Lindqvist

PhD student since 2025-05, working on the [[clonal-hematopoiesis-filter|Clonal Hematopoiesis Filter]]. Supervised by Ines with [[dmitri-halvorsen|Dmitri Halvorsen]]; she also works closely with [[lucas-brandt|Lucas Brandt]] because both projects use the same CH calls.

## Role

- Designed and ran the 60-patient matched buffy coat pilot ([[ch-buffy-coat-pilot-results-2026-01]]).
- Built the panel of normals from the Northvale CH callset ([[ch-panel-of-normals-v1]]) and maintains the [[ch-filter-gene-list]].
- Co-maintains the CH caller with Lucas; its Northvale output is frozen in [[ch-subcohort-variant-calls]], and release notes tell each project which caller version it used.
- Drafting the clonal hematopoiesis section of the [[mrd-review-article|MRD Review Article]].

## How We Work Together

Fortnightly supervision with Ines and Dmitri, and a short weekly sync with Lucas on the callset. Hannah came from a clinical genetics lab and is careful about variant interpretation; she pushed for keeping PPM1D and TP53 in the filter even though they overlap with tumour drivers, with a rule that tumour-informed variants override the filter ([[ch-filter-design-meeting-2025-11-05]]). She is more confident in writing than in presenting, so she presents at every second group meeting by agreement.

## Current Threads

- Costed recommendation on whether the remaining 152 St Aldric patients need buffy coat sequencing ([[do-we-still-need-matched-buffy-coat-sequencing]]).
- Testing fragment length of supporting reads as a variant-origin feature, after [[brown-2025-fragmentomic-variant-origin]].
- Reviewer 1 response on CH handling for the [[mrd-manuscript]].

## Background

Before her PhD Hannah worked for three years as a variant scientist in a hospital genetics laboratory, which shows in her careful documentation and in her suspicion of variant calls that look too clean. Her thesis plan: the CH filter in the MRD study, the panel-of-normals size question, and plasma-only variant origin prediction. She was daily supervisor of [[noor-haddad|Noor Haddad]]'s 2026 summer project, a small healthy-donor panel of normals for CH filtering ([[intern-project-panel-of-normals-ch]]), which she is now folding into the filter.
