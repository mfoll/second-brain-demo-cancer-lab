# Association of personalized and tumor-informed ctDNA with patient survival outcomes in pancreatic adenocarcinoma

Botta GP et al., The Oncologist, 2024.

## Summary

Retrospective analysis of commercial tumour-informed, exome-based ctDNA tests in pancreatic cancer: 1,329 plasma samples from 298 patients, collected at diagnosis, in the post-operative MRD window and during surveillance. About 29% of patients were ctDNA-positive in the MRD window and similarly in surveillance, and MRD-window positivity was associated with much shorter disease-free survival.

## Why It Matters

The closest published comparator for the [[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study|MRD study]] when we designed it in early 2025. It supported a post-operative window of 2–12 weeks, which matched St Aldric's day-28 sample, and set expectations for positivity rates. Cited in the introduction of the [[mrd-manuscript]].

## Caveats

Commercial test data with heterogeneous follow-up; no comparison with imaging lead time, which is our main question.
