# Can Halo Samples Serve as External Validation?

Can post-resection pancreatic cancer samples from Halo sites provide the external validation Reviewer 2 requires for the [[mrd-manuscript|MRD Manuscript]], and on what terms?

## Why It Matters

This is the critical path of the revision ([[mrd-manuscript-decision-2026-07-14]]). No other independent cohort with serial post-operative plasma and outcomes is within reach before 2027-01-29.

## Scientific Fit

- Two Halo sites have post-resection pancreatic plasma with imaging follow-up, about 150 patients together, and the [[halo-truth-set]] itself holds 30 post-resection pancreatic patients that Corvane has already sequenced blind. Sampling schedules differ from St Aldric (day 30 and six-monthly), which affects lead-time but not landmark analyses.
- Extraction chemistries differ across sites, so the fragment-length component needs per-site correction using the truth set's healthy-donor plasma ([[does-the-stacked-classifier-generalise-across-extraction-batches]]).
- Methylation: one site ran a different panel; only overlapping regions can be scored there.
- Size: 30 truth-set patients alone give wide intervals ([[is-the-halo-patient-tier-large-enough-for-validation]]), hence the request for the sites' own cohorts.

## Governance Fit

- The Halo consortium agreement allows reuse; Halo legal ([[eva-novak|Eva Novak]]) requires each institution's policy to permit it ([[halo-validation-request-2026-08-04]]).
- Corvane's current policy lacks a consortium clause; adoption is pending in the [[data-access-policy-revision|Data Access Policy Revision]] ([[data-governance-board-2026-09-10]]).

## Current Answer

Scientifically yes, for the landmark analysis and with per-site correction; legally not yet. The board re-tables the clause on 2026-11-12. Plan B is federated scoring at Halo sites, which the editor has said she would accept ([[mrd-revision-extension-2026-10-07]], [[should-halo-validation-run-federated-instead-of-transfer]]).
