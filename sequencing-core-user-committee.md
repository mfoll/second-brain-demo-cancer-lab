# Sequencing Core User Committee

The advisory committee of the institute sequencing core, chaired by [[rafael-ibarrondo]]. Ines represents computational and liquid biopsy users since 2025. Quarterly meetings.

## What It Decides Or Advises On

- The annual price list ([[sequencing-core-price-list-2026]]).
- Queue rules and turnaround targets.
- Instrument replacement and service contracts (advisory; the director decides).
- Data delivery locations, now Halyard project storage.

## Recent Meetings

- [[sequencing-core-user-committee-2026-06-18]]: price list, low-input surcharge cut from +12% to +7%; minutes in [[sequencing-core-user-committee-minutes-2026-06-18]].
- [[sequencing-core-user-committee-2026-10-08]]: 2027 instrument replacement; buffy coat volume discount.

## What The Group Gets From It

Advance notice of price and queue changes, and a voice on low-input cfDNA libraries, which are most of what [[elias-moreau]] sends. [[priya-raman]] sends the quarterly forecast that gives the group priority.
