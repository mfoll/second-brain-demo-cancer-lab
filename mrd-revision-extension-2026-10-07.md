# MRD Revision Extension 2026-10-07

[[clara-hoffmann|Clara Hoffmann]], handling editor at Tumour Evolution Reports, granted an extension of the [[mrd-manuscript|MRD Manuscript]] revision deadline from 2026-10-12 to 2027-01-29.

Ines wrote on 2026-10-01 explaining that the external validation requested by Reviewer 2 depends on a change to the institute's data access policy for consortium data, discussed by the board on 2026-09-10 and not yet adopted ([[data-governance-board-2026-09-10]]). She described plan B, federated scoring of the frozen model at Halo sites, and listed what was already done for Reviewers 1 and 3.

## Conditions

- If only federated validation is possible, report per-site metrics on the frozen model with pre-specified thresholds; the editor considers that acceptable.
- No further extension expected.

## Consequence

The revision timeline in [[mrd-revision-response-plan]] now works back from 2027-01-29: classifier frozen by 2026-11-30, external results by 2027-01-08 at the latest. If the board does not adopt the clause on 2026-11-12, switch to plan B.
