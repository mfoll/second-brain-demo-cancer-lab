# Frontier Grant Atlas Workplan

The atlas work packages as funded in the [[frontier-starting-grant]] (awarded 2025-11-28, running to 2030). This is the version the [[frontier-interim-report]] will be measured against.

## Key Details

- WP1: atlas data generation, 80 sections, months 1 to 18.
- WP2: deconvolution and segmentation methods, including the [[deconvolution-benchmark]], months 1 to 24.
- WP3: clonal and niche dynamics under chemotherapy, months 12 to 48; one postdoc from 2027 (now [[owen-mcallister]]).
- WP4: open resource, months 36 to 60; this is the [[atlas-data-portal]].
- Milestone M1, "atlas analysis set frozen", month 15 (February 2027).
