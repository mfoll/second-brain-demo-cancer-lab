# Buffy Coat Sequencing Cost Estimate 2025-11

Costing prepared by [[rafael-ibarrondo|Rafael Ibarrondo]] at the sequencing core and Hannah for the [[ch-filter-design-meeting-2025-11-05|CH filter design meeting]].

## Key Details

- Per sample, CH panel at about 1,500x: DNA extraction from buffy coat 12 EUR, library and capture 58 EUR, sequencing 110 EUR. Total about 180 EUR.
- All 212 patients: about 38,000 EUR. Remaining 152 after the pilot: about 27,000 EUR.
- Cheaper option considered: 500x depth at about 120 EUR, rejected because it cannot see 0.5% VAF clones reliably.
- Prices at 2025 internal core rates; the core warned of a 6% increase from 2027.

## Why It Matters

The cost side of [[do-we-still-need-matched-buffy-coat-sequencing]]. Also the basis for the buffy coat line in the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027|Aurora grant]] budget.
