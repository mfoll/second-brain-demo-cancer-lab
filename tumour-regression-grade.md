# Tumour Regression Grade

A histological score of how much viable tumour remains after neoadjuvant treatment, relative to fibrosis and necrosis. Several scales exist; for liver metastases the common ones grade from complete response (no viable tumour) to no response.

## How We Use It

[[benedikt-kraus]] scores every post-chemotherapy resection in the atlas on a five-level scale, collapsed to three groups for analysis: major response, partial response, minimal or no response. Scores are recorded in [[atlas-clinical-annotation-table]] and drive stratification in the [[colorectal-metastasis-atlas]].

## Caveats

- Scored on the section we sequence, which may not represent the whole lesion.
- Major responders have little tumour left; their post-treatment sections are mostly fibrosis, where counts are low and [[low-count-spot-collapse]] is a risk.
- Inter-observer agreement is moderate; a second pathologist reviews borderline cases.

Current distribution in the 26 sequenced pairs: 7 major, 12 partial, 7 minimal or no response.
