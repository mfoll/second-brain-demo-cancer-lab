# CH Buffy Coat Pilot Results 2026-01

Hannah's report on the 60-patient matched buffy coat pilot designed at the [[ch-filter-design-meeting-2025-11-05|2025-11-05 meeting]], presented 2026-01-28. Panel-of-normals comparison added on 2026-02-24.

## Key Results

- 60 patients, baseline buffy coat sequenced with the CH panel at about 1,500x.
- 39 of 60 (65%) had at least one CH variant at 0.5% VAF or more in buffy coat; median age of the pilot 69.
- Of 212 non-tumour-informed plasma variants passing the caller across all time points, 66 (31%) were present in buffy coat.
- Genes: DNMT3A 27, TET2 14, ASXL1 6, PPM1D 6, TP53 5, other 8.
- Without a CH filter, 9 of 60 patients would have had at least one false molecular recurrence call.
- Panel of normals v1 ([[ch-panel-of-normals-v1]]) flagged 55 of the 66 (84%); with it alone, 2 of 60 patients would still have had a false call. All 11 misses were private variants below 1% VAF.

## Caveats

The pilot was enriched for patients with low-VAF plasma variants, so the 31% figure is higher than it would be in the whole cohort.
