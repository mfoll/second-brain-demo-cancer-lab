# Data Request Statistics 2025

Annual summary of data access requests handled by the board and the data stewards in 2025, prepared by the board secretariat for the November 2026 board.

## Key Details

- 61 requests in total: 38 internal, 23 external.
- Internal: 29 approved by data stewards, 9 by the board. Median decision time 6 working days (stewards) and 34 working days (board).
- External: 19 approved, 2 withdrawn, 2 refused (one lacked ethics approval, one asked for identifiable data without need).
- External requests needing a new bilateral agreement took a median of 97 days from approval to data release, mostly in contract negotiation.
- 7 of the 23 external requests were from consortia, each handled as separate bilateral agreements.

## Why It Matters

The 97-day median and the seven consortium requests are the strongest numbers for the [[data-access-policy-revision]]. Ines plans to cite them in the cover note for draft 4.
