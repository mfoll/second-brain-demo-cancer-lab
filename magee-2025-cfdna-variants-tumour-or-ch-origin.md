# Characterization of Plasma Cell-Free DNA Variants as of Tumor or Clonal Hematopoiesis Origin in 16,812 Advanced Cancer Patients

Magee D et al., Clinical Cancer Research, 2025.

## Summary

Liquid biopsy profiles of 16,812 patients across 49 cancer types, sequenced from plasma and from buffy coat with a whole-exome and whole-transcriptome assay, with variant origin assigned by comparing the two. About 42% of patients had at least one CH variant in reportable genes. Substantial shares of variants in BRCA2, CHEK2, BRCA1, ATM and TP53 were of CH origin, smaller shares in KRAS and EGFR, and the proportion of CH calls rose with age.

## Why It Matters

Hannah brought it to the 2025-07 CH reading session; it is the strongest evidence in our files that a cfDNA assay without white-cell sequencing will misattribute variants, including in genes that matter for pancreatic cancer (TP53, KRAS, BRCA2). It shaped the [[ch-filter-gene-list]] and supports matched sequencing in [[do-we-still-need-matched-buffy-coat-sequencing]].

## Caveats

Advanced cancers and treatment selection, not post-operative MRD; our VAFs are lower, where CH is proportionally even more important.
