# Visium HD Pilot Report

Amara's 18-page report closing the [[visium-hd-pilot]], circulated 2025-08-26 ahead of the decision meeting.

## Summary

Seven FFPE sections (three pre/post pairs and one normal liver) ran on Visium HD. At 8 µm, median UMIs per bin were 214 in resections and 128 in biopsies, above the stop-rule threshold of 100. Pathologist-annotated structures were recoverable at 8 µm and lost at 16 µm. Bin2cell segmentation worked on resections and was patchy on two of three biopsies.

## Key Details

- One biopsy with DV200 of 24% gave a median of 61 UMIs per bin and was excluded; one resection section was recut after a fold.
- Expression-based copy-number inference separated two subclones in one resection, consistent with Benedikt's morphology.
- Recommendation: adopt, 8 µm bins, segmentation secondary, write a section QC procedure.

## Caveats

Seven sections; no biological conclusions drawn.
