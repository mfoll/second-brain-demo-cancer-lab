# Symposium Venue Contract

Pointer to the contract for the Corvane auditorium, two breakout rooms and catering for 2026-11-19/20, signed by the institute's facilities office on 2026-05-14. The contract itself stays in the institute register; this note records the terms we need.

## Key Terms

- Auditorium capacity 180 seated, which set the registration cap.
- Catering per head for two lunches, four coffee breaks and the poster reception; final numbers due 2026-11-05, with no charge for reductions up to 10% after that date.
- Cancellation free until 2026-09-30; after that 50% of the room fee.
- AV and streaming for one room included; streaming the keynote only was chosen partly because a second streamed room would have cost extra.

## Caveats

Ines cannot sign institute contracts; any amendment goes through facilities, with [[priya-raman]] as contact.
