# Internship Hosting Guidelines

## Summary

The institute's rules for hosting MSc interns, version 2025, read when the [[summer-internships-2026]] call opened.

## Key Details

- Maximum twelve weeks; a named daily supervisor and a group leader as formal supervisor.
- Interns may work on personal data only after the institute's data protection training and only inside governed storage; linked registry data are excluded.
- Stipend set by the institute; the group pays from its own budget.
- A short written report and a talk are expected at the end.

## Why It Matters

The data rule is why both 2026 projects used synthetic, healthy-donor or already de-identified data; [[yara-castellanos]] ran the training for [[noor-haddad]] and [[kofi-mensah]] in their first week.
