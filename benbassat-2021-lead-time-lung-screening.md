# Duration of lead time in screening for lung cancer.

## Summary

A non-systematic review of lead-time estimates in lung cancer screening, comparing estimates derived from longitudinal studies, prevalence-to-incidence ratios, tumour doubling time and probability modelling. Estimates differ widely by method and by screening test, from a few months for chest radiography to a few years for low-dose CT.

## Why It Matters

The main lesson for us is methodological: lead time is not one number, and its estimate depends on how it is derived. That is part of why the [[lead-time-analysis]] reports windows rather than a single lead-time figure. Read with [[duffy-2013-overdiagnosis-lead-time]] for the same journal club.

## Caveats

Lung cancer imaging, not biomarkers.
