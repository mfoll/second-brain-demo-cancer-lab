# September 2026

## Consortium transfer clause deferred

On [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-09-10|10 September]] the data governance board supported the principle of consortium transfer but did not adopt the [[data-access-policy-revision|policy revision]]. It wants a DPIA covering receiving sites, the registry's written position, and tighter deletion wording. Next attempt is 12 November. The MRD revision timeline now assumes adoption then.

## Postdoc hired

The panel selected Owen McAllister on [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-09-25|25 September]] for the clone-tracking gap in the atlas, with Mateo Silva as reserve. Owen accepted on 9 October ([[postdoc-hiring-2026|Postdoc Hiring 2026]]).

## Aurora aims reduced to two

The [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-09-29|29 September mock panel]] led to proteomics being merged into the MRD aim of the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027|Aurora Programme Grant]], run on St Aldric plasma only. Aims v3 followed in October.

## Compute

Kestrel stopped taking jobs mid-month and was switched off on 30 September after the data inventory was signed off. The Halyard GPU partition, which the spatial pipeline had been waiting for since July, was granted on 22 September ([[halyard-cluster-migration|Halyard Cluster Migration]]).
