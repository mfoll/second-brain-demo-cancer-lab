# Symposium Abstract Scoring Sheet

Shared sheet where four scorers (Ines, [[oluwaseun-adebayo]] and two external session chairs) rated the 112 abstracts for the [[tumour-evolution-symposium-2026]] between 2026-06-15 and 2026-07-03.

## Method

Rubric written by Oluwaseun: novelty, rigour and fit to session, each 1 to 5, plus a flag for early-career presenters. Each abstract was scored by two people; disagreements above three points went to a third. Scorers did not score abstracts from their own institution, so Ines did not score the four from the group.

## Outcome

16 short talks and 58 posters selected on [[symposium-planning-call-2026-07-08]]; 9 of the 16 talks are early-career, meeting the target set in February. 38 abstracts were declined for lack of space or fit.
