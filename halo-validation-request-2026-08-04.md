# Halo Validation Request 2026-08-04

Ines sent the formal request to the Halo steering committee for external validation data for the [[mrd-manuscript|MRD Manuscript]], using the consortium's data request form ([[halo-validation-sample-request-2026-08]]). Addressed to [[maximilian-auer|Maximilian Auer]] as coordinator, copied to [[henrik-dahl|Henrik Dahl]] and [[eva-novak|Eva Novak]].

## What Was Requested

- Shallow WGS and methylation-panel-compatible data, plus recurrence outcomes, for post-resection pancreatic cancer patients at two Halo sites; about 150 patients expected.
- Results and outcomes for the 30 post-resection pancreatic patients in the [[halo-truth-set]], plus the healthy-donor tier to calibrate the fragment-length component across sites before scoring patients.
- Commitment that the frozen model is applied once, and that site investigators are co-authors.

## Responses

- Maximilian: scientifically supportive; the [[halo-cross-site-benchmark]] was designed partly for this kind of reuse.
- Eva Novak (Halo legal and ethics), 2026-08-11: the consortium agreement allows it, but each party's institutional policy must also permit the transfer. Corvane's current policy has no consortium clause, so she cannot release data to Corvane until the revised policy is adopted.
- [[arjun-mehta|Arjun Mehta]] offered to test federated scoring at his site as a fallback.

## Status

Pending on the [[data-access-policy-revision|Data Access Policy Revision]]. The board discussed the consortium clause on 2026-09-10 without adopting it ([[data-governance-board-2026-09-10]]).
