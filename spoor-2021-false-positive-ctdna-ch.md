# Liquid biopsy in esophageal cancer: a case report of false-positive circulating tumor DNA detection due to clonal hematopoiesis

Spoor J et al., Annals of Translational Medicine, 2021.

## Summary

Case report of a patient followed with ctDNA after neoadjuvant chemoradiotherapy and oesophagectomy. The tumour's TP53 mutation became detectable in plasma only after metastases were already found clinically, while surveillance repeatedly returned other mutations that white-blood-cell sequencing traced to clonal hematopoiesis. The authors recommend checking plasma mutations against the tumour biopsy.

## Why It Matters

Hannah's first reading for the [[clonal-hematopoiesis-filter|CH filter]], in 2025-06. One case, but it shows both failure modes of MRD surveillance at once: CH false positives and true ctDNA below the detection limit. It is cited in [[matched-normal-sequencing-is-cheaper-than-false-recurrence]] and supports our rule that tumour-informed variants override the filter.

## Caveats

Single case, different cancer and treatment setting.
