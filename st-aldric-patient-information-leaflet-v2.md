# St Aldric Patient Information Leaflet v2

Revised patient information leaflet for the St Aldric follow-up sampling, rewritten after the [[patient-advisory-meeting-2025-10-22|patient advisory meeting]] and reviewed by [[anna-sorensen|Anna Sorensen]]. Submitted to the St Aldric ethics committee as a minor amendment in 2025-11.

## Key Changes From v1

- "Minimal residual disease" replaced by "signs that the cancer may come back".
- New section on false alarms, explaining that some blood changes come from normal ageing of blood cells ([[clonal-hematopoiesis]]) and that results are not returned to individual patients in this study.
- Reading age reduced; two pages instead of four.

## Why It Matters

It is the template for any future interventional MRD study and a candidate example of patient-facing language for the [[mrd-review-article]].
