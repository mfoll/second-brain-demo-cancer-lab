# Data Governance Board Membership

Ines's seat on the institute data governance board: what it involves, how she prepares, and how she handles items where her own group has an interest.

## The Seat

Appointed by [[margarethe-sell]] in 2024 as one of four group leader members, term to 2027-03-31. The board is chaired by [[ruth-abernathy]]; standing members include [[victor-lindahl]] (DPO) and [[paolo-ferrante]] (biobank); [[helena-strom]] attends as observer for consent questions. Rules are in [[data-governance-board-terms-of-reference]]. About six meetings a year, plus written procedures for urgent items.

## How Ines Prepares

Papers arrive ten working days ahead. She reads them with [[yara-castellanos]], who often knows the requesting group's data situation. She sends questions to the requester before the meeting rather than raising them cold.

## Interests

- Group items (DMP, policy revision, any request by the group): declares, presents, does not vote.
- Industry contacts declared on the register: [[ben-whitfield]], [[ravi-vellanki]]. See [[conflict-of-interest-handling-vendor-contacts]].
- Time cost: about four hours per meeting, more during the policy revision. Worth it for the group; the registry linkage would not exist without the seat.
