# Cancer Genomics Course 2026 Syllabus

## Summary

The syllabus of the third edition of the institute PhD course, approved by the graduate school on 2025-12-05. 4 ECTS, Monday afternoons from 2026-01-12, two practical weeks, final projects on 2026-05-22.

## Key Details

- Block 1, tumour evolution and phylogenies (four sessions, Ines).
- Block 2, liquid biopsy, with the practical week from 2026-02-16 (Ines, [[dmitri-halvorsen]], guest [[zoe-carmichael]]).
- Block 3, spatial and single-cell methods, with the practical week from 2026-04-13 ([[amara-okonkwo]], [[kenji-mori]]).
- Block 4, molecular epidemiology and causal inference basics ([[maja-kovac]]).
- Assessment: final group project (60%: report 20%, oral defence of methods 40%) and weekly exercises (40%).
- Capacity 24; 31 applications.
