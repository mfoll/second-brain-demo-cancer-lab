# Standard Visium Atlas Plan

Archived. The original February 2025 plan to build the atlas on standard Visium with 55 µm spots. Superseded on 2025-08-28 when the [[visium-hd-pilot]] showed that Visium HD at 8 µm was enough ([[visium-hd-pilot-decision-2025-08-28]]).

## The Plan as Written

- 40 pairs, 80 sections, standard Visium FFPE slides, 6.5 mm capture areas.
- Deconvolution per spot to estimate cell-type mixtures, with 10 to 30 cells per spot.
- Copy-number subclones inferred per spot.
- Cost estimate about 3,100 EUR per section, about 250,000 EUR in total.

## Why It Was Dropped

Spots larger than the structures of interest. Thin rims of residual tumour along fibrosis after chemotherapy are often narrower than 55 µm, and spot-level mixtures could not separate them from stroma. The extra 180,000 EUR for Visium HD was accepted in the grant budget ([[visium-hd-cost-model]]).

Kept for the cost comparison and because reviewers of the [[frontier-starting-grant]] saw this version in the pre-proposal.
