# Pancreatic Cancer Early Detection Biomarkers for High-Risk Individuals: Insights From the PRECEDE Consortium.

## Summary

A consortium review of early detection of pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma in high-risk individuals: imaging-based surveillance as the foundation, organ-specific fluids as complementary tools, and liquid biopsy, radiomics, microbiome and multi-omics approaches as emerging options. It stresses that precursor lesions are hard to detect and grade non-invasively.

## Why It Matters

Relevant to Mei-Lin's argument for a 6-month exclusion: pancreatic cancer progresses quickly and the window in which a signal is both present and actionable may be short. Ines added it to the discussion reading list after [[northvale-analysis-meeting-2026-06-24]]. It also frames why a population cohort (average risk) answers a different question from high-risk surveillance.

## Caveats

Focus on high-risk groups, not the general population.
