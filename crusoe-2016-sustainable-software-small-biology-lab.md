# Walking the Talk: Adopting and Adapting Sustainable Scientific Software Development processes in a Small Biology Lab.

Crusoe MR, Brown CT. Journal of Open Research Software, 2016.

## Summary

Describes how a small lab with one full-time developer, several graduate students and a professor brought design, testing and open development to a sequence analysis tool used in research and production.

## Why It Matters

Closest analogue to our group: one bioinformatics engineer ([[tobias-wren]]) supporting students and postdocs who write research code. Ines liked that it is honest about trade-offs in a lab where research features always compete with maintenance. It shaped the idea that each tool needs a named second person, not a software team ([[every-group-tool-needs-two-maintainers]]).

## Caveats

Ten years old and about one tool; the tooling has changed, the staffing problem has not.
