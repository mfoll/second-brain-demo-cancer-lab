# MRD Statistical Analysis Plan v2

Statistical analysis plan for the St Aldric MRD analysis, version 2, signed 2025-12-15 by Dmitri and [[maja-kovac|Maja Kovac]] before the 2025-12-31 data freeze.

## Key Details

- Primary: molecular lead time over radiological recurrence ([[molecular-lead-time]]), median and IQR; interval-censored estimate as sensitivity analysis.
- Secondary: day-28 landmark sensitivity and specificity for recurrence within 24 months; per-layer performance; comparison with CA19-9.
- Positivity rule for variants ([[what-vaf-threshold-defines-a-positive-mrd-call]]); combined call as OR across layers with layer thresholds ([[should-the-mrd-call-combine-methylation-and-fragmentomics]]).
- Missing data: patients with fewer than two post-operative samples excluded from serial analyses only.

## Changes From v1

v1 (2025-06) had no CA19-9 comparison and no interval-censoring sensitivity analysis; both added after the [[mrd-interim-analysis-meeting-2025-12-03|interim meeting]].

## Note

The interval-censored sensitivity analysis was pre-specified but not reported in the submitted manuscript. Reviewer 3 asked for it; the revision reports it.
