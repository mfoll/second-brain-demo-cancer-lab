# Shallow WGS QC Report 2026-01

QC summary of all St Aldric shallow WGS data at the 2025-12-31 freeze, generated by the `varga-cfdna` pipeline and reviewed by Sofia on 2026-01-16.

## Key Details

- 1,402 samples, median depth 5.3x (range 2.1–9.8); 31 below 3x flagged, 14 re-sequenced.
- Median duplicate rate 9%; median mapping rate 97.8%.
- Modal fragment length 167 bp overall. Per-lot medians: 168 bp lot A, 165 bp lot B. Noted in the report as "possible lot effect, to check", which was not followed up until the leave-batch-out run in February.
- GC bias after fragment-level correction within tolerance for all but 6 samples.

## Why It Matters

The lot difference was visible here two months before the [[lab-meeting-2026-03-12|March lab meeting]]. A plain example of a QC flag that needed an owner and a deadline ([[batch-confounding-in-cfdna-studies]]).
