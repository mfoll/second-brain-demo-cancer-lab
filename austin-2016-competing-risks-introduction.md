# Introduction to the Analysis of Survival Data in the Presence of Competing Risks.

## Summary

A tutorial on competing risks. It explains why the complement of the Kaplan-Meier estimate overstates incidence when competing events occur, why the cumulative incidence function should be used instead, and the difference between modelling cause-specific hazards and modelling the cumulative incidence function (subdistribution hazards).

## Why It Matters

Lucas handed it to the group when the project opened in October 2025 as the shared vocabulary for the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]]. It is the main source for the [[competing-risks]] concept note.

## Caveats

Cardiovascular examples.
