# Ben Whitfield

Field application scientist for the vendor of the sequencing core's main short-read instruments. External contact; no affiliation hub in this vault.

## Role

Ben supports the core on library chemistry and run troubleshooting. He presented the vendor's 2027 instrument roadmap at [[sequencing-core-user-committee-2026-10-08]] and helped [[elias-moreau]] with low-input cfDNA library yields in early 2026.

## How We Work Together

Professional and useful on technical questions. Contacts go through [[rafael-ibarrondo]] or happen in committee; Ines does not meet him alone about procurement. Because Ines sits on the [[sequencing-core-user-committee]], which advises on instrument replacement, the contact is declared on the institute conflict-of-interest register. Ines has accepted no hospitality beyond meeting refreshments and declined a vendor-sponsored conference trip in 2026. The handling rules are in [[conflict-of-interest-handling-vendor-contacts]].

## Current Threads

- Low-input library protocol update: technical exchange only, through Elias and the core.
- Ines will recuse from the committee's recommendation vote on the 2027 instrument if the vendor is shortlisted.

## History

The first contact was in 2024 at a core training day on low-input libraries. In 2025 he arranged a loan of a new reagent kit for an evaluation run, organised and documented by [[rafael-ibarrondo]]'s team rather than by the group; the results went to the core and were shared with all users. The declaration on the register dates from September 2025, when the core started discussing instrument replacement and Ines realised the committee seat made a routine technical contact relevant.

## Boundaries

No group data, sample information or unpublished results go to Ben. Technical questions about protocols are fine and useful. Any proposal of a collaborative project, co-authorship or vendor-funded study goes through Rafael and the institute's procurement and integrity rules first, and Ines would raise it at the committee before agreeing to anything.
