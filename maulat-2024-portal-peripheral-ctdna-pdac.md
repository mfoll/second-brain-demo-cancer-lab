# Prognostic impact of circulating tumor DNA detection in portal and peripheral blood in resected pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma patients

Maulat C et al., Scientific Reports, 2024.

## Summary

Single-centre prospective study of 34 patients undergoing pancreaticoduodenectomy, with portal and peripheral blood drawn during surgery and over 12 post-operative months; ctDNA measured by droplet digital PCR for tumour mutations. Intraoperative ctDNA detection was associated with worse recurrence-free and overall survival. Portal sampling did not improve detection, and tissue mobilisation raised peripheral ctDNA levels without prognostic meaning.

## Caveats
