# Thursday 28 August 2025

## Closing the Visium HD pilot

The pilot cleared its stop rule comfortably, so the atlas goes ahead on Visium HD with 8 µm bins as the analysis unit. The evidence, the costing and the rejected alternatives are in the [[visium-hd-pilot-decision-2025-08-28|pilot decision]] and the [[visium-hd-pilot-report|pilot report]]; the bin-size reasoning lives in [[visium-hd-bin-size|Visium HD bin size]].

Benedikt's argument carried the day more than the UMI counts: the thin tumour rims along fibrosis only survive at 8 µm. Cell segmentation stays a secondary layer, with the question parked in [[should-the-atlas-use-bin2cell-segmentation|Should the atlas use bin2cell segmentation?]].
