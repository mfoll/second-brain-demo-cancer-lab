# Impact of Long-Term Plasma Storage on Cell-Free DNA Epigenetic Biomarker Studies.

## Summary

In 1,070 plasma samples stored for up to 14 years, cfDNA yield stayed largely stable, but longer storage was associated with more genomic DNA contamination, and contamination in turn reduced fragment integrity, library success and usable reads in 5-hydroxymethylcytosine sequencing. The authors recommend measures to limit genomic DNA contamination in long-term storage.

## Why It Matters

Found by [[elias-moreau]] a month before the [[northvale-sample-shipment-2025-09-23|shipment]], when the wet lab was planning extraction QC. It led to the genomic DNA contamination flag (high-molecular-weight fraction above 15%) in the [[northvale-storage-time-qc-report]] and is the main published reason the storage question is still open ([[does-storage-time-confound-the-northvale-methylation-signal]]). Northvale plasma has been stored 16 to 21 years, longer than any sample here.

## Caveats

5hmC sequencing rather than targeted bisulfite methylation; storage durations shorter than ours.
