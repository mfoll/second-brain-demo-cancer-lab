# Should Proteomics Be An Aurora Work Package?

Should Marta Kowalczyk's plasma proteomics be a separate aim of the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]], a work package inside another aim, or left out?

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> Answered on 2026-09-29: proteomics becomes work package 2.3 inside aim 2 (relapse detection after surgery), run on St Aldric post-surgery plasma only, with its own hypothesis that a protein score adds to cfDNA for earlier relapse detection.

## Why It Was Open

From the [[aurora-kickoff-2026-06-10]] until the mock panel, proteomics was aim 3, applied to both Northvale and St Aldric plasma. Two problems surfaced: aim 3 had no hypothesis independent of aims 1 and 2, and Northvale baseline aliquots are too small to share between cfDNA and proteomics under the [[corvane-biobank]] withdrawal rules.

## Evidence

- Panel discussion and scores: [[aurora-mock-panel-2026-09-29]], [[aurora-mock-panel-feedback]].
- Pilot data: [[kowalczyk-plasma-proteomics-pilot]].
- Funder scope: Rosa Lindgren's written confirmation that proteomics is in remit if it serves the cancer question ([[rosa-lindgren]]).
