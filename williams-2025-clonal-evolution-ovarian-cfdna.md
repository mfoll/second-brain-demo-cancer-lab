# Tracking clonal evolution during treatment in ovarian cancer using cell-free DNA.

Williams MJ et al., Nature, 2025.

## Summary

CloneSeq-SV combines single-cell whole-genome sequencing of the tumour with deep targeted sequencing of clone-specific structural variants in serial cfDNA, so the relative abundance of coexisting clones can be followed through treatment. In 18 patients with high-grade serous ovarian cancer followed from diagnosis to recurrence, resistance usually came from selective expansion of one or a few clones already present at diagnosis, often with distinctive genomic features such as whole-genome doubling or high-level oncogene amplification.

## Why It Matters

Ines used it to frame the MRD session of the [[tumour-evolution-symposium-2026]]: it shows that cfDNA can do more than say "tumour present or not", which is all our [[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study]] claims. In her session introduction it is the example of where MRD work is heading. [[dmitri-halvorsen]] noted that structural variants as markers sidestep the CH contamination problem we handle in the [[clonal-hematopoiesis-filter]], since CH rarely produces large tumour-like rearrangements.

## Caveats

Requires single-cell sequencing of the tumour first, which is not realistic for most pancreatic resections. Small cohort, one cancer type.
