# Group Retreat 2026

Two-day group retreat on 2026-09-17 and 2026-09-18 at the institute's Hollin House conference centre, organised by [[priya-raman]], to agree a research plan for 2027 and fix the practices that strained during a year of migrations.

## Outcome

A retreat held with the whole group and a written 2027 research plan. Reached: [[research-plan-2027]] was circulated on 2026-09-25 and accepted at [[lab-meeting-2026-10-08]].

## Current State

- Completed. 15 attended, including [[leila-farouk]]; [[elias-moreau]] joined day 1 only.
- The plan sets four 2027 priorities: the [[mrd-manuscript]] revision with external validation, the Northvale lead-time paper from [[lead-time-analysis]], completing the [[colorectal-metastasis-atlas]] cohort, and submitting the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]].
- Two practice changes adopted: review of every pipeline change ([[code-review-guidelines]]) and a split lab meeting format ([[lab-meeting-format]]).
- Follow-ups live in [[lead-tumour-evolution-group]] next actions and in the owning projects, not here.

## Scope and Boundaries

Planning, logistics and the retreat itself. The retreat could recommend; decisions affecting a single project were confirmed by its lead afterwards. Budget came from the group's core allocation (EUR 4,850 including venue, meals and travel).

## Working Context

Preparation started 2026-07-01. Priya ran an anonymous pre-retreat survey ([[retreat-2026-pre-survey]]) with 13 responses; the two strongest themes were "lab meetings run long and decide little" and "only Tobias can fix the pipelines". Those shaped the agenda ([[group-retreat-2026-agenda]]): day 1 for science (one session per umbrella project, each ending with a proposed 2027 milestone), day 2 for how we work.

What worked: project leads presenting a single slide of "what we will finish in 2027"; the session where students ran the discussion on lab meetings without Ines in the room for the first 30 minutes. What did not: the compute session ran over because the GPU request was still pending and everyone wanted to plan around both outcomes.

Details of the two days are in [[group-retreat-2026-09-17]].
