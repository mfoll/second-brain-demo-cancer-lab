# Patient Advisory Meeting 2025-10-22

First patient and public involvement meeting of the [[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study|Pancreatic cfDNA MRD Study]], chaired by [[anna-sorensen|Anna Sorensen]]. Four people took part: two patients treated for pancreatic cancer at other hospitals and two carers. From the group: Ines, [[dmitri-halvorsen|Dmitri Halvorsen]], [[priya-raman|Priya Raman]]. [[samuel-okoye|Samuel Okoye]] joined for the second half.

## What We Heard

- An early blood signal is only welcome if there is something to do with it. Participants were uneasy about a test that would announce recurrence months earlier with no change in treatment.
- False positives worry people more than missed early signals. One carer described months of anxiety after an ambiguous scan.
- The word "residual" is frightening after surgery that was described as curative. Suggested wording: "signs that the cancer may come back".

## What We Changed

- The manuscript reports specificity and the false-positive count in the abstract, not only sensitivity and lead time.
- The CH filter got higher priority, because CH is the main false-positive source ([[clonal-hematopoiesis]]).
- Lay wording in the revised leaflet ([[st-aldric-patient-information-leaflet-v2]]).
- The clinical meaning of lead time became an open question ([[what-lead-time-over-imaging-is-clinically-meaningful]]).

Next meeting planned after the manuscript revision.
