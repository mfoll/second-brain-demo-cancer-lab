# Northvale Storage-Time QC Report

## Summary

A QC report on how storage time and freezer history relate to cfDNA yield, genomic DNA contamination and methylation score in the 1,230 Northvale samples, prepared by [[elias-moreau]] and [[maja-kovac]] for [[northvale-analysis-meeting-2026-05-20]].

## Key Details

- Storage at assay: 16.1 to 21.4 years.
- cfDNA yield fell by about 4% per five years of storage; not significant after adjusting for recruitment centre.
- Genomic DNA contamination flag (high-molecular-weight fraction above 15%): 7.6% of samples; 7.8% of cases and 7.4% of controls. Strongly associated with centre 4 (longer processing delay) and with two freezer moves.
- Methylation score versus storage years in controls: slope 0.02 SD per year (95% CI -0.01 to 0.05).
- Within matched sets, storage time differs by at most 0.25 years by design.

## Caveats

Score and storage time are close to independent in controls, but cases with the longest time to diagnosis can only come from the earliest recruitment years, so storage time and time to diagnosis are correlated across matched sets even though they are balanced within them. That is the open part of [[does-storage-time-confound-the-northvale-methylation-signal]].
