# Evidence categories in systematic assessment of cancer overdiagnosis.

## Summary

A review of the categories of evidence used to assess cancer overdiagnosis: surveillance data, studies of the true prevalence of undiagnosed cancer, studies of diagnostic use and its effect on incidence and mortality, and studies of changes in treatment. It discusses the strengths and weaknesses of each.

## Why It Matters

Mei-Lin sent it after the June meeting when the discussion turned to what the Northvale paper may and may not claim. It supports the group's stance that a prediagnostic association study informs natural history and cannot speak to overdiagnosis or benefit ([[prediagnostic-detection-is-not-screening-evidence]]).

## Caveats

Conceptual review.
