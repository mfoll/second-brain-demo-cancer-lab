# From concept to clinic: a roadmap for DNA methylation biomarkers in liquid biopsies.

## Summary

A review of why few methylation liquid-biopsy markers reach clinical use, covering choice of liquid biopsy source, discovery workflow, targeted validation in clinical sample series, and strategies for accuracy and reproducibility.

## Why It Matters

Useful framing for the discussion section of the lead-time paper: Northvale sits early on this roadmap, as a natural-history study, not a validation of a test. Ines used its stages in the [[northvale-lead-time-slides-2026-06-24|June slides]] to show where the study stops. It also supports [[prediagnostic-detection-is-not-screening-evidence]].
