# Sun-hee Park

Halo site PI at Kelstow Cancer Institute and the consortium's senior biostatistician. Kelstow runs the ddPCR hotspot assay, the simplest and cheapest of the six. Sun-hee is the WP4 deputy lead and co-wrote the [[halo-wp4-analysis-plan]] with Ines.

## Role

- Site PI, Kelstow Cancer Institute: ddPCR panel of eight hotspot targets, tumour-informed where tissue is available ([[halo-site-and-assay-overview]]).
- WP4 deputy: owns the statistical sections of the analysis plan, in particular how limit of blank and limit of detection are estimated for count-based and sequencing-based assays on the same footing ([[limit-of-blank-and-limit-of-detection]]).
- Reviews every WP4 report before it goes to the steering committee.

## How We Work Together

The most careful reader in the consortium. She insists that every comparison in the benchmark be pre-specified, and she was the one who pushed at the [[halo-steering-committee-2025-11-20]] for a patient tier in the truth set, arguing that contrived dilutions only answer analytical questions. She and [[maja-kovac]] got on well at the annual meeting and now exchange code for bootstrap confidence intervals.

Disagreements are argued in writing and she changes her mind when shown data. On the pre-analytics question she preferred statistical correction for legacy samples over discarding them; the steering committee followed a mixed position ([[should-halo-harmonise-preanalytics-or-correct-statistically]]).

## Current Threads

- Kelstow is the one site that has not returned truth-set results; a ddPCR reader failed in August and the replacement was installed in October. Results promised for mid-November.
- How to report assays that cannot produce a fragmentomics score on the contrived tier.
- Reviewing the federated concept note, sceptical that 60 patient samples split across sites can train anything ([[can-federated-training-match-a-pooled-classifier]]).

## History With The Group

Sun-hee reviewed the St Aldric MRD analysis plan informally in 2025, before Halo had a formal role, and her comments on interval censoring of lead time anticipated the point Reviewer 3 later raised on the [[mrd-manuscript]].
