# Review Report For Annals Of Liquid Biopsy, June 2026

Report on a manuscript claiming early detection of a solid cancer from cfDNA in a case-control design. Generic record only; the manuscript is confidential.

## Main Points Raised

- Cases were sampled at diagnosis and controls from a different setting, so pre-analytical differences could explain much of the signal. Ines asked for evidence that sample handling was matched.
- Performance was reported only at a single threshold chosen on the same data.
- The title implied screening use, which the design cannot support.

## Recommendation

Major revision, with a note to the editor that the conclusions should be narrowed regardless. Two weeks late: submitted 2026-06-29, after the Halo annual meeting.

## Notes For Us

This is the same reasoning we apply to ourselves in the Northvale [[lead-time-analysis]], where cases and controls come from the same cohort and blood-draw dates are matched ([[case-control-sample-selection]]). Ines considered whether the topic overlapped with our own work enough to be a conflict and decided it did not: different cancer, different design, no shared authors.
