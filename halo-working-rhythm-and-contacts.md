# Halo Working Rhythm And Contacts

How Halo work runs week to week, for anyone in the group who joins a Halo thread.

## Rhythm

- Steering committee every three to four months, papers a week ahead; Corvane gives a standing policy update until the clause is adopted.
- WP4 call on the second Thursday of each month, 60 minutes, Ines chairs; notes go to the WP4 folder at the hub.
- Fortnightly modelling call: [[arjun-mehta]], [[sofia-reyes-calderon]], [[leila-farouk]].
- Annual meeting in June; 2026 was at Corvane ([[halo-annual-meeting-2026]]).
