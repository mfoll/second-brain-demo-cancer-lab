# Halo Annual Meeting 2026-06-10

The Halo annual meeting at Corvane, 2026-06-10 to 2026-06-12. 41 participants from six sites and two patient-advisory observers. Programme in [[halo-annual-meeting-2026-programme]]; Ines's WP4 talk in [[halo-annual-meeting-2026-wp4-slides]].

## At a Glance

Day one: site updates and first experience with the truth set shipped on 2026-05-19. Day two: the WP4 day, built around the pilot-panel results. Day three: governance, data flow, consortium paper, steering committee in closed session.

## Decisions

- Commutability is a result, not a nuisance. The pilot panel ([[halo-pilot-panel-results-2026-04]]) showed that both fragmentomics assays behave differently on sheared genomic DNA than on nucleosomal material. The consortium paper will report it with per-assay detail ([[commutability-of-reference-materials]]).
- Fragmentomics assays are not scored on the mutation-dilution tier; they are scored on patient and donor plasma only.
- Every clinical sensitivity figure is reported with lead time over imaging, at [[isabel-montes]]'s request.
- Upload deadline for truth-set data fixed at 2026-09-30.
- Each site's data access rules to be documented for the hub by end of July. This is where Corvane's gap became formal: [[ngozi-adeyemi]] and [[eva-novak]] confirmed that our policy does not allow the multilateral clause.

## Discussion Worth Keeping

[[fatima-benali]] and Dmitri presented side by side on size profiles; the room accepted that two independent fragment assays showing the same artefact was convincing. [[sun-hee-park]] warned against post hoc dropping of awkward samples; the agreement was to report everything and flag what is not commutable. [[anna-sorensen]] asked how patients would hear about results from a benchmark; the answer, a plain-language summary with the paper, became a WP6 action.
