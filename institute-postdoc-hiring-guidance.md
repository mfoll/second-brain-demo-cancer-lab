# Institute Postdoc Hiring Guidance

Corvane HR guidance for recruiting fixed-term research staff, 2025 edition, sent by [[aisha-bello]] at the start of [[postdoc-hiring-2026]].

## Summary

Sets the minimum process for postdoc hiring: advert approval by HR, criteria fixed before applications open, a panel of at least three including one member from outside the hiring group, independent scoring before discussion, and documented reasons for the final choice.

## Key Details

- Panels should not be all one gender; ours had three women and one man.
- Informal sessions with the team may contribute to scoring only if every candidate gets the same session and the feedback is collected in writing.
- Unsuccessful interviewed candidates are offered feedback within two weeks.
- A reserve candidate may be held for up to three months.

## Why It Matters

It is why [[jonas-eriksen]] sat on the panel and why the team session was a fixed 30 minutes for every candidate. The three-month reserve limit set [[mateo-silva]]'s reserve date of 2026-12-31.
