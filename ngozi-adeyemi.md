# Ngozi Adeyemi

Halo data manager at the Brightwater coordinating centre. She runs the consortium data hub, keeps the sample registry, and holds the unblinding key for the [[halo-truth-set]]. Nothing moves in or out of the hub without her.

## Role

- Maintains the central data hub where sites deposit truth-set results, and the [[halo-data-management-plan]].
- Custodian of the truth-set blinding key; follows the [[halo-truth-set-blinding-and-unblinding-rules]].
- Organised the shipment of 720 aliquots on 2026-05-19 with the courier and the sites ([[halo-truth-set-shipment-2026-05-19]], [[halo-truth-set-shipment-manifest]]).
- First contact for upload problems, file naming and checksum mismatches.

## How We Work Together

Precise and fast. She sends checklists, not prose, and expects the same back. Our day-to-day contact on her side is [[yara-castellanos]], our data steward, and the two of them worked out the upload format for our shallow-WGS results in June. Ines involves her early in anything touching data flow, because she knows which consortium rules are flexible and which are written into the agreement.

She was the first to spot, in July, that Corvane's upload credentials would be useless for the patient tier under our current policy, and flagged it to [[maximilian-auer]] before it became a surprise at a steering committee.
