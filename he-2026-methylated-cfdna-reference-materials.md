# Development, characterization, and inter-laboratory validation of methylated human cell free DNA candidate reference materials.

He Z, Konigshofer Y, Garlick R, et al. Clinical Epigenetics, 2026.

## Summary

Two candidate reference materials for cfDNA methylation measurement were built: one series mixing in vitro methylated and native cfDNA simulant from the same cell line at fractions from 0% to 100%, and a two-component material from amplified genomic DNA. Methylation at three targets was confirmed by ddPCR. Six laboratories then measured the materials with their own methods, including methylation-specific qPCR, digital PCR, immunoprecipitation-based assays and whole-genome bisulfite sequencing. Designed and observed methylation fractions correlated well across labs, and each method was reproducible.

## Why It Matters

The design is close to our own problem: six labs, six methods, one material. [[tomasz-wilk]] circulated it to WP3 in July as a model for a second Halo contrived batch in 2027. For WP4 it is a reference point for how to report methylation recovery per method rather than one pooled number, which is what [[halo-interim-benchmark-report-2026-10]] does for Hollinmoor.

## Caveats

Simulant cfDNA from a cell line, so fragment profile and commutability for fragmentomics are not addressed ([[are-contrived-reference-materials-commutable-for-fragmentomics]]). We read the abstract and the methods summary only.
