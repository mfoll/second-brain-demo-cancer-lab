# Genetics of smoking and risk of clonal hematopoiesis.

## Summary

Observational and two-sample Mendelian randomization analyses of smoking and two forms of clonal hematopoiesis. Smoking was strongly associated with mosaic chromosomal alterations in both analyses, but in the Mendelian randomization analysis it was not associated with CHIP defined by point mutations. The authors suggest smoking may shape the fitness of some clones more than others.

## Why It Matters

Central to the smoking question in the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]]. If smoking affects CHIP only weakly, it is a weaker confounder of the CH and cancer association than feared for most cancers, but it still drives lung and bladder cancer strongly, so the site-specific models need the best smoking adjustment we can get. Lucas cites it in [[how-should-we-adjust-for-smoking-in-the-ch-cancer-risk-analysis]].

## Caveats

Mendelian randomization assumptions; CHIP and mosaic alterations are measured differently from our panel calls.
