# CH VAF Threshold Comparison

## Summary

Report by [[lucas-brandt]] and [[hannah-lindqvist]] comparing CH definitions at VAF 2% and 1% in the subcohort, prepared for [[ch-analysis-meeting-2026-09-16]].

## Key Details

- Prevalence: 708 participants (11.9%) at 2%, 1,164 (19.5%) at 1%.
- Technical replicate concordance (96 samples sequenced twice): 97% for variants at 2% or more, 84% for variants between 1% and 2%.
- Hazard ratio for any solid cancer with follow-up to 2023, adjusted model: 1.18 (1.01 to 1.38) at 2%; 1.11 (0.97 to 1.27) at 1%.
- Most added carriers at 1% have a single DNMT3A or TET2 variant.

## Caveats

The weaker estimate at 1% may reflect dilution by noisier calls or a real dose-response; the data cannot tell.
