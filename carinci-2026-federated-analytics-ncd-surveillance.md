# Federated analytics for non-communicable disease surveillance in the European health data space: a scoping review and conceptual framework

Carinci F, Fava S, Štotl I, Nicholson N. Frontiers in Public Health, 2026.

## Summary

A scoping review of federated and distributed analytical approaches for producing chronic disease indicators without moving person-level data, with a conceptual framework that links distributed statistics, privacy-preserving infrastructure and governance requirements. The literature it maps groups into distributed regression, federated infrastructures, and a third strand on governance and infrastructure.

## Why It Matters

Read after [[data-governance-board-2026-09-10]], where a board member asked whether federated analysis could replace the consortium clause. It is a fair map of what federated methods do well (indicators, regression, comparable estimates across sites) and is useful background for [[federated-cfdna-learning]]. It does not change Ines's view that a journal-audited validation of a locked classifier is easier to defend with a controlled transfer ([[federated-analysis-is-not-a-substitute-for-a-transfer-clause]]).

## Caveats

Surveillance indicators, not diagnostic model validation. Scoping review: no pooled evidence on accuracy or leakage.
