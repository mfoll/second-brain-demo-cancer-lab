# Yara Castellanos

Data steward, 50% with the group and 50% with the institute's research data office. She is the named steward for all clinical-linked group data under the [[group-data-management-plan]].

## Role

- Author of the group DMP with Ines ([[lab-data-management-plan]]) and owner of its annual access review.
- Handles data access requests to and from the group, including Halo consortium requests, and checks them against the [[corvane-research-data-policy]].
- Led the triage of Kestrel data before retirement ([[kestrel-data-inventory]], [[archive-kestrel-data]]).
- Bridges to institute governance: [[ruth-abernathy]]'s board and [[victor-lindahl]] for data protection.

## How We Work Together

Yara's group time is Monday to Wednesday. She wants requests that touch personal data in writing, with the dataset, purpose and recipient named; a chat message is not enough. She is direct about what the policy does not allow and good at finding what it does.

Her institute half matters to us: she sees the [[data-access-policy-revision]] drafts and can tell us early what the consortium transfer clause will and will not permit. That clause decides when Halo samples can be used for external validation in the [[mrd-manuscript]] revision.
