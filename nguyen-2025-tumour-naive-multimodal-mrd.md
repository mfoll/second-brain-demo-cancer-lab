# Tumor-naïve multimodal profiling of circulating tumor DNA to detect minimal residual disease in solid tumors

Nguyen T et al., Therapeutic Advances in Medical Oncology, 2025.

## Summary

Retrospective development of a tumour-naive ctDNA assay combining amplicon and hybridisation-capture mutation detection with copy-number and fragment-length profiles, evaluated on blood from about 950 cancer patients and 570 non-cancer donors, then on post-surgical samples from breast and colorectal cancer patients against clinical recurrence, with a direct comparison to a tumour-informed method. CH variants, mostly in TP53, were common and had to be excluded. Copy number and fragment length improved sensitivity clearly in metastatic disease but only modestly in early-stage disease.

## Why It Matters

Read while writing the [[mrd-manuscript|manuscript]] discussion in 2026-01. It supports our multi-layer design ([[should-the-mrd-call-combine-methylation-and-fragmentomics]]) and its modest early-stage gain is a realistic expectation for the fragmentomics layer after surgery.

## Caveats

Breast and colorectal, not pancreatic; tumour-naive design differs from our mixed design.
