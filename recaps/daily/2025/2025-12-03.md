# Wednesday 3 December 2025

## First full MRD interim results

The monthly [[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study|MRD study]] meeting saw the first interim results across all extracted samples. Headline sensitivity, specificity and lead time are recorded in the [[mrd-interim-analysis-meeting-2025-12-03|interim analysis meeting]]; fragmentomics helped most where tumour-informed variants were missing.

## Is a four-month lead time useful?

Samuel's challenge was the clinically important one: lead time only matters if it changes management. It is now an open question, [[what-lead-time-over-imaging-is-clinically-meaningful|What lead time over imaging is clinically meaningful?]].

## Interval censoring

Maja pointed out that lead time against three-monthly imaging is interval-censored and that a naive median overstates it. We put her sensitivity analysis into the [[mrd-statistical-analysis-plan-v2|statistical analysis plan v2]] but not into the main analysis. Worth remembering if a statistical reviewer raises it.
