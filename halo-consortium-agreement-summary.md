# Halo Consortium Agreement Summary

Our working summary of the Halo consortium agreement, signed by all six institutions between 2025-02-10 and 2025-03-03 and in force from the kick-off on 2025-03-18 to 2028-12-31. Written by Ines after the signature, checked by [[chloe-armitage]] in the grants office and by [[eva-novak]] for Halo.

## Summary

Six partners, one coordinator (Brightwater University Hospital), six work packages. Corvane leads WP4 (analysis). Total budget about 3.1 million EUR over four years from a public research programme; Corvane's share is 412,000 EUR, mostly WP4 staff time and sequencing of our truth-set aliquots.

## Key Details

- Work packages: WP1 coordination (Brightwater, [[maximilian-auer]]); WP2 pre-analytics and SOPs (Westmere); WP3 reference materials and truth set (Hollinmoor, [[tomasz-wilk]]); WP4 analysis (Corvane, Ines); WP5 ethics, legal and data protection (Brightwater, [[eva-novak]]); WP6 clinical validation (Westmere, [[isabel-montes]]).
- Governance: steering committee of the six site PIs plus an independent chair ([[henrik-dahl]]); decisions by simple majority, changes to data rules by unanimity.
- Data clause (article 9): pseudonymised data generated on consortium samples are deposited in a central hub at Brightwater and may be accessed by any partner for consortium purposes. This is a multilateral arrangement; it is not a set of bilateral agreements.
- Each partner must ensure that its internal rules permit article 9 before accessing hub data. Article 9 is implemented through a separate multi-party data sharing agreement ([[halo-data-sharing-agreement-template]]); four sites have signed it, Corvane and Thornvale have not. Corvane is blocked because the current institute policy ([[corvane-data-access-policy-2023]]) only permits bilateral transfers.
- Publications: consortium papers list all sites; site-specific papers using hub data need steering committee approval.
- Background IP stays with each partner; foreground IP from joint work is jointly owned, with a later agreement to settle shares. Models trained on hub data are not mentioned ([[who-owns-models-trained-on-halo-data]]).

## Caveats

A summary, not the legal text. For any decision, read the signed agreement in the grants office files.
