# Tuesday 29 September 2026

## Aurora mock panel: three aims become two

At the [[aurora-mock-panel-2026-09-29|mock panel]], chaired remotely by Leah, the clearest message was that three aims read like three projects sharing a budget. The proteomics aim depended on the other two and competed with them for the same small Northvale aliquots. Marta proposed merging it into the MRD aim before I did. Proteomics becomes a work package on St Aldric post-surgery plasma, where volume is not limiting and a protein score can be tested against the same relapse endpoint.

Scores, the rejected options and the panel's comments are in the panel note and the [[aurora-mock-panel-feedback|written feedback]]. The aims sequence is in [[aurora-aims-history|Aurora aims history]]. The general lesson is now [[dependent-aims-risk|Dependent aims risk]].

Leah also asked that the first-year exclusion in the Northvale aim be stated on the aims page itself. The panel accepted the reverse-causation handling but did not want it hidden in the methods.
