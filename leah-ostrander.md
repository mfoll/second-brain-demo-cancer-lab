# Leah Ostrander

Ines's former PhD supervisor and long-standing mentor; professor of cancer genetics at another institute. External contact without an affiliation hub in this vault.

## Role

Leah is the person Ines calls about careers, difficult supervision situations and large grant applications. She has chaired funding panels for charities similar to Aurora and has been on the receiving end of most kinds of reviewer comment.

## How We Work Together

- A call roughly every two months, more often near a large deadline. Ines writes a short list of questions in advance.
- Leah chaired the [[aurora-mock-panel-2026-09-29]], remotely. Her summary at the end, that three aims made the programme read like three projects sharing a budget, is what moved the application to two aims.
- She reads the near-final case for support of large applications; for Aurora that read is planned for early November ([[aurora-programme-grant-2027]]).
- She does not want to be named in applications or acknowledgements.

## Influence

- Ines's habit of weekly one-to-ones and written committee preparation comes from Leah's lab ([[supervision-meeting-structure]]).
- The stance in [[mock-panels-are-worth-the-time]] is partly hers.

## History

- Supervised Ines's PhD on clonal evolution in colorectal cancer. Ines left her lab about fifteen years ago; they have stayed in contact since.
- Read the [[frontier-starting-grant]] proposal in February 2025 and told Ines to put the design figure on page one; all three reviewers later referred to it ([[frontier-application-lessons]]).
- Recommended George Papadakis as a possible external examiner for statistical methods theses.

## Boundaries

Leah gives advice and reads drafts; she does not want to know confidential details about students or staff, and Ines describes situations in general terms when asking for advice.
