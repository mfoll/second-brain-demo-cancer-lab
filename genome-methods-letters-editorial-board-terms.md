# Genome Methods Letters Editorial Board Terms

The appointment letter and board handbook Ines received when joining the Genome Methods Letters editorial board on 2025-03-10.

## Key Terms

- Term 2025-03 to 2028-02, renewable once.
- Expected load: up to three handling-editor assignments a month, a quarterly board call, and one in-person board meeting a year when budget allows.
- Conflict rules: no handling of papers from one's own institution, from co-authors of the last three years, or from consortia one belongs to. For Ines this excludes Corvane and all Halo consortium papers.
- Honorarium: none. Open-access fee waiver for one paper a year as corresponding author.
- Confidentiality: manuscripts and reviews may not be stored outside the journal system except as personal notes without content.

## Why It Matters

Sets the limits in [[review-workload-policy]] and explains why reviews and handling stay generic in the vault. [[lena-vogt]] joined in 2023 and is on a similar term.
