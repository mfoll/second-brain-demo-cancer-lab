# Should Lab Meetings Be Split Into Science And Operations?

## Question

Lab meetings in 2025 and 2026 mixed project updates, operational announcements and science talks, often ran over 100 minutes, and ended without clear decisions ([[retreat-2026-pre-survey]]: 4 of 13 agreed they end with clear decisions). Should operations and science be separated?

## Current Answer

> [!success]
> Yes. Adopted at the [[group-retreat-2026]]: each fortnightly meeting opens with a strict 15-minute operations block with written decisions, then one 30-minute science slot. Started on 2026-10-08.

## Rejected Options

- Separate monthly operations meeting: one more meeting, and students would skip it.
- Operations entirely by email: decisions without discussion produced confusion in 2025.

## Review

Review after one quarter, at the first operations block of January 2027. Details in [[lab-meeting-format]]; the previous format is in [[lab-meeting-format-2025]].
