# cfDNA Extraction SOP v3

Standard operating procedure for cfDNA extraction from frozen plasma in the group's wet lab, version 3, effective 2025-03-03. Author [[elias-moreau|Elias Moreau]], approved by Dmitri.

## Key Details

- Input: 2–4 ml plasma, thawed once on ice; second centrifugation at 16,000 g for 10 minutes before extraction.
- Column-based extraction kit, elution in 40 µl.
- Batches of up to 24 including one pooled plasma control.
- Records: kit lot, operator, date, plasma volume. v3 required the lot to be recorded only in the lab log.
- Acceptance and logging are defined separately in [[cfdna-extraction-qc]].

## Known Gaps

v3 did not require randomised extraction order or bridging samples for a new kit lot. Both are added in v4, drafted in 2026-10 after the [[extraction-kit-lot-change-2025-06-16|lot change]] was found to be confounded with time point.
