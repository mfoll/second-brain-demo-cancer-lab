# Northvale Biobank Sample Request 2025

## Summary

The application to the biobank access committee for 1,230 plasma aliquots, submitted on 2025-05-30 under Mei-Lin Zhou's name with [[maja-kovac]] as contact. Approved at [[biobank-access-committee-2025-06-18]].

## Key Details

- Request: one 0.5 ml straw pair (1 ml) per participant, 410 cases and 820 controls, plus up to 14 spare aliquots from the same participants for failed extractions.
- Justification: scientific rationale, [[northvale-power-calculation]], ethics coverage ([[northvale-ethics-amendment-2025]]), and the last-aliquot check.
- Retrieval plan sorted by freezer and rack position at [[paolo-ferrante]]'s suggestion.
- Destination: Corvane sequencing core, cfDNA extraction by [[elias-moreau]].
- Returns: unused spares returned within three months.

## Caveats

No spare was needed: all 14 were returned on 2025-11-04. The request did not anticipate later control replacements; the three replacements in 2026 went through as a minor variation under the same approval.
