# Atlas Tissue Access Agreement

Agreement between the Corvane Institute tissue unit and the Westmere Cancer Centre for use of archived liver metastasis blocks and limited clinical data in the [[colorectal-metastasis-atlas]]. Signed 2025-05-16.

## Key Details

- Covers up to 50 metastasis pairs, research use only, sections and derived data.
- Permits publication of de-identified derived data. Does not mention images explicitly, which matters for the [[atlas-data-portal]] ([[what-access-model-for-the-atlas-data-portal]]).
- Remaining tissue returns to Westmere after the project; no third-party transfer of tissue.
- Clinical data limited to the variables in [[atlas-clinical-annotation-table]].
