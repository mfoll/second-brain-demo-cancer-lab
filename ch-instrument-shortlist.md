# CH Instrument Shortlist

Working sheet kept by [[lucas-brandt]] listing candidate germline instruments for clonal hematopoiesis, drawn mainly from [[liu-2025-germline-variation-clonal-hematopoiesis]] and earlier CH GWAS. Updated after the 2026-09-03 call with [[hiroshi-tanabe]].

## Contents

For each candidate variant: locus, CH subtype it predisposes to, effect size and source GWAS, discovery sample age range, nearest genes, and a pleiotropy flag (known association with cancer or with telomere length, smoking or blood counts).

## State On 2026-09-04

- 31 candidates. 12 flagged for likely direct effects on cancer risk (TERT region, DNA repair genes), 6 more uncertain.
- 13 remain as a "clean" set with a plausible haematopoiesis-only mechanism. Lucas estimates they explain only a small fraction of CH liability, so the instrument would be weak.
- Two single loci noted as candidates for a cis design ([[van-der-graaf-2025-mr-link-2]]).
- Discovery samples older than 70 flagged for survival bias ([[smit-2019-survival-bias-mendelian-randomization]]).

## Caveats

Working document, not reviewed by Hiroshi yet. Effect sizes are from different GWAS and not harmonised.
