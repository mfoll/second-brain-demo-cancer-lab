# Aurora Aims Draft v3

## Summary

The current aims page of the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]], circulated on 2026-10-12 after the [[aurora-mock-panel-2026-09-29]]. Two aims.

## Key Details

- Aim 1, Timing the first signal: estimate when cfDNA methylation signals of pancreatic and colorectal cancer appear before diagnosis, by time-to-diagnosis window, in 410 cases and 820 matched controls from the [[northvale-health-cohort]]. Cases diagnosed within one year of blood draw are excluded from the main analysis and reported separately. Lead [[maja-kovac]].
- Aim 2, Detecting relapse: detect recurrence after pancreatic cancer surgery earlier than imaging. WP 2.1 fragmentomics classifier, WP 2.2 methylation panel, WP 2.3 plasma proteomics ([[marta-kowalczyk]]). Builds on the 212-patient [[st-aldric-mrd-cohort]] plus a prospective extension of 150 patients. Lead [[dmitri-halvorsen]].
- 590 words; the timeline figure now runs both aims from year 1.
