# Aurora Programme Grant 2027

A five-year Programme Grant application to the Aurora Cancer Foundation that would fund the pancreatic cancer work beyond 2027, from pre-diagnostic signals to relapse after surgery. Co-applicant: [[marta-kowalczyk]] (proteomics). Funder deadline 2026-12-04.

## Outcome

A complete application submitted through the grants office by 2026-11-20 and to the Foundation by 2026-12-04, with two aims, a budget under the 2.5 M EUR call ceiling and director sign-off.

## Current State

- Aims are at v3: two aims, see [[aurora-aims-draft-v3]]. Aim 1 times pre-diagnostic cfDNA methylation signals in the [[northvale-early-detection-study]]; aim 2 detects recurrence after surgery in the [[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study]], with plasma proteomics as work package 2.3.
- The change from three aims to two was decided at the [[aurora-mock-panel-2026-09-29]]. The full trail is in [[aurora-aims-history]].
- Budget stands at 2.41 M EUR after the [[aurora-budget-review-2026-10-15]]; the proteomics share is provisionally 380 k EUR and still open ([[how-much-of-the-aurora-budget-goes-to-proteomics]]).
- Whether Brightwater joins aim 2 as a second recruitment site is undecided ([[should-aurora-include-brightwater-as-second-mrd-site]]).
- The case for support is about 70% drafted; the lay summary and data management section are not started.

## Scope and Boundaries

This Project holds the application: aims, budget, letters, panel feedback and the submission route. The scientific results it cites belong to the two studies and their child Projects. Preliminary data quoted in the application must already be in a group note or manuscript; nothing goes into the case for support that the group would not stand behind at an interview.

## Working Context

The programme builds on two lines that were funded separately until now. The Northvale nested case-control study (410 cases, 820 controls) gives the pre-diagnostic side; the St Aldric MRD cohort of 212 post-surgery patients gives the relapse side. The pitch is that the same cfDNA features carry information across the whole timeline. Reviewers at the mock panel found three aims diluted that message and made aim 3 depend on samples the first two aims also needed. Proteomics now runs only on St Aldric post-surgery plasma, where aliquot volume is not limiting; Northvale aliquots stay reserved for cfDNA. See [[dependent-aims-risk]].

Rosa Lindgren at the Foundation confirmed on 2026-07-08 that a two-institution programme with one lead applicant is eligible, and that letters from the hospital partners count towards the feasibility score ([[rosa-lindgren]]).
