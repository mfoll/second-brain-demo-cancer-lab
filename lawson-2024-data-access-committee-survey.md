# Achieving Procedural Parity in Managing Access to Genomic and Related Health Data: A Global Survey of Data Access Committee Members

Lawson J, Rahimzadeh V, Baek J, Dove ES. Biopreservation and Biobanking, 2024.

## Summary

A survey of data access committee members attached to genomic data repositories worldwide, asking how their committees operate, where requests get stuck, and what would improve review quality. The authors found that operations and systemic problems are broadly similar across repositories and regions, which argues for shared procedures rather than each committee inventing its own.

## Why It Matters

[[ruth-abernathy]] circulated it before [[data-governance-board-2026-05-14]] when the board discussed whether consortium requests need a standing [[data-access-committee]] function or can stay with the full board. It supports the line in draft 3 ([[data-access-policy-draft-3]]) that consortium uses get a defined, documented review with a target turnaround, instead of case-by-case board discussion.

## Caveats

Survey of committee members, not of requesters; it says nothing about how long researchers actually wait. Repository DACs review many similar requests, while our board sees a few heterogeneous ones a month, so procedural lessons transfer better than volume metrics.
