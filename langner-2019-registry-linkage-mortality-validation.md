# Individual mortality information in the German Pharmacoepidemiological Research Database (GePaRD): a validation study using a record linkage with a large cancer registry.

## Summary

A validation of vital status and date of death in a claims database against official mortality information obtained by record linkage with a cancer registry, in about 256,000 women. The study reports the performance of the linkage and the agreement between sources on who died and when.

## Why It Matters

Death ascertainment matters for the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]], where non-cancer death is a competing event, and for censoring in the cohort. This paper was the reference [[lucas-brandt]] used to argue that the 2025 extract must include cause of death from the registry's death-certificate link, not only vital status. Daniel agreed and the variable was added.

## Caveats

Claims data validation; our deaths come directly from the registry.
