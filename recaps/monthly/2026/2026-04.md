# April 2026

## Fragmentomics classifier settles on a stacked design

The month's main decision corrected the one from March. At the [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-04-02|2 April design review]] the group found that the batch problem in the [[fragmentomics-classifier|Fragmentomics Classifier]] came from fragment-length features only. Logistic regression is kept for length features with a bridging-sample correction, and gradient boosting is kept for end motifs, stacked together. The March rationale still stands for length features. Anyone asking why gradient boosting was dropped should read both the [[lab-meeting-2026-03-12|March lab meeting]] and the [[classifier-design-review-2026-04-02|April review]].

## Northvale lead-time signal sits close to diagnosis

The [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-04-22|22 April meeting]] showed that the Northvale methylation signal is concentrated within two years of blood draw. The exclusion question was opened but not decided; it was settled in June. A new question links the signal to clonal hematopoiesis.

## Open at month end

- Which early-diagnosis exclusion the [[lead-time-analysis|Lead-Time Analysis]] uses for its main model.
