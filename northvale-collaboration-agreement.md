# Northvale Collaboration Agreement

## Summary

The collaboration agreement between the group and the Northvale cohort steering group, signed on 2025-04-24, covering the [[northvale-early-detection-study]] and, through a 2025-10 annex, the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]].

## Key Details

- The cohort PI ([[mei-lin-zhou]]) or a named delegate is co-author on every paper and approves any public communication of results, including conference abstracts.
- Data and samples are used only for the approved questions; new questions need a new application to the steering group.
- Derived data (methylation scores, CH calls) are returned to the cohort within 12 months of publication for use by other approved projects.
- Unused samples are returned to the biobank.
- Disputes go first to the steering group chair, then to the institute director ([[margarethe-sell]]).
