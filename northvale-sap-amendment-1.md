# Northvale SAP Amendment 1

## Summary

The one amendment to the [[northvale-statistical-analysis-plan]], signed on 2026-06-30. It implements the [[northvale-analysis-meeting-2026-06-24|2026-06-24 decision]] on reverse causation.

## Key Details

- Main model: exclude cases with less than 12 months from blood draw to diagnosis, together with their matched controls (27 matched sets).
- All four time-to-diagnosis windows reported in the main results table with case counts.
- Named sensitivity analyses: "6-month exclusion" (requested by [[mei-lin-zhou]]) and "no exclusion".
- A short paragraph records that the cohort PI prefers the 6-month exclusion and why.
- Adds a CH sensitivity analysis for the 86 participants who are both in the case-control set and the CH subcohort ([[can-ch-explain-part-of-the-northvale-methylation-signal]]).

## Caveats

The amendment was written after the window results were seen. The paper must say so; the windows themselves were prespecified in v1.0, only the choice of main-model exclusion was not.
