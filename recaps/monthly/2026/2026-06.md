# June 2026

## Halo comes to Corvane

We hosted the [[halo-annual-meeting-2026|Halo Annual Meeting]] ([[recaps/daily/2026/2026-06-10|10 June]]). Its practical result for us was a formal record that Corvane's data access policy cannot yet accommodate multilateral transfer; the minutes went out on 24 June. That gap later turned out to block the MRD revision.

## Aurora grant starts

Planning for the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027|Aurora Programme Grant]] began on 10 June with three aims and Marta Kowalczyk as co-applicant. The Northvale aliquot volume concern was noted but left unresolved.

## Northvale reverse-causation decision

On [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-06-24|24 June]] the [[lead-time-analysis|Lead-Time Analysis]] adopted a first-year exclusion for the main model, with all time-to-diagnosis windows reported separately. Mei-Lin Zhou's preference for a 6-month exclusion is recorded as dissent and kept as a named sensitivity analysis. Maja's SAP amendment followed on 30 June.
