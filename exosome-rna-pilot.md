# Exosome RNA Pilot

A short wet-lab pilot that tested whether RNA from plasma extracellular vesicles could add a tumour signal to cfDNA for pancreatic cancer monitoring. It was stopped on 2025-06-19 and superseded by the targeted methylation panel ([[methylation-panel-design]]).

## Outcome

A decision on whether exosome RNA should become a second analyte in the [[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study]]. Reached: no. The budget and Elias's bench time moved to the methylation panel.

## Current State

- Superseded since 2025-06-30. No actions expected.
- 24 plasma samples were processed (12 pre-surgery patients, 12 controls), 4 ml each.
- Median RNA yield was 2.1 ng per sample; 9 of 24 libraries failed complexity thresholds. A tumour-associated transcript signal was detectable in 5 of 12 patients, all with high cfDNA tumour fraction already ([[exosome-rna-yield-results]]).
- The original plan is kept, archived, as [[exosome-rna-pilot-plan]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Bench feasibility and a first look at signal, on samples already in the freezer. No new sample collection was requested. Methods for vesicle isolation were limited to the two kits the wet lab already had.

## Working Context

[[dmitri-halvorsen]] proposed the pilot in January 2025 on the argument that RNA could report tumour state when cfDNA tumour fraction is low. The results showed the opposite: signal appeared only where cfDNA already detected the tumour, so the RNA added cost without adding sensitivity. [[elias-moreau]] did the bench work; the decision meeting is recorded in [[exosome-pilot-stop-2025-06-19]].

The methylation panel had started on 2025-01-20 in parallel and was showing better yield per ml plasma; that comparison made the decision easy. See [[lessons-from-the-2025-pilots]] for what we took away about running pilots in parallel.
