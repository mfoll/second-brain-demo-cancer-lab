# Northvale Case-Cohort Option

> [!warning]
> Rejected at the kick-off on 2025-02-10 in favour of a nested case-control design. Kept as a record of the option and the reasons.

## The option

Assay a random subcohort of about 1,500 participants plus all eligible pancreatic and colorectal cases. The subcohort would have served as the comparison group for both cancers and for any future outcome.

## Why it was rejected

- Cost: about 1,900 assays against 1,230, at roughly 237 EUR each ([[northvale-assay-budget]]).
- Storage time: a random subcohort is not matched on blood-draw date, so storage effects would need modelling ([[pre-analytical-storage-effects]]).
- Only two outcomes were planned.

## Afterlife

The [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]] later used a random subcohort, for the opposite reasons: all solid cancers as outcomes and a cheaper assay. Superseded by [[nested-case-control-design]] as the design of record for Northvale.
