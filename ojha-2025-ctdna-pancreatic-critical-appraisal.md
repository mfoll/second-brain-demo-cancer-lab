# ctDNA in Pancreatic Adenocarcinoma: A Critical Appraisal

Ojha S et al., Current Oncology, 2025.

## Summary

Critical review of ctDNA in pancreatic adenocarcinoma: prognostic and predictive value in resectable and metastatic disease, serial measurements improving risk stratification and recurrence prediction beyond CA19-9, and the limits: low assay sensitivity in early disease, pancreatic-specific shedding patterns, patient heterogeneity and technical variability. Concludes that context-specific validation is still needed.

## Why It Matters

Cited in the [[mrd-manuscript|MRD Manuscript]] discussion and the main pancreatic reference for the [[mrd-review-article|review]]. Its point on validation within specific clinical contexts is the same one Reviewer 2 later made to us. Also supports reporting CA19-9 alongside cfDNA ([[should-we-report-ca19-9-alongside-cfdna]]).

## Caveats

Narrative review; quality of underlying studies varies.
