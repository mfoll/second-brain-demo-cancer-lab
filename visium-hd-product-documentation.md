# Visium HD Spatial Gene Expression Product Page

The vendor's product page and linked technical notes for Visium HD, read when planning the pilot.

## Why It Matters

Defined what we could expect before running anything: a continuous 2 µm capture grid, FFPE compatibility through CytAssist probe transfer, default outputs at 2, 8 and 16 µm. [[ben-whitfield]], the vendor's field application scientist, walked us through the FFPE section requirements on 2025-03-12.

## Caveats

Vendor material; performance figures are from vendor tissues, not treated liver metastases. Our own numbers are in [[visium-hd-run-qc-metrics]].
