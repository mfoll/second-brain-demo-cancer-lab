# Grace Tanaka

Communications office of the institute. She writes the institute news, handles press contacts and edits the lay summaries the institute publishes for cohort participants.

## Role

Grace wrote the institute news item and the participant newsletter piece on the [[registry-linkage-agreement]] after the signature on 2025-10-31, checked by Ines and [[mei-lin-zhou]] for accuracy. She also supports the [[tumour-evolution-symposium-2026]] programme page and press.

## How We Work Together

Give her the facts and the caveats early; she writes quickly and asks good questions. She routes any journalist request through the scientist concerned, for instance [[freya-holm]]'s questions on early detection in 2026.

## Current Threads

- A lay explanation of the consortium transfer clause for the institute website, to publish only after the board adopts the [[data-access-policy-revision]]. Draft text held until then.
- Symposium press release, planned for early November.

## History With The Group

The registry linkage news item in November 2025 was the first time the group's cohort work appeared on the institute site. Grace insisted on a sentence explaining that the registry, not the institute, holds identities, which reduced participant questions to two emails. She has since helped with the participant newsletter piece on the Northvale early detection study, which [[mei-lin-zhou]] approves before publication.

## Notes For Working With Her

She needs to know what must not be said as much as what should. For the policy revision, the line agreed with her is: the institute is changing how it shares pseudonymised research data with trusted research partners, under a single agreement with strict controls, and nothing changes for participants' consent. Nothing about the MRD manuscript or Halo results goes out before publication. Ask her before talking to any journalist about the registry or the biobank, since institute press lines exist for both.
