# Liquid biopsies reveal dual compartments of cancer risk from tumor and host-derived mutations.

## Summary

A large tumour-type-resolved analysis of more than 9,000 patients with CHIP data and 1,500 with ctDNA data across solid tumours. CHIP mutations, especially in DNA damage response and epigenetic genes, and ctDNA driver mutations carried separate, non-overlapping prognostic signals; the authors argue that CHIP should not be treated only as background noise.

## Why It Matters

A published version of the point the group makes about its own calls: the same blood-derived variants are noise for MRD and information for risk ([[clonal-hematopoiesis]], [[ch-calls-data-flow]]). Ines plans to cite it in the [[mrd-review-article]] as well. For the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]] it supports reporting DNA damage response genes as a separate category.

## Caveats

Patients with cancer and outcomes after diagnosis; not incident risk in healthy people.
