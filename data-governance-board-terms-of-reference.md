# Data Governance Board Terms Of Reference

Terms of reference of the institute data governance board, version of 2024.

## Key Details

- Membership: chair, data protection officer, biobank manager, head of research IT, four group leaders appointed by the director for three years, data stewardship representative. Ethics committee secretary as observer.
- Remit: data access decisions for cohort and linked data, institute data policies, DPIAs for new processing, data agreements with external holders.
- Meetings: about every two months; papers ten working days before; quorum five members including the chair or deputy and the DPO.
- Conflicts: members declare interests at the start of each item; a member with a direct interest may present but does not vote.
- Decisions: by consensus, otherwise simple majority of voting members.

## Pending Change

[[ruth-abernathy]] proposes adding a standing [[data-access-committee]] function (chair, DPO, one rotating group leader) for per-use consortium requests, as required by [[data-access-policy-draft-3]]. To be adopted together with the policy.
