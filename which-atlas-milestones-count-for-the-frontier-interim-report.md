# Which Atlas Milestones Count For The Frontier Interim Report?

Which of the five milestones in the [[frontier-starting-grant-proposal-2025]] work plan can be reported as met by 2027-03-31, the end of the first reporting period?

## Current View

- M1, pilot resolution decision: met in August 2025, before the grant started. Unclear whether pre-grant work can be reported.
- M2, deconvolution benchmark: likely met if the preprint is posted by March 2027. Depends on whether a preprint counts as a publication milestone; to ask [[thabo-nkosi]].
- M3, 30 of 40 metastases profiled by month 15: will not be met; 24 profiled by October 2026. Report as a deviation, explaining the GPU partition delay on [[halyard-cluster]].
- M4 and M5 fall in later periods.

## Why It Matters

The [[frontier-reporting-guidelines]] say an unexplained missed milestone can trigger a review meeting. Reporting M3 honestly with a recovery plan is the safer route.
