# Leila Farouk

Visiting PhD student from Thornvale University, one of the Halo sites, at Corvane from 2026-04-13 to 2026-12-18. Her supervisor is [[fatima-benali]]. She works with [[sofia-reyes-calderon]] on the [[fragmentomics-classifier]] and is the practical bridge between the two fragmentomics assays in Halo.

## Role

- Third-year PhD student; her thesis is on nucleosome-footprint features in low-pass WGS.
- At Corvane: porting our end-motif feature code to Thornvale's size-selected assay and testing whether the classifier's fragment-length block transfers between the two assays.
- Funded by a Halo mobility grant from the WP1 budget; desk in the computational office next to Sofia.

## How We Work Together

Ines is her local supervisor; [[dmitri-halvorsen]] gives day-to-day wet-lab context, and [[elias-moreau]] showed her our library preparation in her first week. She and Sofia pair-program twice a week and present jointly at lab meeting. Her arrival came a month after the [[lab-meeting-2026-03-12]] decision on the classifier, so she has worked on the stacked logistic and boosting version from the start.

Data boundaries are explicit: Leila analyses Corvane data on [[halyard-cluster]] under our approvals, and Thornvale data stay at Thornvale; she runs code there remotely through her home account. This small two-site set-up is the first real test of the federated idea, and her notes fed the [[federated-cfdna-learning-concept-note]].

## Current Threads

- End-motif features on Thornvale data: first comparison shows the same top motifs but lower effect sizes after size selection.
- A short methods section for the consortium paper, with Sofia.
- Talk at the institute PhD seminar on 2026-11-26.
- Fortnightly call with [[arjun-mehta]] on the cross-assay feature layer.

## Practical Arrangements

Her Corvane account and Halyard access run until 2026-12-31 and were set up by [[priya-raman]] and [[tobias-wren]] in her first week. Thornvale remains her employer; Corvane covers no salary.
