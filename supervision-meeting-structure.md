# Supervision Meeting Structure

How Ines runs supervision with the group's PhD students. Written down in 2026 so that co-supervisors and new students know what to expect.

## Weekly One-to-One

- 30 minutes, same slot each week, with [[sofia-reyes-calderon]], [[kenji-mori]] and [[hannah-lindqvist]].
- The student sends three lines the evening before: what was done, what is blocking, what they want from the meeting.
- The first five minutes are not about results. Ines adopted this from [[leah-ostrander]]'s lab after reading on how isolation and supervisor relationships drive PhD burnout ([[dumic-2026-phd-student-psychological-outcomes]]).
- During grant crunches the slot moves, it is not cancelled.

## Monthly Student Round

Students present to each other without Ines for the first half hour, then she joins. Kenji suggested this in 2025; it gives them a place to ask the questions they would not ask the PI.

## Committee Preparation

Two weeks before each annual committee the student sends a written report on the [[thesis-committee-report-template]], and Ines returns comments within four working days. See [[sofia-thesis-committee]].

## What Is Not Recorded

Personal circumstances, health and wellbeing discussions stay out of the vault.
