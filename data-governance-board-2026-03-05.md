# Data Governance Board 2026-03-05

Board meeting at which the three-year review of [[corvane-data-access-policy-2023]] came up, one month late.

## Discussion

[[ruth-abernathy]] opened the review with a list of friction points. The longest discussion was about consortia: seven external requests in 2025 came from consortia and each became a bilateral agreement ([[data-request-statistics-2025]] gave the final number later). Ines described the Halo situation: the [[halo-cross-site-benchmark]] had started in January and Corvane was the only site unable to contribute data to the pooled environment.

## Decisions

- Open a revision of the policy, limited to consortium sharing, definitions and the data steward thresholds. A full rewrite was rejected as too slow.
- Ines to lead drafting with [[yara-castellanos]], with [[victor-lindahl]] advising. Ines declared her group's interest; Ruth recorded it and ruled that Ines drafts but does not vote on the item.
- Target adoption: September 2026 board. (This slipped; see [[data-governance-board-2026-09-10]].)

## Follow-Up

The [[data-access-policy-revision]] project opened on 2026-03-16.
