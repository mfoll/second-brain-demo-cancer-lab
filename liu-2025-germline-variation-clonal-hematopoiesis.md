# Germline genetic variation impacts clonal hematopoiesis landscape and progression to malignancy.

Liu J et al., Nature Genetics, 2025.

## Summary

Studied germline effects on clonal hematopoiesis in over 730,000 people and found 22 new CH predisposition genes, most acting on CH driven by specific mutational events. Predisposition genes shape which somatic drivers expand, and somatic-germline interactions affect progression to haematological malignancy. The authors suggest the principle likely extends to other tissues.

## Why It Matters

Two reasons. First, it is the obvious source of instruments for a CH MR, and [[ch-instrument-shortlist]] starts from it. Second, it is the strongest argument against a naive MR: if the same germline variants shape clonal expansions in other tissues, they may raise solid cancer risk without going through blood CH at all. That is exactly the [[pleiotropy]] problem [[hiroshi-tanabe]] warned about. [[hannah-lindqvist]] also read it for the [[clonal-hematopoiesis-filter]], since driver-specific predisposition may change which variants appear in cfDNA.

## Caveats

Driver-specific predisposition means "CH" is not one exposure. Any MR would need gene-specific instruments, which are fewer and weaker.
