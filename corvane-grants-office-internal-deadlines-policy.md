# Corvane Grants Office Internal Deadlines Policy

## Summary

The institute rule for outgoing applications, version effective 2026-01-01: the grants office must receive a complete package ten working days before the funder deadline, and every application needs director sign-off.

## Key Details

- Notification of intent at least six weeks before the deadline (the group uses eight).
- Budget built from the office template; PIs do not compute overhead.
- Director sign-off within five working days of a complete package.
- Late packages are submitted only with the director's written exception.

## Why It Matters

Basis of [[internal-grant-review-and-sign-off]] and of the 2026-11-20 internal date for the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]].
