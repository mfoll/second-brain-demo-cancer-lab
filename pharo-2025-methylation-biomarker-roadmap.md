# From concept to clinic: a roadmap for DNA methylation biomarkers in liquid biopsies

Pharo H et al., Oncogene, 2025.

## Summary

Review of why few DNA methylation liquid biopsy tests reach clinical use, covering choice of liquid biopsy source, biomarker discovery workflows and targeted validation in clinical series, with strategies for better accuracy, reproducibility and clinical utility.

## Why It Matters

Collected for the [[mrd-review-article|MRD Review Article]] in 2026-08. Its sequence of discovery, targeted assay and staged validation matches what the [[methylation-panel-design|Methylation Panel Design]] did, so it is a good external frame for section 1 of the review and a useful check on what our panel did not do (independent clinical validation, still pending through Halo).

## Caveats

Review; no data. General to all cancers.
