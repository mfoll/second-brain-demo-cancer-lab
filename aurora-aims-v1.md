# Aurora Aims v1

First aims page for the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]], written after the [[aurora-kickoff-2026-06-10]] and dated 2026-06-30. Superseded by [[aurora-aims-draft-v2]] and then by the two-aim [[aurora-aims-draft-v3]]; kept for the record in [[aurora-aims-history]].

## Working Title

From first signal to relapse: cfDNA and plasma proteins across the pancreatic cancer timeline.

## Aims

1. Time the appearance of cfDNA methylation signals before diagnosis of pancreatic and colorectal cancer in the Northvale nested case-control study (410 cases, 820 controls), and estimate lead time by window.
2. Detect recurrence after pancreatic cancer surgery earlier than imaging, combining fragmentomics and methylation in the 212-patient St Aldric cohort and a prospective extension of 150 patients.
3. Measure about 3,000 plasma proteins in the same Northvale and St Aldric samples and test whether a protein score adds to cfDNA at both ends of the timeline.

## Why It Was Retired

Aim 3 depended on the samples of aims 1 and 2 and competed with aim 1 for small Northvale aliquots. See [[aurora-mock-panel-2026-09-29]].
