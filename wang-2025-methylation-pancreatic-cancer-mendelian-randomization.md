# Exploring the association between DNA methylation and pancreatic cancer susceptibility through epigenome-wide Mendelian randomization and multi-omics data integration.

Wang P et al., Epigenetics, 2025.

## Summary

Epigenome-wide MR of genetically predicted blood CpG methylation and pancreatic cancer, with sensitivity analyses, Bayesian colocalisation, external validation and meta-analysis, followed by enrichment, PheWAS overlap, drug-target and mediation analyses.

## Why It Matters

[[maja-kovac]] flagged it in April while drafting the [[lead-time-analysis]] discussion. Our Northvale methylation signals are measured in cfDNA years before diagnosis and are read as tumour-derived. A germline-driven blood methylation effect on susceptibility is a different thing, and a reviewer could confuse the two. The paper gives Maja vocabulary for that paragraph; it does not bear on our results.

## Caveats

Blood CpG instruments reflect leukocyte methylation, not cfDNA from tumour. Many CpG-level tests; we have not checked how robust individual hits are.
