# Registry Extract Received 2026-09-08

The [[northern-cancer-registry]] delivered the 2025 extract for the Northvale cohort through secure file exchange to governed storage on [[halyard-cluster]]. First delivery under the institute [[registry-linkage-agreement]]; [[daniel-asante]] sent the accompanying linkage report the same day.

## What arrived

- Incidence and deaths to 2025-12-31 for all 86,400 participants, with causes of death as requested in [[registry-linkage-variable-specification]].
- Linkage report: 99.2% deterministic, 412 probabilistic, 37 unlinked.
- A list of revisions to records already delivered in the 2023 extract.

## Immediate checks

[[yara-castellanos]] logged receipt and checksums. [[maja-kovac]] ran the nested-set check within a week: three controls diagnosed before their index date, four case dates corrected ([[northvale-control-replacement-log]]). [[lucas-brandt]] extended the CH subcohort follow-up.

## Why it matters

Eight weeks from request to delivery, against the six months the 2023 linkage took under the cohort-specific route. The institute agreement paid off on its first use, which Ines reported to [[serve-corvane-institute]] colleagues at the [[data-governance-board-2026-09-10]].
