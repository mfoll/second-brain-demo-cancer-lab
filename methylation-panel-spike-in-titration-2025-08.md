# Methylation Panel Spike-In Titration 2025-08

Analytical sensitivity experiment for the methylation panel, wet lab by [[elias-moreau|Elias Moreau]], analysis by Dmitri. Report finished 2025-08-22.

## Design

Fragmented DNA from two pancreatic cancer cell lines spiked into pooled healthy-donor cfDNA at 1%, 0.5%, 0.2%, 0.1%, 0.05% and 0.02% tumour fraction, at 10 ng and 30 ng input, four replicates each, plus 12 unspiked blanks.

## Results

- 30 ng input: 95% detection down to 0.1% tumour fraction; 50% at 0.05%.
- 10 ng input: 95% detection only from 0.5%; 0.2% detected in 3 of 4 replicates.
- Blanks: 0 of 12 called positive at the scoring threshold.

## Caveats

Cell-line DNA was enzymatically fragmented and does not have the length profile of real ctDNA, so these figures are likely optimistic ([[limit-of-detection-in-cfdna-assays]]). The 10 ng result matters more in practice: a third of St Aldric post-operative extracts are below 10 ng.
