# Elias Moreau

Research technician running the cfDNA wet lab since 2024-02. Elias extracts, quantifies and prepares libraries for all St Aldric plasma samples and keeps the extraction QC logs.

## Role

- Plasma handling, cfDNA extraction and fragment analysis for the [[st-aldric-mrd-cohort|St Aldric MRD cohort]]: 1,468 time points extracted between 2025-03 and 2025-11.
- Library preparation for shallow WGS and the methylation panel; hands libraries to the sequencing core with run sheets ([[sequencing-run-sheet-sa-r04]] and later runs).
- Owner of [[cfdna-extraction-qc]] and the logs [[cfdna-extraction-qc-log-2025]] and [[cfdna-extraction-qc-log-2026]].
- Ran the wet-lab part of the methylation panel validation and spike-in titration ([[methylation-panel-spike-in-titration-2025-08]]).

## How We Work Together

Elias reports to [[dmitri-halvorsen|Dmitri Halvorsen]] and to [[priya-raman|Priya Raman]] for lab operations. He logged the extraction kit lot change on 2025-06-16 exactly as the SOP asked ([[extraction-kit-lot-change-2025-06-16]]); that log entry is what let Sofia trace the batch effect nine months later, and the group said so at the [[lab-meeting-2026-03-12|March lab meeting]]. Since then he records lot numbers in the sample sheet itself, not only in the lab notebook.

He prefers written protocols with explicit acceptance criteria and has asked to be included in analysis meetings when wet-lab variables are discussed; he now attends the monthly MRD study meeting.

He holds the lab's training records for plasma handling and is the second safety officer for the wet lab after Priya.
