# Registry Linkage Agreement Signed 2025-10-31

[[margarethe-sell]] and the director of the [[northern-cancer-registry]] signed the institute-level linkage agreement (contract NCR-2025-017) on 2025-10-31, closing the [[registry-linkage-agreement]] project after six and a half months.

## What It Enables

> [!success]
> Outcome follow-up for both the Northvale Health Cohort and the St Aldric MRD patients now runs under one agreement, with refreshes on request instead of a new application per study.

- The [[registry-linkage-update]] for the [[northvale-early-detection-study]] (follow-up 2023 to 2025), requested 2026-06-15.
- Death and second-primary ascertainment for the [[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study]], used for the survival analyses in the [[mrd-manuscript]].
- Incident cancer outcomes for the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]].

## Around The Signature

- [[grace-tanaka]] published an institute news item and a participant newsletter piece in November, reviewed by Ines and [[mei-lin-zhou]].
- [[daniel-asante]] and Ines agreed an informal working rhythm: a call before each refresh request to agree the variable list and the cut-off date.
- The terms are summarised in [[registry-linkage-agreement-summary]].

## In Hindsight

Clause 9.2 (no onward transfer of registry-derived variables without registry consent) was accepted as written. In 2026 it became one of the open points of the [[data-access-policy-revision]].
