# Protocol for a nested case-control study design for omics investigations in the Environmental Determinants of Islet Autoimmunity cohort.

## Summary

A published protocol for a nested case-control study (54 cases, 161 matched controls) using longitudinal biospecimens from a pregnancy-birth cohort on islet autoimmunity. It sets out case definition, matching, sample selection across time points and the omics analyses planned.

## Why It Matters

Maja used it as a template for how much detail a sample-selection protocol should carry before samples leave the biobank: explicit fallback rules when a match cannot be found, and sample-availability checks before matching. Both appear in [[northvale-matching-specification-v2]].

## Caveats

Paediatric cohort with repeated samples; our cohort has one baseline draw.
