# Frontier Starting Grant Proposal 2025

## Summary

The submitted proposal for the [[frontier-starting-grant]], "Mapping how colorectal liver metastases remodel under chemotherapy", submitted on 2025-03-18. Fifteen pages plus CV and budget.

## Key Details

- Two work packages: WP1 spatial profiling of 40 colorectal liver metastases, 28 with paired pre- and post-chemotherapy resections; WP2 computational methods for cell-type deconvolution and clone mapping at spot level.
- Five milestones: M1 pilot resolution decision (month 0), M2 deconvolution benchmark (month 15), M3 30 metastases profiled (month 15), M4 full atlas (month 36), M5 public release (month 48).
- Budget 1.48 M EUR: two postdocs, half a PhD studentship, Visium HD runs, a microscope upgrade later moved to consumables.

## Why It Matters

The milestone list is the yardstick for the [[frontier-interim-report]] ([[which-atlas-milestones-count-for-the-frontier-interim-report]]).
