# Exosome RNA Pilot Plan

Archived. The January 2025 plan by [[dmitri-halvorsen]] for the [[exosome-rna-pilot]]. Superseded by the targeted methylation panel ([[methylation-panel-design]]) after the [[exosome-pilot-stop-2025-06-19]].

## The Plan as Written

- Aim: test whether RNA from plasma extracellular vesicles reports tumour presence when cfDNA tumour fraction is low.
- Samples: 12 pre-surgery pancreatic cancer patients and 12 controls from banked plasma, 4 ml each.
- Isolation with two commercial kits, small RNA and long RNA libraries, sequencing on the core's mid-output runs.
- Analysis: tumour-associated transcripts from a published list, compared with cfDNA tumour fraction.
- Timeline: four months.

## What the Plan Lacked

No stop rule. It did not say what result would justify continuing, so the first discussion of results was about yield. This is the example behind [[pilots-need-a-written-stop-rule]].
