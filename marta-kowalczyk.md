# Marta Kowalczyk

Group leader of the proteomics group at Corvane, two corridors down. Co-applicant on the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]].

## Role

Marta's group runs plasma and tissue proteomics with a mass spectrometry platform and an affinity-based panel of about 3,000 proteins. Her interests overlap with ours on early detection: whether circulating proteins add to cfDNA signals before and after diagnosis.

## How We Work Together

- Weekly Aurora writing session with Ines on Tuesday mornings since June 2026.
- She is direct and data-led; she would rather cut an aim than defend one she cannot power. At the [[aurora-mock-panel-2026-09-29]] she was the first to propose folding the proteomics aim into aim 2.
- Her group's pilot data on St Aldric plasma are summarised in [[kowalczyk-plasma-proteomics-pilot]]. She shares figures as slide decks, not raw tables, until a dataset is QC-locked.
- Sample requests for her work go through the same biobank rules as ours ([[corvane-biobank]]). She agreed early that Northvale aliquots stay reserved for cfDNA.

## Current Threads

- Aurora aim 2, work package 2.3: proteomics on post-surgery St Aldric plasma, alongside fragmentomics and methylation.
- Budget share: provisionally 380 k EUR including a 0.5 FTE postdoc and instrument time as a service line; still open in [[how-much-of-the-aurora-budget-goes-to-proteomics]].
- She would like a joint PhD student from 2028 if Aurora is funded.

## History

- First joint meeting at the institute retreat in 2025; she offered to run 40 pilot plasma samples at cost.
- Co-led the [[aurora-kickoff-2026-06-10]].

## Working Style Notes

Marta plans in quarters and likes decisions written down the same day; after the mock panel she sent a half-page summary of what her group would and would not do in aim 2 before Ines had finished her own notes. She is cautious about promising instrument time a year ahead because the platform is shared with three other groups.
