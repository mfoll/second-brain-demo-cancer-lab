# Reference Materials Cannot Replace Patient Plasma

## Stance

For cfDNA assays that use more than the presence of a mutation, and fragmentomics in particular, no contrived reference material available today can stand in for patient plasma in a benchmark. Contrived material is good for analytical limits of mutation assays; anything claimed about clinical performance, or about fragment features, needs real plasma with a known outcome.

## Scope

Applies to benchmarks and validations, including Halo and our own [[low-vaf-caller-benchmark]]. Does not apply to routine run quality control, where a stable, non-commutable material is still useful to catch drift.

## Why I Think This

- The pilot panel and the interim report showed fragmentomics scores unrelated to nominal VAF on sheared DNA ([[halo-pilot-panel-results-2026-04]], [[halo-interim-benchmark-report-2026-10]]).
- Even well-made nucleosomal materials are characterised for mutation detection only ([[hong-2026-nucleosomal-egfr-reference-materials]]).
- Clonal haematopoiesis, a main false-positive source in MRD, is absent from cell-line blanks ([[clonal-hematopoiesis]]).
- I argued the opposite in the draft truth-set plan in 2025, which used contrived material only ([[halo-truth-set-draft-plan-2025]]). [[sun-hee-park]] was right to reject it.

## What Would Change My Mind

A material shown to be commutable for fragment-length and end-motif features across both Halo fragment assays, for example plasma-derived material pooled and characterised at scale.

## Implications

Budget patient plasma with follow-up into every future benchmark, even if it shrinks the contrived tier. Report non-evaluable cells openly, in line with [[benchmarks-must-report-failure-modes]].
