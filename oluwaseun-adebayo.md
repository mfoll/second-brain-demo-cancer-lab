# Oluwaseun Adebayo

Group leader in cancer evolution at Westmere Cancer Centre and co-organiser of the [[tumour-evolution-symposium-2026|Tumour Evolution Symposium 2026]]. External contact, so no affiliation hub in this vault.

## Role

Oluwaseun works on metastatic seeding and treatment resistance in colorectal and gastric cancer using multi-region sequencing. He and Ines first co-chaired a session together at a meeting in 2024 and proposed a joint symposium then; he brought the idea back in January 2026, and the Project started on 2026-02-02.

## How We Work Together

- Fortnightly 30-minute call, usually Wednesday at 08:30, minutes by whoever hosts. The calls that shaped the programme are [[symposium-planning-call-2026-02-18]], [[symposium-planning-call-2026-07-08]] and [[symposium-planning-call-2026-10-07]].
- Division of labour agreed on 2026-02-18: Oluwaseun leads speaker invitations for the metastasis and treatment sessions and the abstract scoring rubric; Ines leads the pre-diagnostic and MRD sessions, venue and budget through [[priya-raman]].
- He prefers decisions in writing after each call; Ines sends a three-line summary the same day.
- He argued for a hybrid format at first and accepted in-person with a streamed keynote ([[should-the-symposium-be-hybrid]]). He was right that a few speakers would struggle with travel funding; see the Project's open risk.
