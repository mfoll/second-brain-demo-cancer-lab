# Matched Normal Sequencing Is Cheaper Than a False Recurrence

## Stance

For MRD studies that could inform patient care, matched buffy coat sequencing at about 180 EUR per sample is worth paying for. A false molecular recurrence call costs more in patient harm, extra imaging and lost credibility than the sequencing.

## Scope

Studies where an MRD result could reach a patient or a clinician, and any publication claiming clinical specificity. For pure methods development a panel of normals may be enough.

## Why I Think This

- In our pilot, the panel of normals alone would still have left 2 of 60 patients with a false recurrence call ([[ch-buffy-coat-pilot-results-2026-01]]).
- Patients told us false positives worry them more than late detection ([[patient-advisory-meeting-2025-10-22]]).
- A case report of repeated false-positive ctDNA surveillance from CH shows the clinical cost concretely ([[spoor-2021-false-positive-ctdna-ch]]).
- Dmitri argued this at the [[ch-filter-design-meeting-2025-11-05|2025-11-05 design meeting]]; I chose a pilot first, which I still think was right for evidence, but the pilot results moved me towards his view.

## What Would Change My Mind

A plasma-only method (fragment features, larger panel of normals, or both) that brings false recurrence calls to zero in the 60-patient pilot. Hannah is testing this ([[do-we-still-need-matched-buffy-coat-sequencing]]).

## Implications

Budget buffy coat sequencing in the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027|Aurora programme grant]] for any prospective MRD cohort.
