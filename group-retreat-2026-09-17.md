# Group Retreat 2026-09-17

The group's two-day retreat at Hollin House, 17 and 18 September 2026. Fifteen attended; agenda in [[group-retreat-2026-agenda]].

## At a Glance

> [!abstract]
> Day 1 produced four 2027 priorities, one per umbrella line plus the Aurora submission. Day 2 adopted mandatory review of pipeline changes and a split lab meeting format, and set a target of two maintainers per pipeline by March 2027.

## Day 1: Science

- **Pancreatic MRD.** [[dmitri-halvorsen]] framed 2027 around the [[mrd-manuscript]] revision. External validation with Halo samples is the critical path and depends on the [[data-access-policy-revision]]. Fallback discussed: a temporal validation within St Aldric, which Reviewer 2 may not accept.
- **Northvale and CH.** [[maja-kovac]] committed to submitting the lead-time paper in 2027. [[lucas-brandt]] showed the CH calls on the full 6,000 subcohort; the same calls feed [[hannah-lindqvist]]'s filter, and both agreed to share one call set version.
- **Atlas.** [[amara-okonkwo]] and [[kenji-mori]]: 26 of 40 pairs sequenced, batch 3 waiting for a sequencing slot in November; clone tracking waits for the new postdoc. GPU access was still the blocker; it was granted four days after the retreat, on 22 September.
- **Aurora.** Long discussion of three aims; no decision, left to the mock panel on 29 September.

## Day 2: How We Work

- **Lab meetings.** Students ran the first 30 minutes without Ines. Proposal adopted: 15 minutes of operations with written decisions, then one science slot; operations items not decided go to a written follow-up within a week ([[lab-meeting-format]], [[should-lab-meetings-be-split-into-science-and-operations]]).
- **Code review.** Tobias presented the [[retreat-2026-pre-survey]] result that only 3 of 13 felt able to fix a failing run. Adopted: one reviewer other than the author for every pipeline change, with a rota ([[code-review-guidelines]]). Extending this to analysis code is still open ([[should-we-require-code-review-for-all-pipeline-changes]]).
- **Compute.** Storage charging from April 2027 means raw data retention on Halyard needs a rule ([[how-long-should-we-keep-raw-fastq-on-halyard]]).
- **Credit.** [[authorship-and-credit-guidelines]] updated: authorship conversation at project start, recorded in the project note.

## Outcomes

- [[research-plan-2027]] drafted and circulated on 2026-09-25.
- Second maintainers named for each pipeline: [[sofia-reyes-calderon]] for cfDNA, Kenji for spatial.
