# Dmitri Halvorsen

Staff scientist and cfDNA lead of the group since 2023. Dmitri runs the [[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study|Pancreatic cfDNA MRD Study]] day to day, owns the cfDNA analysis pipelines scientifically, and co-supervises [[sofia-reyes-calderon|Sofia Reyes Calderon]] and [[hannah-lindqvist|Hannah Lindqvist]].

## Role

- Scientific lead for cfDNA: assay choices, QC thresholds, variant calling and the MRD call definition.
- Joint first author of the [[mrd-manuscript|MRD Manuscript]] with Sofia; owns the Reviewer 1 and Reviewer 3 analyses in the revision.
- Led the [[methylation-panel-design|Methylation Panel Design]] and signed the QC off on 2025-09-15 ([[methylation-panel-qc-sign-off-2025-09-15]]).
- Started the [[low-vaf-caller-benchmark|Low-VAF Caller Benchmark]] and proposed pausing it until the Halo truth set could serve as physical truth; it is still waiting for the contrived-tier unblinding.
- Group contact for the Halo analysis work package on assay-level questions, with [[arjun-mehta|Arjun Mehta]] on the modelling side.

## How We Work Together

Weekly one-to-one with Ines on Tuesdays, plus the monthly MRD study meeting he chairs. Dmitri prefers decisions written down before analyses start: he wrote the first statistical analysis plan ([[mrd-statistical-analysis-plan-v2]]) and insisted that the [[fragmentomics-classifier-retraining]] procedure include a leave-batch-out step. He tends to argue for the more conservative claim, which was useful when the batch overfitting surfaced in March 2026. He was the one who suggested at the [[classifier-design-review-2026-04-02|2026-04-02 review]] that end-motif features might not share the problem, which led to the stacked design.

He works closely with [[elias-moreau|Elias Moreau]] on the wet lab and with [[rafael-ibarrondo|Rafael Ibarrondo]] at the sequencing core on run scheduling.

## Background

Dmitri joined the group in 2023 from a clinical sequencing laboratory, where he built error-corrected panels for haematological cancers. That background explains both his comfort with very low allele fractions and his insistence on matched normal sequencing.
