# Cancer Genomics Course 2026 Feedback Survey

## Summary

Anonymous end-of-course survey run by the graduate school, open 2026-05-22 to 2026-06-05. 19 of 24 students responded.

## Key Details

- Overall rating 4.3 out of 5 (2025: 3.9; 2024: 4.0).
- Best-rated session: [[zoe-carmichael]]'s guest lecture on reporting results to clinicians (4.8).
- Worst-rated element: computing access in the first weeks (2.9); eleven free-text comments on Kestrel logins and queue times.
- Spatial block "too many methods in too little time" (seven comments).
- Twelve students said the oral defence was the most useful part of the assessment; four found it stressful.
- Five students asked for a session on how real cohort data are accessed and governed.

## Caveats

Small numbers; one strongly negative respondent accounts for most of the lowest scores. Free-text comments are paraphrased here, not quoted.

## Source Location

Graduate school survey platform export, saved in the course repository's private `feedback/2026/` folder.
