# Kowalczyk Plasma Proteomics Pilot

## Summary

Eight-slide summary from [[marta-kowalczyk]]'s group of a pilot run on 40 St Aldric plasma samples (20 patients with relapse within 18 months of surgery, 20 without), using the affinity panel. Shared on 2026-07-15 as preliminary data for the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]].

## Key Details

- All 40 samples passed QC; median 2,870 proteins detected per sample.
- A handful of proteins differed between relapse and non-relapse groups at the first post-surgery draw; none survived correction for multiple testing at this size.
- Minimum plasma input 40 µL, which is why the St Aldric post-surgery tubes are a better fit than Northvale baseline aliquots.

## Caveats

Pilot only, not QC-locked. Marta asked that no figure be reused outside the application until her group has finished the batch analysis. Do not quote effect sizes.
