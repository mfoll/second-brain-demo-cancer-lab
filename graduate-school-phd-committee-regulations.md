# Graduate School PhD Committee Regulations

## Summary

The graduate school's rules for PhD thesis committees, version 2025.

## Key Details

- Each student has a committee of supervisor, one internal member from another group and one external examiner, appointed in the first six months.
- Committees meet once a year; the second-year committee decides whether the student continues to the third year.
- The student's written report reaches the committee at least two weeks before the meeting.
- An unsatisfactory outcome triggers a follow-up meeting within three months.
- Students must complete 60 hours of teaching or equivalent over the PhD.
