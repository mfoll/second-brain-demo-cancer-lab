# Comparison of spatial transcriptomics technologies using tumor cryosections.

Rademacher A et al., Genome Biology, 2025.

## Summary

Compares four imaging-based methods and standard Visium on cryosections of a nodular medulloblastoma chosen for its clear microanatomy. Imaging methods capture the microanatomy and cell-type profiles well; the authors propose ways to compare sensitivity and specificity across platforms, and show that reimaging slides after the assay improves segmentation and adds protein readouts.

## Why It Matters

Useful framework for our pilot evaluation: compare platforms against known anatomy rather than against each other. We used the same logic with [[benedikt-kraus]]'s annotations as the reference in the [[visium-hd-pilot]]. The reimaging idea is on the list for the atlas follow-up.

## Caveats

Cryosections and standard Visium, not FFPE and Visium HD; one tumour type.
