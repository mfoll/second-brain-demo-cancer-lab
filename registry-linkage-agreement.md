# Registry Linkage Agreement

Institute-level data linkage agreement between the Corvane Institute and the [[northern-cancer-registry]], covering participants of the Northvale Health Cohort and patients of the St Aldric MRD study. Ines led the negotiation on the institute side as the board member representing research users.

## Outcome

Signed agreement that lets the institute request cancer incidence and death follow-up for consented study participants, without a new application per study. Reached on 2025-10-31.

## Current State

- Completed. Signed by [[margarethe-sell]] and the registry director on 2025-10-31 ([[registry-linkage-agreement-signed-2025-10-31]]).
- The agreement runs to 2030-10-31 and allows up to two linkage refreshes a year. Its terms are summarised in [[registry-linkage-agreement-summary]].
- First use: death and second-primary ascertainment for the [[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study]], delivered 2026-01-22. Second use: the [[registry-linkage-update]] for the [[northvale-early-detection-study]], extending follow-up from 2023 to 2025, requested 2026-06-15.
- Onward transfer of registry-derived variables needs the registry's written consent. This clause now matters for the [[data-access-policy-revision]].

## Scope and Boundaries

The agreement covers linkage, not analysis: the registry returns pseudonymised extracts keyed by study ID, and each study remains responsible for its own ethics approval. It does not cover the Brightwater or Westmere cohorts in Halo.

## Working Context

Before the agreement, each study applied separately. The earlier Northvale follow-up (extract to 2023), under a cohort-specific approval, took eleven months from application to extract. The institute agreement was meant to bring that to under three months for a refresh; the initial St Aldric extract, requested on 2025-11-10, arrived on 2026-01-22, ten weeks later.

Negotiation points that took time: the identifier set (the registry wanted national ID plus date of birth; we settled on that plus sex and postcode sector for fallback matching), the retention period of the linkage key at the registry (deleted 90 days after delivery), and the onward transfer clause, which we accepted as written because nothing at the time needed it. With hindsight, that last point is the one we would negotiate differently.
