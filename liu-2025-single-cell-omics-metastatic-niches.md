# Decoding metastatic microenvironments through single-cell omics reveals new insights into niche dynamics and tumor evolution.

Liu F, Zhang XH. PLOS Biology, 2025.

## Summary

Review of what single-cell technologies have shown about metastatic niches: how primary tumours precondition distant organs, how disseminated cells interact with host cells, and the divergent and convergent adaptation strategies seen across cancer types. Frames metastasis as a dynamic, cooperative process between tumour and host.
