# MRD Study Kickoff 2025-01-14

Kickoff of the [[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study|Pancreatic cfDNA MRD Study]] at Corvane with [[samuel-okoye|Samuel Okoye]] and his research nurse joining by video. Present: Ines, [[dmitri-halvorsen|Dmitri Halvorsen]], [[elias-moreau|Elias Moreau]], [[priya-raman|Priya Raman]], [[yara-castellanos|Yara Castellanos]].

## Decisions

- Use the existing St Aldric post-resection collection rather than start a prospective study: 212 patients with serial plasma already banked ([[st-aldric-mrd-cohort]]).
- Three signal layers from the start: tumour-informed variants, a targeted methylation panel to be designed ([[methylation-panel-design]]), and shallow WGS for fragmentomics. The exosome RNA pilot continues in parallel until the panel is validated ([[exosome-rna-pilot]]).
- Primary endpoint: time from first positive cfDNA sample to radiological recurrence. Samuel insisted on blinded adjudication of recurrence.
- Ethics amendment needed to add Corvane as analysis site and allow registry linkage; Samuel to file it ([[st-aldric-ethics-amendment-2025-05]]).

## Open Points Recorded

- Whether the old plasma (some from 2022) is still fine for fragmentomics after storage. Dmitri to check fragment profiles on 20 samples before full extraction.
- Who pays for buffy coat sequencing. Deferred; it came back in November ([[ch-filter-design-meeting-2025-11-05]]).

## Follow-Up

Sample transfer of the first 600 aliquots on 2025-03-03; extraction started the same month with the SOP in [[cfdna-extraction-sop-v3]].
