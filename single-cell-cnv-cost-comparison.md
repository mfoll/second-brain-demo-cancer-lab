# Single-Cell CNV Cost Comparison

Felix's cost table comparing single-cell copy-number profiling with adding bulk regions, prepared for the [[single-cell-cnv-pilot-review-2025-07-24]].

| Item | Single-cell, per tumour | One extra bulk region |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sample preparation | 2,400 EUR | 250 EUR |
| Library and sequencing | 7,900 EUR | 1,450 EUR |
| Analyst time (days) | 6 | 1 |
| Total, approximate | 11,500 EUR | 1,900 EUR |

## Reading It

For the same money, six extra bulk regions per tumour, which would also separate most of the clones the single-cell data found. Single-cell becomes interesting below about 3,000 EUR per tumour.
