# Course Final Project Presentations 2026-05-22

Closing session of the [[cancer-genomics-course-2026]]: six groups of four presented their final projects, fifteen minutes each. Assessors: Ines, [[maja-kovac]], [[amara-okonkwo]].

## Projects

- Two groups reconstructed tumour phylogenies from public multi-region data.
- One group reanalysed a public cfDNA dataset for fragment-length differences.
- One group reproduced a published clonal deconvolution and found a sample label swap in the supplementary table; they wrote to the authors, who confirmed it.
- Two groups did case-control analyses on a simulated cohort prepared by Maja, one of which handled matching correctly.

## Assessment

23 of 24 students passed on the day. One student's individual report was incomplete and was resubmitted and passed in June. The rubric weighted the oral defence of methods at 40%, which caught two students who could not explain code they had submitted.

## Notes

Best project prize (book voucher) to the deconvolution group. Feedback survey opened the same evening ([[cancer-genomics-course-2026-feedback-survey]]).
