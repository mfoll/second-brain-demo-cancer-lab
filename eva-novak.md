# Eva Novak

Halo legal and ethics lead at the Brightwater coordinating centre. She leads Work Package 5 (ethics, legal and data protection), drafted the consortium agreement and the material transfer agreement, and is the counterpart of our data protection officer [[victor-lindahl]] on every transfer question.

## Role

- Author of the data-sharing clause in the consortium agreement ([[halo-consortium-agreement-summary]]), of the multi-party [[halo-data-sharing-agreement-template]] that implements it, and of the [[halo-material-transfer-agreement]].
- Keeps the ethics approvals of all six sites on file and checks that each use of truth-set data matches them.
- Advises the steering committee on intellectual property, including ownership of models trained on consortium data.

## How We Work Together

Formal in writing, informal on the phone. She prefers to talk to Victor directly rather than through Ines, which has saved time. In August she and Victor agreed that the physical aliquots were covered by the material transfer agreement and by our existing ethics approval, but that pseudonymised data with linked clinical outcomes were not covered by Corvane's current policy. That joint note is the clearest statement of the block ([[halo-data-flow-and-transfer-constraints]]).

She attended the [[data-governance-board-2026-09-10]] discussion as an external observer at Victor's invitation and answered questions on how the other five sites handled the clause.

## Current Threads

- What Corvane's revised policy must contain for the clause to work; she sent model wording to Victor in September.
- Whether federated evaluation counts as a data transfer ([[federated-cfdna-learning]]).
- Model ownership ([[who-owns-models-trained-on-halo-data]]).

## History With The Group

Eva drafted the article 9 wording in 2024, before Corvane's policy problem was known; she has said openly that she would have flagged it earlier had she checked each partner's internal rules at the time. Since then she has made a habit of checking site rules before any new data request.
