# Symposium Programme Draft v1

First programme draft for the [[tumour-evolution-symposium-2026]], circulated by Ines to [[oluwaseun-adebayo]] on 2026-02-20 after the [[symposium-planning-call-2026-02-18]]. Superseded on 2026-03-25 by v2 and now by [[symposium-programme-draft-v3]]; kept for the record of what changed.

## What It Proposed

- Dates 2026-11-12/13. Moved to 2026-11-19/20 in March to suit the keynote speaker and to avoid the institute's budget week.
- Two keynotes, one per day. Cut to one because the budget could not cover a second long-haul trip and because the call agreed more time should go to short talks.
- Five sessions, no separate spatial session; spatial work sat inside "Metastasis". Split out in v2 after Oluwaseun pointed out that half the expected abstracts would be spatial.
- 12 short talks, 40 posters. Raised to 16 and about 60 once the auditorium's poster space was measured.
