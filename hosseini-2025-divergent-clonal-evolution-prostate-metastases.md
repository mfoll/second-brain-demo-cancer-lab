# Divergent Clonal Evolution and Early Dissemination Promote Genetic Heterogeneity of Metastases in Castration-Resistant Prostate Cancer.

Hosseini N et al., Cancer Research, 2025.

## Summary

Deep exome sequencing of 93 tumours from 26 patients with lethal prostate cancer, with phylogenetic reconstruction and mathematical modelling. Early driver mutations were truncal, but most other mutations, copy-number changes and clones arose after spread and were confined to individual sites because of polyclonal and polyphyletic seeding. Single-site sequencing tended to overestimate clonality.
