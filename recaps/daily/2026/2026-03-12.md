# Thursday 12 March 2026

## Fragmentomics classifier and the extraction batches

Sofia's batch analysis was the main item of the [[lab-meeting-2026-03-12|lab meeting]]. The gradient-boosting [[fragmentomics-classifier|Fragmentomics Classifier]] looks excellent within batches and much worse when whole extraction batches are held out, because the [[extraction-kit-lot-change-2025-06-16|kit lot change]] lines up with pre-operative versus follow-up samples. The mechanism is written up in [[batch-confounding-in-cfdna-studies|Batch confounding in cfDNA studies]].

We decided to replace gradient boosting with regularised logistic regression, which loses far less under leave-batch-out folds and is easier to defend. Lucas proposed ComBat-style correction before boosting; we rejected it because batch and time point are confounded and correction could remove real signal. Leave-batch-out validation becomes the reported default for every cfDNA model, and the classifier stays unlocked.

This decision was partly reversed three weeks later: see the [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-04-02|recap of 2 April]].

## Manuscript consequence

The submitted [[mrd-manuscript|MRD Manuscript]] uses the boosted model. We will not withdraw; I will tell the editor and fix it in revision.
