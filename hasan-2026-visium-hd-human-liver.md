# High-resolution spatial transcriptomics of adult and pediatric human liver with Visium HD.

Hasan F et al., Scientific Data, 2026.

## Summary

A data paper releasing Visium HD profiles of three healthy human livers (two adult, one paediatric). Cell types were identified by clustering 8 µm bins and integrating with single-cell reference maps, and differential expression gives a spatial map of liver zonation and anatomy.

## Why It Matters

The first public Visium HD map of normal human liver we know of. For the atlas it gives a check on our adjacent-liver regions: zonation markers and hepatocyte bin counts should look similar away from the tumour. [[kenji-mori]] plans to use it as an external sanity check for the hepatocyte proportions that the low-count collapse inflates ([[low-count-spot-collapse]]). It also shows another group settled on 8 µm bins for cell typing.

## Caveats

Healthy donors only, three samples; no tumour, no chemotherapy exposure.
