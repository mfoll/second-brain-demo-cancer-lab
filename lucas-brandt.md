# Lucas Brandt

Postdoc in genetic epidemiology since September 2025. He leads the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]], the cohort study of clonal hematopoiesis as a risk factor for solid cancer in a 6,000-person Northvale subcohort.

## Role

Lucas came from a statistical genetics background and brought the cohort-analysis habits the group lacked: attained age as the time scale, cause-specific hazards, careful thinking about what a covariate is doing. He drew the [[ch-subcohort-sampling-frame]], wrote the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis-plan]] and owns the analysis code.

He works closely with [[hannah-lindqvist]], whose caller from [[clonal-hematopoiesis-filter]] produces the variant calls he analyses. The arrangement is deliberate: one caller, one set of frozen calls, two uses ([[ch-calls-data-flow]]). When he needs a different filter he asks Hannah to add an option rather than patching a copy.

## How we work together

- Fortnightly one-to-one with Ines; CH meetings with Hannah and [[maja-kovac]] as needed.
- He argues from data. At [[ch-analysis-meeting-2026-09-16]] he made the case for a 1% VAF threshold with a sensitivity plot rather than an opinion, and accepted 2% as primary when the group weighed comparability with published work ([[should-ch-be-defined-at-vaf-2-or-1-percent]]).
- He is interested in Mendelian randomization and reads in the [[mendelian-randomization]] cluster; Ines asked him to keep MR out of this paper.

## History with the group

Lucas interviewed in June 2025 after Ines and [[maja-kovac]] agreed the cohort needed a second epidemiologist for the CH question. His first month went on the [[ch-subcohort-data-access-approval]] and the [[ch-subcohort-sampling-frame]]; by December he had an agreed [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis-plan]]. He also reviewed the Northvale SAP and pushed for the unconditional-model sensitivity analysis ([[kuo-2018-conditional-vs-unconditional-logistic-regression]]).
