# Data Governance Board 2025-05-22

Regular board meeting chaired by [[ruth-abernathy]]. Two items concerned the group.

## Items

- **Group data management plan.** The board approved the [[lab-data-management-plan]] with one change: Northvale extracts held by the group move from Kestrel project storage into the institute secure data environment by the end of 2025. Ines declared the interest and did not vote.
- **Registry linkage application.** Ines presented the case for an institute-level agreement with the [[northern-cancer-registry]] instead of per-study applications. The board endorsed submission of the [[registry-linkage-application-2025]] and gave Ines and [[yara-castellanos]] a negotiating mandate, with [[victor-lindahl]] to run the DPIA in parallel.
- **Biobank last aliquots.** [[paolo-ferrante]] reported that 11% of Northvale participants are down to their last plasma aliquot. The board confirmed that last aliquots are released only by explicit board decision, case by case.

## Outcome

The DMP approval closed the [[lab-data-management-plan]] project on 2025-05-30. The linkage application went to the registry on 2025-05-26.
