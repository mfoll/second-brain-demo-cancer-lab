# Northvale SAP Sign-off 2026-02-26

The [[northvale-statistical-analysis-plan]] v1.0 was signed by [[maja-kovac]], Ines and [[mei-lin-zhou]] at a short video call, after which [[yara-castellanos]] released the case-control key for joining to the frozen [[northvale-methylation-data-release-v1]].

## Decisions

- Main-model exclusion left open, to be fixed after a blinded look at the time-to-diagnosis distribution. Mei-Lin asked for this; Maja would have preferred to fix 1 year in advance. In hindsight this left the decision to be made after seeing results, which the paper must state ([[northvale-sap-amendment-1]]).
- Region-level analyses are exploratory and cannot redefine the score.
- [[lucas-brandt]]'s suggestion of an unconditional model kept as a sensitivity analysis ([[kuo-2018-conditional-vs-unconditional-logistic-regression]]).
