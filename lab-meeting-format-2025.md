# Lab Meeting Format 2025

The lab meeting format used from 2023 until 2026-10-01. Archived; replaced by [[lab-meeting-format]] after the [[group-retreat-2026]].

## How It Ran

- Thursday 10:00, nominally 90 minutes, often 100 or more.
- A round of project updates, one per project lead, about 5 minutes each, then announcements, then one science talk if time remained.
- Notes taken by a rotating note-taker in a shared document, without a fixed structure.

## Why It Was Replaced

With ten active projects the update round alone took an hour. Science talks were regularly cut short, and decisions were scattered in the notes. Only 4 of 13 members agreed that meetings ended with clear decisions ([[retreat-2026-pre-survey]]).

## What Was Kept

The fortnightly rhythm, the rotating note-taker, and the Event note per meeting introduced in 2025 (for example [[lab-meeting-2025-01-16]]).
