# Northvale Baseline Questionnaire Codebook

## Summary

The cohort's own codebook for the baseline questionnaire, provided by the cohort team with each data release. Used by both the [[northvale-early-detection-study]] and the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]].

## Key Details

- Two questionnaire versions: V1 (2004 to mid-2005, about 18,000 participants) and V2 (thereafter). V1 asked cigarettes per day only for current smokers; V2 added years smoked and former-smoker intensity, which is why pack-years are incomplete.
- Alcohol in drinks per week; physical activity in a four-level index; self-reported diabetes and aspirin use.
- Height, weight and waist measured by staff at the visit.
- Special codes: -7 not asked (version), -8 refused, -9 missing.

## Caveats

The cohort codebook is authoritative; our derived variables are documented separately ([[northvale-smoking-history-derivation]]).
