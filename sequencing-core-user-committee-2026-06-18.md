# Sequencing Core User Committee 2026-06-18

Quarterly meeting chaired by [[rafael-ibarrondo]]. Approved minutes: [[sequencing-core-user-committee-minutes-2026-06-18]].

## What Ines Pushed For

- The low-input library surcharge. Rafael proposed +12%; Ines and two other liquid biopsy groups argued that nearly all cfDNA libraries from post-surgery plasma fall under 5 ng and that the surcharge was becoming a de facto price rise for one user community. Agreed at +7%.
- A clearer queue rule: forecast projects first. Accepted.

## Other Points

- Turnaround slipped to a median of 19 working days in the first half of 2026 because of a three-week instrument outage.
- Delivery to Halyard storage from September, coordinated with [[ilse-van-dorn]].

## Outcome

The [[sequencing-core-price-list-2026]] took effect on 2026-07-01. The buffy coat price (180 EUR) was not discussed in detail; Ines wants it on the October agenda.
