# Halo Site And Assay Overview

Who is where in Halo and which assay each site brings to the [[halo-cross-site-benchmark]]. Kept as a quick reference; detail on methods in [[halo-sop-comparison-library-prep]] and [[halo-sop-comparison-bioinformatics]].

| Site | People | Assay | Work package |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brightwater University Hospital (coordinator) | [[maximilian-auer]], [[ngozi-adeyemi]], [[eva-novak]] | Tumour-informed personalised panel with UMIs, 16 variants per patient | WP1, WP5 |
| Corvane Institute for Cancer Genomics | Ines, [[dmitri-halvorsen]], [[sofia-reyes-calderon]] | Shallow WGS, about 0.5x, fragment-length and end-motif features | WP4 |
| Hollinmoor University Medical Centre | [[tomasz-wilk]], [[liam-oconnell]] | Targeted methylation sequencing, about 2,600 regions | WP3 |
| Thornvale University | [[fatima-benali]], [[leila-farouk]] (visiting us) | Size-selected low-pass WGS, nucleosome footprints | WP4 task 4.2 |
| Kelstow Cancer Institute | [[sun-hee-park]] | ddPCR, eight hotspot targets | WP4 deputy |
| Westmere Cancer Centre | [[isabel-montes]], [[arjun-mehta]], [[henrik-dahl]] (chair) | Tumour-naive hybrid capture with UMIs | WP2, WP6 |

## Notes

- Two fragmentomics sites (Corvane, Thornvale) and two methylation-capable assays (Hollinmoor, and Corvane's shallow-WGS proxy, which is not designed for it).
- Tumour-informed assays need tissue; for patient-tier samples without tissue, Brightwater falls back to a fixed hotspot set.
- Westmere is a Halo site only; it does not contribute to the St Aldric MRD study ([[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study]]).
