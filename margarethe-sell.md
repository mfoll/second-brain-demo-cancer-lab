# Margarethe Sell

Director of the Corvane Institute for Cancer Genomics. She signs institute-level agreements, appoints board members and sets the institute's strategic priorities.

## Role

Margarethe signed the [[registry-linkage-agreement]] on behalf of the institute on 2025-10-31 and will sign the Halo consortium agreement once the [[data-access-policy-revision]] is adopted. She appointed Ines to the [[data-governance-board-membership|data governance board]] in 2024 for a three-year term ending 2027-03-31.

## How We Work Together

Short, prepared meetings. She wants a one-page brief with the decision she is asked to make, the risk if she does not, and who else has been consulted. She does not attend board meetings routinely but joins for items with institutional exposure, as she did for the consortium clause at [[data-governance-board-2026-09-10]].

She has been supportive of consortium work as long as the institute's name is not attached to a data incident. Her line on the policy revision: "fast, but not first to get it wrong".

## Current Threads

- Policy revision: she expects adoption in November and has asked for a briefing before the 2026-11-12 board.
- Board term renewal for Ines, to discuss early 2027.
- Institute support letter for the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]], requested via [[chloe-armitage]].

## History With The Group

Margarethe recruited Ines to Corvane and has followed the group's shift from tumour evolution alone towards cohort and liquid biopsy work with interest, partly because it makes heavier use of institute resources: the [[corvane-biobank]], the sequencing core and now the registry linkage. In 2025 she agreed to fund the registry agreement fees from institute core funds for its first two years, on the argument that several groups, not only ours, would use it. She will want evidence of that wider use before the third year; [[data-request-statistics-2025]] is a start.

She is also the escalation route if the policy revision stalls again. Ines has agreed with [[ruth-abernathy]] not to use that route unless the November board defers for reasons unrelated to the substance.
