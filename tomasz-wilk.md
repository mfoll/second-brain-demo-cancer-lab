# Tomasz Wilk

Halo site PI at Hollinmoor University Medical Centre, where the consortium's targeted methylation sequencing assay runs. He leads Work Package 3 (reference materials and truth set) and his group prepared the contrived tier of the [[halo-truth-set]].

## Role

- Site PI, Hollinmoor: enzymatic-conversion targeted methylation panel, about 2,600 regions, with a site-specific classifier.
- WP3 lead: sourced the nucleosomal reference material, ran the dilution series, verified VAFs by ddPCR before shipment, and wrote the contrived-tier section of the [[halo-truth-set-design-document]].
- Works with [[liam-oconnell]], his site bioinformatician.

## How We Work Together

Tomasz is our closest methodological neighbour: his panel and our [[methylation-panel-design]] overlap on 412 regions, and he reviewed our panel design in 2025 informally before Halo had any formal role. Generous with protocols, quick on email, and direct when he thinks an analysis is unfair to his assay.

He was initially unhappy that the WP4 analysis would score his methylation assay on mutation-dilution samples it was never designed to detect. The fix, agreed at the [[halo-steering-committee-2026-02-26]], was assay-appropriate primary endpoints per tier, with cross-assay comparisons only where every assay can produce a result.

## Current Threads

- Commutability: he added the eight sheared genomic DNA samples to the contrived tier at Ines's request, which is what exposed the fragmentomics problem ([[commutability-of-reference-materials]]).
- Wants a second contrived batch in 2027 with methylation and fragment features both realistic; he cited [[he-2026-methylated-cfdna-reference-materials]] as a model.
- Possible joint methods note on overlapping methylation regions with [[dmitri-halvorsen]].

## History With The Group

Tomasz drafted the contrived-only truth-set plan with Ines in October 2025 ([[halo-truth-set-draft-plan-2025]]) and accepted its rejection gracefully; the dilution design survived into v2. He also hosted Dmitri for a methods visit at Hollinmoor in 2025.
