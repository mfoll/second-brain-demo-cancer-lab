# Sequencing Core User Committee Minutes 2026-06-18

Approved minutes of [[sequencing-core-user-committee-2026-06-18]], circulated by [[rafael-ibarrondo]]'s office on 2026-06-25.

## Key Points Recorded

- Price list for 2026-07-01 approved with the low-input surcharge reduced from the proposed 12% to 7% after objections from Ines and two other liquid biopsy users ([[sequencing-core-price-list-2026]]).
- Median turnaround for cfDNA low-pass runs in Q1 to Q2 2026: 19 working days against a 15-day target. Cause: one instrument down for three weeks in April.
- Queue rule clarified: projects with a submitted quarterly forecast get priority over unforecast requests of the same size.
- Instrument replacement: the core will prepare a 2027 replacement plan for the October meeting.
- Data delivery: from September 2026 the core delivers to Halyard project storage instead of Kestrel, coordinated with [[ilse-van-dorn]].

## Notes For The Group
