# Owen McAllister Offer 2026-09-25

Decision meeting of the interview panel for the spatial-omics postdoc, Friday 2026-09-25, 09:00 to 10:30, followed by the offer call. Panel: Ines Varga (chair), [[amara-okonkwo]], [[jonas-eriksen]], [[aisha-bello]] (HR).

## At a Glance

> [!success]
> The panel selected Owen McAllister from five interviewed candidates; Mateo Silva was the runner-up and agreed to a reserve place. Offer made by phone the same afternoon, accepted on 2026-10-09, start date 2027-01-11.

## Inputs

- Completed [[postdoc-interview-scoring-sheet]] forms from all four panel members for the five candidates interviewed on 2026-09-14 and 2026-09-15.
- Written feedback from the team session, collected by [[priya-raman]] without names attached to comments.
- References for the two leading candidates, received by 2026-09-23.

## Discussion

After totals, two candidates were clearly ahead: Owen McAllister (mean weighted score 4.3 of 5) and [[mateo-silva]] (4.1). The other three were between 3.2 and 3.6.

The panel compared the two against what the [[colorectal-metastasis-atlas]] needs in 2027: tracking tumour clones across paired pre- and post-chemotherapy samples, and handling batch differences between sequencing batches.

- Owen: had inferred copy-number clones from spatial transcriptomics and followed them across matched samples, and had run the analysis for a multi-site spatial study. His talk was less polished on methods but concrete about what went wrong and how they fixed it. References described him as the person others went to when a dataset would not behave.
- Mateo: stronger and more rigorous on deconvolution, which overlaps with [[kenji-mori]]'s [[deconvolution-benchmark]]. Less experience with clonal inference and multi-site work. Strong team-session feedback.

Jonas pointed out that hiring Mateo would double the group's deconvolution expertise while leaving clone tracking across the pairs uncovered. Amara agreed, and said she would rather supervise someone who fills the gap. Aisha confirmed the process met [[institute-postdoc-hiring-guidance]] and that the margin was documented.

## Decisions

- Offer to Owen McAllister at postdoc grade, step 3, three-year fixed term funded by the [[frontier-starting-grant]], start 2027-01-11 or earlier by agreement.
- Mateo Silva as reserve until 2026-12-31, with feedback from Ines.
- The three other candidates receive written feedback from Ines by 2026-10-02.

## Follow-up

- Offer call 2026-09-25 afternoon; Owen asked for two weeks to decide and accepted on 2026-10-09.
