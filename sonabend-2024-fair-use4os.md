# FAIR-USE4OS: Guidelines for creating impactful open-source software.

Sonabend R et al., PLOS Computational Biology, 2024.

## Summary

Extends FAIR with "USE": user-centred, sustainable and equitable. The authors argue that funder mandates for open-source software can be met by a public repository with a DOI and nothing more, and propose criteria that build user input, accessible design and long-term sustainability planning into software from the start.

## Why It Matters

Read with [[barker-2022-fair-principles-research-software]]. The "user-centred" part matters most for the [[atlas-data-portal]] idea: a public browser nobody can use is worse than a well-documented data release. Ines noted it as a criterion if the portal ever moves from Idea to Active.

## Caveats

Guidelines for planning and assessment; no evidence yet on whether they change outcomes.
