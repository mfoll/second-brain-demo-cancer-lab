# Paolo Ferrante

Biobank manager at Corvane and custodian of the Northvale samples in the [[corvane-biobank]]. Every Northvale aliquot the group has used went through him.

## Role in our work

Paolo runs the biobank's access process: he checks a request against the cohort consent, the remaining volume per participant and the biobank's retrieval rules, prepares it for the biobank access committee, and plans retrieval. For the [[northvale-early-detection-study]] he retrieved 1,230 plasma aliquots (1 ml each) and shipped them to the sequencing core on 2025-09-23 ([[northvale-sample-shipment-2025-09-23]]). For the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]] he released 6,000 buffy coat DNA aliquots in two batches in late 2025.

Rules he enforces that shape our designs:

- The last-aliquot rule: no participant's final plasma aliquot is released. This excluded 23 eligible cases before matching.
- Maximum 1 ml plasma per participant per project unless the access committee agrees otherwise.
- Retrieval in freezer order, not request order; a request sorted by rack position is processed weeks faster.
- Unused aliquots are returned, not discarded, and the return is logged.

## How we work together

- Talk to him before writing a request, not after. He reviewed the draft of [[northvale-biobank-sample-request-2025]] and suggested matching on draw date within three months would be feasible because the 2004 to 2009 freezers are well indexed.
- He asks for manifests as CSV with the biobank's sample identifiers in the first column.
- Lead time from committee approval to shipment was about three months for 1,230 aliquots.
