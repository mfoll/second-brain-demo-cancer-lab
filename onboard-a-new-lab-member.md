# Onboard A New Lab Member

How we bring a new postdoc, student, engineer or visitor into the group so that they can run real work within two weeks. Owner: [[priya-raman]]. Next use: [[owen-mcallister]], starting 2027-01-11.

## Inputs

- Signed contract or visitor agreement from HR ([[aisha-bello]]).
- Start date, role, main project and supervisor.
- A copy of the [[lab-onboarding-checklist]] tab for the person.

## Steps

1. **Four weeks before.** Priya opens the checklist tab and requests institute ID, email and GitLab accounts. Ines names a buddy (not the supervisor).
2. **Three weeks before.** Send the data protection training link; the certificate is required for Halyard. Ask [[yara-castellanos]] to prepare data access for the datasets the role needs, and only those.
3. **Two weeks before.** Once the certificate arrives, Tobias requests the Halyard account and any partition memberships ([[request-halyard-gpu-time]] for GPU users). Desk and equipment ordered.
4. **Day one.** HR appointment, building induction, lunch with the buddy, welcome email to the group, reading list: [[lab-handbook]], [[halyard-user-guide]], [[group-data-management-plan]].
5. **First week.** Run the cfDNA test profile ([[run-the-cfdna-pipeline-on-halyard]]) even if the role is not cfDNA; 20-minute meetings with each project lead; added to the lab meeting and review rotas ([[who-owns-what]]).
6. **First month.** Introductory lab meeting talk; authorship conversation for their main project ([[authorship-and-credit-guidelines]]); 30-day check-in with Ines and the buddy.

## Verification

All checklist items have a done date by day 30. If the person could not submit a Halyard job in week one, record why and fix the step for the next person.

## Guardrails

Never grant access to clinical-linked data before the training certificate and Yara's approval, even for a day.
