# MR Reading Group Proposal 2025

Archived. Proposal from 2025-10-20, when the [[mendelian-randomization]] cluster was created, to run a six-session reading group on Mendelian randomization for the group and two other institute groups.

## What It Proposed

Fortnightly sessions from 2025-11 to 2026-01, led by [[lucas-brandt]], following a reading list to be sourced externally.

## What Happened

Only two sessions ran before the Christmas break, and attendance fell to four. [[hiroshi-tanabe]]'s reading list ([[mr-reading-list-hiroshi-tanabe]]) arrived in 2025-11 and made a structured course unnecessary for the people who needed it. Archived at the [[exploratory-reading-review-2026-10]]; superseded by [[proposal-fold-mendelian-randomization-into-ch-cancer-risk-analysis]], which turns the reading into analysis.
