# How Should The Course Handle Generative AI In Assessments?

What rule should the 2027 cancer genomics course set for generative AI tools in assessed code and reports?

## Background

In the [[course-week-liquid-biopsy-2026-02-16]] practical, several students submitted working classifier code they could not explain. The final project oral defence ([[course-final-project-presentations-2026-05-22]]) caught two such cases. The graduate school has no policy yet beyond "declare use".

## Options

- Ban for assessed work. Unenforceable and at odds with how students will work in the group afterwards.
- Allow with declaration, and weight the oral defence of methods higher (from 40% to 50%).
- Allow, and add a short written critique of AI-generated code as an exercise, in the spirit of [[teufel-2026-teaching-bioinformatics-generative-ai]].

## Leaning

Ines leans towards the second and third options combined. [[maja-kovac]] worries that the epidemiology block's simulated-cohort exercise becomes trivial with an assistant. Decision before the syllabus freeze in January 2027.
