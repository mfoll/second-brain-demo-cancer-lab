# Authorship And Credit Guidelines

How the group decides authorship and other credit. First written in 2024, revised at the [[group-retreat-2026]] after the [[retreat-2026-pre-survey]] asked for earlier conversations.

## When

Authorship is discussed when a project starts and recorded in its Project note, then revisited when the first draft is written. Changes after that are explained to everyone affected.

## Who Is An Author

- Anyone who made a substantial contribution to design, data generation, analysis, software or interpretation, and who reads and approves the final text.
- Engineering and data stewardship count when the result depends on them ([[research-engineers-deserve-authorship]]).
- Clinical and consortium collaborators follow the agreed rules of their study or consortium.

## Order

First author did most of the work and wrote the draft; last author is the senior person accountable. Joint positions are allowed and stated.

## Other Credit

- Pipelines get citable releases, cited in methods.
- Students present their own work at conferences before anyone else does.
- Acknowledgements name people, not just teams.

## Disagreements

Raise them with Ines first; if that does not resolve it, the institute ombudsperson.
