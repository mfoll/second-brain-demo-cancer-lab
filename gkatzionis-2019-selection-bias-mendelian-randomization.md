# Contextualizing selection bias in Mendelian randomization: how bad is it likely to be?

Gkatzionis A, Burgess S. International Journal of Epidemiology, 2019.

## Summary

Simulation study of selection bias in MR, which arises when entering the sample depends on a collider between the instrument and confounders. Bias and type 1 error inflation were severe only when selection effects were large; for moderate selection the bias was generally small. Inverse probability weighting was examined as a correction.

## Why It Matters

The reassuring half of the selection-bias argument for Northvale. Participation in the cohort is voluntary and healthier people enrol, but the exposure here is CH, which is mostly invisible to participants at recruitment. [[maja-kovac]] reads this as "probably small" for CH but not for smoking-related instruments. It anchors [[does-selection-into-northvale-bias-a-one-sample-mendelian-randomization]].

## Caveats

Results depend on simulated selection strengths; nobody knows the real ones for Northvale. Read with [[smit-2019-survival-bias-mendelian-randomization]], which looks at the related problem of survival to enrolment.
