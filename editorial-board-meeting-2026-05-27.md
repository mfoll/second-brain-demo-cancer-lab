# Editorial Board Meeting 2026-05-27

Annual in-person meeting of the Genome Methods Letters editorial board, held online this year. 14 board members, chaired by the editor-in-chief. Ines attended the full three hours.

## Main Decision

The board agreed a trial of [[open-peer-review]] starting with submissions from 2027-01: review reports and author responses published alongside accepted papers when authors consent, reviewer names published only when the reviewer opts in. Uptake and any effect on reviewer acceptance rates will be reviewed in 2028.

## Discussion

- [[lena-vogt]] proposed signed reviews by default with an opt-out. Ines and three others argued that early-career reviewers would decline or soften criticism of senior authors; the opt-in compromise passed by a show of hands.
- Concern from two members that publishing reports would slow reviewers down. No data either way; the trial will record time to report.
- Ines was asked to draft reviewer guidance for the trial (due 2026-11-30).

## Other Items

- Submission numbers up 18% on 2025; desk rejection rate unchanged at about 40%.
- New policy that methods papers must include runnable code with test data, or explain why not. Ines had pushed for this since her February review ([[review-report-genome-methods-letters-2026-02]]).
