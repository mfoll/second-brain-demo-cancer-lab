# Corvane RSE Survey 2025

Institute survey of research software practice run by [[nikolai-petrov]]'s research software engineering team in 2025-10 and 2025-11, reported to group leaders on 2025-12-10. 41 of 58 research groups answered.

## Findings That Matter To Us

- 34 of 41 groups maintain code that their published results depend on; 22 said one person knows how to run it.
- 15 groups had lost the ability to rerun an analysis after someone left in the previous three years.
- Only 9 groups had automated tests on any repository.
- Groups wanted help with containers and HPC migration more than with training.

## Why It Matters

It put numbers on what we had seen with the single-cell CNV code. Our group answered "one person" for four of six core tools at the time; the [[group-software-inventory]] tracks how that has changed. The report fed the institute's case for two more RSE posts, still pending.

## Caveats

Self-reported, and groups that answered are probably the more engaged ones.
