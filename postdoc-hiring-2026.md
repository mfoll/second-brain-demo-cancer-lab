# Postdoc Hiring 2026

Recruitment of one postdoc in spatial omics to work on the [[colorectal-metastasis-atlas]] alongside [[amara-okonkwo]], funded from the [[frontier-starting-grant]].

## Outcome

One spatial-omics postdoc hired and a start date agreed. Reached: [[owen-mcallister]] accepted on 2026-10-09 and starts on 2027-01-11.

## Current State

- Completed. Five candidates were interviewed on 2026-09-14 and 2026-09-15; the panel selected Owen McAllister on 2026-09-25 ([[owen-mcallister-offer-2026-09-25]]).
- [[mateo-silva]] was the runner-up. He agreed to stay on the reserve list until 2026-12-31, in case the start falls through.
- Owen's contract is signed; HR paperwork is with [[aisha-bello]]. Pre-arrival tasks have moved to [[lead-tumour-evolution-group]] next actions.

## Scope and Boundaries

This project covered the advert, shortlisting, interviews, decision and offer. Onboarding is a group workflow ([[onboard-a-new-lab-member]]), not part of this project. Candidate documents (CVs, references, scoring forms) stay in the HR system and are not copied into the vault; only the anonymised [[postdoc-interview-scoring-sheet]] template and aggregate counts are kept here.

## Working Context

Timeline:

- 2026-05-04: role defined with Amara and [[priya-raman]]; Frontier budget line confirmed with [[chloe-armitage]].
- 2026-05-18: advert published ([[postdoc-job-advert-2026]]); closing date 2026-06-30.
- 41 applications. Longlist of 9 agreed on 2026-07-10 using the published criteria; shortlist of 5 after short calls in August ([[postdoc-shortlist-2026]]).
- 2026-09-14/15: interviews. Panel: Ines Varga (chair), Amara Okonkwo, [[jonas-eriksen]] as an external group leader, Aisha Bello for HR. Each candidate gave a 15-minute talk, a 45-minute panel interview and a 30-minute informal session with the team without Ines present.
- 2026-09-25: decision meeting; offer made the same day.
- 2026-10-09: offer accepted.

The scoring sheet weighted spatial data analysis experience, ability to work across wet and dry lab, and evidence of collaborative work. The team session fed into scoring as one criterion, following [[institute-postdoc-hiring-guidance]]. The close call between Owen and Mateo is recorded in the offer event: Owen was stronger on clonal inference across paired samples and had run a multi-site study; Mateo was stronger on deconvolution methods.

Lessons for next time: the June closing date overlapped with the [[halo-annual-meeting-2026]] and the longlist slipped by ten days; the team session was the most informative part and should stay.
