# Helena Strom

Secretary of the institute ethics committee. She attends the [[data-governance-board-membership|data governance board]] as an observer when an item touches consent or approvals.

## Role

Helena is the person to ask whether an existing approval or consent covers a new use. She acknowledged the ethics notification for the [[registry-linkage-agreement]] in September 2025, confirming that both the Northvale and the St Aldric consents cover registry follow-up.

## How We Work Together

Send her the exact consent wording and the exact proposed use; she answers within a week, usually with a clear yes, no or "needs an amendment". She does not give informal opinions on email that she would later have to retract.

## Current Threads

- At [[data-governance-board-2026-09-10]] she read the St Aldric consent wording as covering pseudonymised multi-site pooling, and committed to a formal ethics committee note before the November board.
- The consent form for any new St Aldric recruitment in 2027 should name consortium sharing explicitly; she suggested wording and [[samuel-okoye]] has it.

## History With The Group

Helena handled the 2025 ethics amendment for the St Aldric study, which added Corvane as analysis site and allowed registry linkage, and the ethics notification for the registry linkage. In both cases she pointed out wording issues early enough that the documents did not need a second round.

## Notes For Working With Her

Her view is that consent forms should describe the realistic sharing path, not the minimum. She has said more than once that the St Aldric form, with its "academic collaborators" wording, is good enough for consortium pooling but would not be good enough for commercial access, and that any discussion with industry partners about the classifier must keep the two cases apart. That is consistent with the rules in [[conflict-of-interest-handling-vendor-contacts]] and the reading recorded in [[st-aldric-consent-wording]].

Her formal note on the St Aldric consent scope is due before the November board; Ines should check on 2026-11-02 that it is in the board papers.
