# Clonal Hematopoiesis and Solid Cancers.

## Summary

A review of the relationship between clonal hematopoiesis and solid cancers: prevalence of the common CH genes (DNMT3A, TET2, ASXL1, TP53) in people with solid tumours, shared risk factors (age, germline variation, environmental exposures), and how CH-mutant immune cells may shape the tumour microenvironment through inflammation, immune regulation and angiogenesis. It presents CH both as a marker of risk and a possible driver of progression.

## Why It Matters

The starting review for the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]], read by Lucas in the project's first week. Its list of shared risk factors became the confounder list in the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis-plan]]; smoking is the one we cannot measure well ([[how-should-we-adjust-for-smoking-in-the-ch-cancer-risk-analysis]]).

## Caveats

Narrative review; much of the evidence is in patients already diagnosed, not prospective cohorts.
