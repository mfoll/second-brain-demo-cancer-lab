# Exosome Pilot Stop 2025-06-19

Decision meeting on the [[exosome-rna-pilot]] with [[dmitri-halvorsen]], [[elias-moreau]] and Ines.

## Evidence Presented

Elias showed the yields from 24 samples: median 2.1 ng RNA from 4 ml plasma, 9 of 24 libraries below the complexity threshold, tumour-associated transcripts in 5 of 12 patients, all of whom already had high cfDNA tumour fraction ([[exosome-rna-yield-results]]).

## Decision

> [!note]
> Stop the exosome RNA pilot. The RNA signal appeared only where cfDNA already detected the tumour, so it adds cost without adding sensitivity. Remaining kit budget and Elias's bench time move to the methylation panel.

## Discussion

Dmitri argued for one more round with a different isolation kit. Ines declined: better yield would not fix the absence of signal in low-tumour-fraction cases, which was the whole point. The [[methylation-panel-design]] was already showing usable yields at the same plasma volume. The pilot was recorded as Superseded on 2025-06-30 and its plan archived ([[exosome-rna-pilot-plan]]).
