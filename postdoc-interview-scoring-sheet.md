# Postdoc Interview Scoring Sheet

The blank scoring form used by the panel in September 2026, agreed with [[aisha-bello]] on 2026-09-10. Completed forms are in the HR system and are not copied here.

## Summary

Each panel member scores each candidate independently, 1 to 5, on six weighted criteria before any discussion. Totals are shared only at the decision meeting.

## Key Details

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Spatial or single-cell data analysis | 25% |
| Clonal or copy-number inference, integration with histology | 20% |
| Handling of messy, batch-affected or multi-site data | 15% |
| Talk: clarity and honesty about limitations | 15% |
| Collaboration and evidence of finishing work | 15% |
| Team session feedback | 10% |

- Each score needs one sentence of evidence; empty evidence cells were sent back before the decision meeting.
- Ties within 0.3 points are discussed against the project's next-year needs, as happened in [[owen-mcallister-offer-2026-09-25]].

## Why It Matters

Reusable for the next hire. The weights would change for a non-spatial role but the structure would not.
