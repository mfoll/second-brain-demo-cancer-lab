# Methylation Panel QC Report 2025-09

Validation QC report for the methylation panel, compiled by Dmitri from the validation runs and the spike-in titration, dated 2025-09-12 and signed off on 2025-09-15 ([[methylation-panel-qc-sign-off-2025-09-15]]).

## Key Details

- Validation runs: 48 libraries over two sequencing runs at the core, coordinated with [[rafael-ibarrondo|Rafael Ibarrondo]].
- Coverage: 98.6% of regions above 500x median deduplicated depth; median duplicate rate 38% at 30 ng.
- Conversion: above 99.2% on lambda controls in all libraries.
- Reproducibility: region-level methylation correlation 0.97 across six replicate pairs.
- Limit of detection: summarised from [[methylation-panel-spike-in-titration-2025-08]].
- 22 regions with persistently low capture (GC above 75%) excluded from scoring; listed in [[methylation-panel-region-list]].

## Verdict

All acceptance criteria from [[methylation-panel-design-document-v4]] met.
