# Lab Meeting Format

How the fortnightly lab meeting runs since 2026-10-08, adopted at the [[group-retreat-2026]]. Replaces [[lab-meeting-format-2025]].

## Shape

Thursday 10:00 to 11:00, seminar room 2, hybrid link for visitors.

1. **Operations, 15 minutes, strict.** Items submitted to [[priya-raman]] by Tuesday noon, each marked decide or inform. Inform items are written in the agenda and not presented. The chair stops discussion at 15 minutes; undecided items get an owner and a written follow-up within a week.
2. **Science, 30 minutes.** One presenter from the [[lab-meeting-rota-2026]]: 15 minutes of talk, 15 of discussion. Half-finished work is welcome.
3. **Round, 10 minutes.** Anyone blocked says so in one sentence.

## Notes

The note-taker writes an Event note `lab-meeting-YYYY-MM-DD` the same day, starting with decisions, then actions with owners and dates, then everything else. The latest is [[lab-meeting-2026-10-22]].

## Roles

Chair (Ines, or Dmitri when she is away), note-taker and timekeeper rotate per the rota.

First review at the January 2027 operations block, against the [[retreat-2026-pre-survey]] baseline.
