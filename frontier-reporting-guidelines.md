# Frontier Reporting Guidelines

## Summary

The Frontier Research Fund's guidance for interim reports on Starting Grants, version of 2026, read on 2026-10-01 when the [[frontier-interim-report]] Project opened.

## Key Details

- Scientific report: eight pages maximum, structured by work package and milestone.
- Every milestone is reported as met, partly met or not met; deviations need a reason and a recovery plan.
- An unexplained missed milestone can trigger a review meeting with the programme manager.
- Publications list with open access status; preprints are listed separately, and the guidance does not say whether they count towards milestones.
- Financial statement prepared by the host institution, separate from the scientific report.

## Caveats

The publication milestone rule is ambiguous; question to [[thabo-nkosi]] pending.
