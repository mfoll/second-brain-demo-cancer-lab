# Exploratory Reading

The designated home for reading that has no Project yet. Ines and the group read well outside the current studies, and a paper about pathology foundation models or Mendelian randomization still needs a place to live before anyone knows whether it will turn into work. This Responsibility answers for those notes until a Project absorbs them.

## Scope

- Exploration clusters are Topics that `belongs_to` this note. Their Sources, Concepts, Questions and Opinions belong to the Topic, not here.
- A cluster is created once three notes share a theme (per [[adr/0011-root-belongs-to-at-responsibilities-and-home-exploration-clusters-in-topics|ADR 0011]]). Loose single papers wait in the Inbox until they find a cluster or a Project.
- Reading for a live Project goes straight to that Project. Reading for review or the symposium belongs to [[peer-review-and-editorial]].
- Not a backlog to clear. Some clusters are expected to stay small and be archived.

## How Promotion Works Here

When a cluster gains an outcome, actions, a collaborator or a deliverable, the next review proposes a Project. If an existing Project absorbs it, the Topic is re-parented under that Project and its notes follow automatically through the Topic. Creating or re-parenting a Project still needs Ines's explicit approval; the review note records the proposal and the decision separately.

Signals we look for: someone in the group is spending paid time on it, a grant aim names it, or a dataset we hold could answer one of the cluster's Questions. Reading volume alone does not count.
