# St Aldric MRD Consent Wording

Version 3 of the participant consent form of the St Aldric MRD study, approved in 2022 and used for all 212 patients. Extracted for the board papers of [[data-governance-board-2026-09-10]].

## Relevant Clauses

- Consent covers cfDNA, tumour and germline research, and data sharing with "academic collaborators under a data transfer agreement".
- Participants agree to follow-up "through hospital records and national health registers".
- Samples may be stored for up to 15 years for research on cancer.
- There is no clause on commercial use.

## Reading

[[helena-strom]] reads the sharing clause as covering pseudonymised multi-site pooling within an academic research consortium, provided the consortium agreement counts as the data transfer agreement; she will confirm in an ethics committee note. The 2025 ethics amendment that added Corvane as analysis site and allowed registry linkage is described in [[st-aldric-mrd-cohort]]. It does not obviously cover commercial partners, which matters for any later discussion with [[ravi-vellanki]]'s company. Registry follow-up is covered, consistent with the [[registry-linkage-agreement]].
