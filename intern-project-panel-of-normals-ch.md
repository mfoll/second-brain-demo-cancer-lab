# Intern Project Panel Of Normals For CH

## Summary

One-page description of [[noor-haddad]]'s internship topic, written by [[hannah-lindqvist]] on 2026-03-20: can a panel of normals built from healthy-donor buffy coat replace matched buffy coat sequencing for removing clonal hematopoiesis variants from cfDNA calls?

## Key Details

- Data: 96 healthy-donor buffy coat samples already sequenced on the methylation panel; 40 St Aldric patients with both cfDNA and matched buffy coat.
- Expected figure: per patient, the CH variants removed by matched buffy coat versus by the panel, with misses listed by gene.
- Supervisors: Hannah (daily), [[dmitri-halvorsen]] (second), Ines (formal).
- Out of scope: changing the MRD calling thresholds.

## Why It Matters

Matched buffy coat costs about 180 EUR per sample. A good-enough panel would save that for the prospective extension planned in the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]]. The internship result is an input, not an answer; the question stays with the [[clonal-hematopoiesis-filter]] Project.
