# Northvale Assay Budget

The cost assumptions behind the Northvale design choices, as agreed with [[rafael-ibarrondo]]'s sequencing core in April 2025.

## Per sample

- cfDNA extraction from 1 ml plasma: 28 EUR (consumables and technician time, [[elias-moreau]]).
- Methylation panel library and capture: 145 EUR.
- Sequencing at the target depth: 52 EUR, rising to 64 EUR at the deeper target chosen after dropping the third control.
- Total about 237 EUR per sample at the final depth.

## Study totals

- 1,230 samples plus 9 re-sequenced: about 294,000 EUR.
- Three controls per case would have added about 97,000 EUR. The [[northvale-power-calculation]] showed the gain was small, so the money went into depth instead ([[are-two-controls-per-case-enough-for-northvale]]).
- CH subcohort panel (buffy coat, 42 genes, 6,000 samples): about 61 EUR per sample, 366,000 EUR, budgeted separately under the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]].

## Caveat

Prices were the core's 2025 internal rates; 2026 rates are about 6% higher, which matters for any second case set.
