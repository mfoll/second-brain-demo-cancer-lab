# Registry Linkage Application 2025

The institute's application to the [[northern-cancer-registry]] for an institute-level linkage agreement, submitted on 2025-05-26 after the board endorsed it at [[data-governance-board-2025-05-22]].

## Key Details

- Requested populations: Northvale Health Cohort (86,400) and St Aldric MRD patients (212).
- Justification: two active studies needing incidence and death follow-up, the [[northvale-early-detection-study]] and the [[pancreatic-cfdna-mrd-study]], plus the planned [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]].
- Proposed refresh frequency: quarterly. The registry offered twice a year, which we accepted.
- Proposed identifiers: national ID and date of birth only. The registry added sex and postcode sector for fallback matching after [[daniel-asante]] showed that about 0.6% of matches in the earlier cohort-specific linkage needed them.
