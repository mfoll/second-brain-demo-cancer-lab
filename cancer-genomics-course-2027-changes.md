# Cancer Genomics Course 2027 Changes

Changes planned for the fourth edition, drawn from the [[cancer-genomics-course-2026-feedback-survey]] and the teaching team's debrief on 2026-06-12. To confirm with the graduate school by 2026-12-15.

## Agreed

- Student accounts on [[halyard-cluster]] instead of Kestrel, created and tested a week before the course starts. [[tobias-wren]] will reserve a small partition for the practical weeks.
- The spatial block shortened from three sessions to two; the survey's most common comment was that it covered too many methods.
- Oral defence of methods kept at 40% or raised to 50%, depending on the generative AI decision.

## Proposed

- A short ethics and data governance session with [[yara-castellanos]], since several students asked how real cohort data are accessed.
- [[zoe-carmichael]] back as guest lecturer.
- Teaching assistants: [[kenji-mori]] again, and [[hannah-lindqvist]] instead of Sofia, whose committee year is busy.

## Not Changing

The simulated cfDNA dataset for the liquid biopsy week stays; no real patient data in teaching.
