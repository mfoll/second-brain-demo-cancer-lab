# Corvane Research Data Policy

The institute's research data policy, 2024 edition, which every group DMP must follow. Read by [[yara-castellanos]] and Ines at the start of [[lab-data-management-plan]].

## Summary

Sets institute-wide rules for storage, retention, access and sharing of research data, and requires each group to name an accountable owner and a data steward.

## Key Details

- Personal data processed for research needs a data protection assessment signed by [[victor-lindahl]].
- Minimum retention of 10 years for data underlying publications.
- Transfers outside the institute need an agreement; the 2024 text did not address consortium transfers explicitly, which is what the [[data-access-policy-revision]] now addresses.

## Why It Matters

Our [[group-data-management-plan]] is written as an application of this policy, and its retention and access rules come from here.
