# Exosome RNA Yield Results

[[elias-moreau]]'s summary of the 24 exosome RNA samples, presented at the [[exosome-pilot-stop-2025-06-19]].

## Key Details

- RNA yield from 4 ml plasma: median 2.1 ng (range 0.6 to 5.8). The two kits differed by less than the sample-to-sample range.
- Library complexity: 9 of 24 below threshold.
- Tumour-associated transcripts: detected in 5 of 12 patients, none of 12 controls. All 5 had cfDNA tumour fraction above 2%; none of the 7 low-fraction patients showed signal.

## Caveats

Banked plasma, some samples stored over three years. Fresh plasma might yield more, but would not change the comparison with cfDNA.
