# Should Halo Harmonise Pre-Analytics Or Correct Statistically?

## Why It Matters

The six sites collected plasma with different tubes, delays and spins ([[halo-sop-comparison-preanalytics]]). Processing delay changes cfDNA yield and fragment profiles, so unharmonised samples can make one assay look worse for reasons unrelated to the assay.

## Options Considered

- Harmonise everything, including legacy site cohorts: cleanest, but would discard most existing samples at three sites.
- Correct statistically with site and delay as covariates: keeps samples, but [[sun-hee-park]] warned that a correction cannot recover fragment-profile information lost to white-cell DNA release.
- Mixed: harmonise prospectively collected truth-set plasma, adjust legacy samples, and report them separately.

## Current Answer

Mixed option, adopted at the [[halo-steering-committee-2026-02-26]]. All truth-set plasma follows one protocol: cfDNA preservative tubes, plasma within 4 hours, double spin. Legacy samples may be used in site-specific analyses with adjustment and are never pooled with truth-set results. [[dohmen-2026-preanalytical-cfdna-evdna]], read a month later, supports making processing delay the strict variable.

## Remaining Doubt

Kelstow abstained on cost; if a 2027 extension of the truth set happens, tube cost will come back.
