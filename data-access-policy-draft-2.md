# Data Access Policy Revision Draft 2

## Summary

Second draft of the revised policy, written by Ines and [[yara-castellanos]] after [[data-governance-board-2026-05-14]] and sent to [[eva-novak]] for Halo legal review on 2026-06-10. It introduced the consortium transfer clause as a new section 5A.

## Key Details

- Section 5A used option A from [[consortium-transfer-clause-options]]: board approval of the consortium agreement once, then free use within the consortium's stated aims.
- Added definitions of pseudonymised, aggregate and anonymous data.
- Kept the onward transfer prohibition but allowed hosting in a consortium environment.

## What Happened Next

Eva's review (2026-07-21) said Halo could live with option A but that the receiving sites' own committees would ask for per-use review anyway. [[victor-lindahl]] considered option A too open: "stated aims" of a consortium can be broad enough to cover almost anything. Draft 3 moved to option B with per-use [[data-access-committee]] approval.
