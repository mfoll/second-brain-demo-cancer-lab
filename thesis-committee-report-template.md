# Thesis Committee Report Template

## Summary

The graduate school's form for annual PhD committee reports, 2026 version. Used for [[sofia-thesis-committee]] and the group's other students.

## Key Details

- Part A, student: progress against last year's plan, outputs, training completed, plan for next year (four pages maximum).
- Part B, committee: assessment of progress (satisfactory, satisfactory with conditions, unsatisfactory), recommendations, and a confidential section the student does not see.
- Signatures from all members within ten working days of the meeting.
- The 2026 version adds a question on teaching hours and one on whether the student has had a conversation about careers.
