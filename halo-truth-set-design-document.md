# Halo Truth Set Design Document

Design v2 of the [[halo-truth-set]], dated 2026-01-30, written by WP3 ([[tomasz-wilk]]) and WP4 (Ines, [[sun-hee-park]]), approved at the [[halo-steering-committee-2026-02-26]]. Replaces the contrived-only draft ([[halo-truth-set-draft-plan-2025]]).

## Summary

Three tiers answering three questions: contrived material for analytical limits, patient plasma for clinical performance, healthy donors for specificity. Six aliquots per sample, one per site, blinded.

## Key Details

- Sample size reasoning: four replicates per VAF level is the most the reference material budget allowed (about 26,000 EUR for the contrived tier). For the patient tier, 60 patients with an expected 35 to 40% recurrence gives roughly plus or minus 15 percentage points on sensitivity per cancer type. The document says plainly that this is a benchmark, not a validation study.
- Sheared genomic DNA samples were added at Ines's request to measure commutability directly rather than assume it ([[commutability-of-reference-materials]]).
- Pre-analytics follow the harmonised protocol from [[halo-sop-comparison-preanalytics]].
- Rejected options: buying a commercial multi-analyte reference panel for everything (no methylation component at the right fractions, and fragment profiles not documented); using residual clinical samples without follow-up (no endpoint).

## Caveats

Written before the pilot panel; the pilot later showed the commutability problem was larger than expected for fragmentomics ([[halo-pilot-panel-results-2026-04]]).
