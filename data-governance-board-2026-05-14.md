# Data Governance Board 2026-05-14

First board discussion of the policy revision, on draft 1 (circulated 2026-04-20).

## Discussion

- Draft 1 only rewrote definitions and steward thresholds; it had a placeholder for consortium sharing. The board accepted the definitions with minor edits.
- On consortium sharing, two models were discussed: approve the consortium agreement once, or approve each use. [[ruth-abernathy]] circulated [[lawson-2024-data-access-committee-survey]] in advance and argued for a defined per-use review with a target turnaround, run by a small [[data-access-committee]] function rather than the full board.
- [[paolo-ferrante]] asked that samples stay out of any new clause.
- [[victor-lindahl]] said any consortium clause would need its own DPIA and that he would need at least six weeks once the text was stable.

## Decisions

- Drafting group to write the consortium clause for the next draft, with both options described for the board ([[consortium-transfer-clause-options]]).
- Halo legal to be consulted on the text before the September board.
