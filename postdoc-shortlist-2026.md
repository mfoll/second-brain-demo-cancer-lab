Anonymised summary of the shortlisting for the spatial-omics postdoc, agreed on 2026-08-28. Names and CVs stay in the HR system.

## Summary

41 applications received by 2026-06-30. 9 longlisted on 2026-07-10 by Ines and [[amara-okonkwo]] against the essential criteria of the [[postdoc-job-advert-2026]]. Short calls of 20 minutes in August reduced the list to 5, interviewed on 2026-09-14 and 2026-09-15.

## Key Details

- 23 applications did not meet the spatial or single-cell essential criterion.
- Of the 9 longlisted, 2 withdrew after accepting other offers, 2 were not invited after calls.
- The 5 interviewed included [[owen-mcallister]] and [[mateo-silva]].
- The longlist slipped ten days because the closing date overlapped with the [[halo-annual-meeting-2026]].
