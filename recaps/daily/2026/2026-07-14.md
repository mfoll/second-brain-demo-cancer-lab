# Tuesday 14 July 2026

## MRD manuscript: major revision

Clara Hoffmann's decision letter on the [[mrd-manuscript|MRD Manuscript]] arrived: major revision, three reports. The reviewers' points are summarised in the [[mrd-manuscript-decision-2026-07-14|decision note]] and kept in full in [[mrd-reviewer-comments-2026-07|the reviewer comments]].

Reviewer 2 is the one that matters. The editor marked external validation as essential, and Reviewer 2 explicitly rules out a temporal split within St Aldric. Reviewer 2 is right, and it is the same weakness we found ourselves in March with the extraction batches. The revision will report the stacked classifier.

## Why this is a governance problem

The realistic external samples are in the [[halo-cross-site-benchmark|Halo Cross-Site Benchmark]]. Using them for a Corvane-led paper needs data to move between Halo sites and Corvane, which the current institute policy does not allow. So the revision depends on the [[data-access-policy-revision|Data Access Policy Revision]] more than on any analysis we can do ourselves.

## Today's choices

I accepted the revision and asked for the standard 90 days, expecting to need an extension. No analysis of Halo data until the classifier is frozen ([[external-validation-before-locking-a-classifier|lock before validating]]). Revision planning next Tuesday.
