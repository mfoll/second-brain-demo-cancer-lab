# Halo Steering Committee 2026-02-26

Fourth steering committee, hybrid, hosted at Brightwater. Minutes in [[halo-steering-committee-minutes-2026-02-26]].

## Decisions

- Truth set design v2 approved: 40 contrived, 60 patient and 20 healthy-donor samples, six aliquots each ([[halo-truth-set-design-document]]).
- Pre-analytics: all truth-set patient and donor plasma collected under one harmonised protocol (cfDNA tubes, plasma within 4 hours, double spin). Legacy site samples outside the truth set may still be used with a site and processing-delay adjustment ([[should-halo-harmonise-preanalytics-or-correct-statistically]]).
- Primary endpoints are assay-appropriate per tier. Mutation-dilution samples score mutation assays; methylation-fraction samples score methylation assays; cross-assay comparisons only on the patient tier. This settled [[tomasz-wilk]]'s objection to scoring his assay on targets it does not measure.
- Pilot panel of 12 contrived samples to be shipped in March as a dry run.
- [[halo-wp4-analysis-plan]] v1.0 accepted with minor changes.
