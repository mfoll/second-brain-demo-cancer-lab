# Northvale Interim Results 2026-10

A one-page summary of where the [[lead-time-analysis]] stands before the re-run, written for Ines's October update to the group and for anyone joining the project.

## Summary

> [!abstract]
> The cfDNA methylation score is strongly associated with pancreatic and colorectal cancer diagnosed within two years of blood draw, weakly at two to five years, and not at all beyond five years. The pattern fits short lead time, reverse causation, or both; the design cannot separate them.

## Numbers (interim, pre-correction)

- Per SD of score: 4.9 in year 1 (27 cases), 2.2 in year 2 (31), 1.31 at 2 to 5 years (84), 1.06 beyond 5 years (268).
- Main model (exclude year 1): 1.24 (1.06 to 1.45).
- Pancreatic and colorectal cases show the same shape; pancreatic intervals are wide.
- Storage time: balanced within sets; residual across-set issue being checked.

Full tables: [[northvale-window-sensitivity-tables]].

## What changes next

The corrected control set (three replacements, all in matched sets of the beyond-five-year window) is unlikely to move the estimates; the replaced controls had unremarkable scores. Results to be frozen in November.
