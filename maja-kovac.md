# Maja Kovac

Postdoc in the group since January 2025, epidemiologist and biostatistician. She leads the [[lead-time-analysis]] of the [[northvale-early-detection-study]] and designed its sampling.

## Role

Maja is the person who turns the cohort into a study. She wrote [[northvale-matching-specification-v2]], ran the incidence-density sampling recorded in [[northvale-sample-selection-log]], wrote the [[northvale-power-calculation]], and is first author of the [[northvale-statistical-analysis-plan]]. She chairs the monthly Northvale analysis meetings and keeps the decision record for them.

She is also the group's default reviewer for study design questions from other projects: the case definitions in the MRD study and the sampling frame for the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]] both went past her.

## How we work together

- Weekly one-to-one with Ines on Tuesdays; Northvale items first.
- She prefers decisions in writing before code. A change to the analysis plan goes into an amendment ([[northvale-sap-amendment-1]]) before any model is re-run.
- She is direct with collaborators. At [[northvale-analysis-meeting-2026-06-24]] she argued for the 1-year exclusion against [[mei-lin-zhou]]'s 6-month preference, and then drafted the 6-month model as a named sensitivity analysis so the disagreement would be visible in the paper.
- Code lives in [[northvale-lead-time-code-repository]]; she reviews every merge herself.

## Current threads

- Re-running all models on the corrected control set after the [[registry-linkage-update]].
- The storage-time question: [[does-storage-time-confound-the-northvale-methylation-signal]].
- First author on the lead-time paper; abstract for [[tumour-evolution-symposium-2026]] in [[northvale-lead-time-symposium-abstract]].

## Career

Her contract runs to December 2027. She is named as a researcher on the [[aurora-programme-grant-2027]] application and wants an independent fellowship application in 2027; Ines agreed to protect two days a month for it from January.

## History

She joined to run the Northvale design from day one, wrote the first matching draft within five weeks ([[northvale-matching-specification-v1]]), and replaced it two months later when the storage-time evidence convinced her that a twelve-month date tolerance was too loose. That revision is the clearest example of how she works: a written reason, a new version, the old one kept.
