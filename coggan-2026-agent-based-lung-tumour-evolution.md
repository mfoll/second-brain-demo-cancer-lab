# Agent-Based Simulations of Lung Tumor Evolution Suggest That Ongoing Cell Competition Drives Realistic Clonal Expansions.

Coggan H et al., Advanced Science, 2026.

## Summary

Tests three three-dimensional tumour growth simulations, differing in how cells compete, for their ability to reproduce the frequent late subclonal expansions seen in lung tumours. A two-stage model, with local competition for space and resources in a fixed-size tumour during the second stage, produced realistic multi-region data efficiently. Inferred selection effects of drivers were much larger under the two-stage model than under single-stage ones.

## Why It Matters

Second paper read for the keynote introduction. Its central point, that model assumptions drive the size of inferred selection, is the bridge Ines wants between [[katarina-vukovic]]'s keynote and the data sessions that follow. She plans one slide on it.

## Caveats
