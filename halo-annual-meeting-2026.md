# Halo Annual Meeting 2026

Host the Halo annual meeting at Corvane from 2026-06-10 to 2026-06-12. Corvane offered to host at the [[halo-steering-committee-2025-11-20]] because WP4 needed the whole consortium in one room to agree how the benchmark would be analysed.

## Outcome

Three-day meeting held at Corvane with all six sites, a WP4 session that produced agreed analysis decisions, and minutes circulated within two weeks. Reached on 2026-06-12; minutes circulated 2026-06-24.

## Current State

- Completed. 41 participants from six sites, plus two observers from the patient advisory panel, one of them [[anna-sorensen]].
- Decisions are recorded in [[halo-annual-meeting-2026-06-10]]; Ines's WP4 talk in [[halo-annual-meeting-2026-wp4-slides]].
- Costs closed at about 18,400 EUR against a 20,000 EUR WP1 meeting budget; the coordinating centre reimbursed the venue and catering in August.

## Scope and Boundaries

Logistics, programme and the WP4 session. Scientific follow-up moved back to [[halo-cross-site-benchmark]] after the meeting.

## Working Context

[[priya-raman]] ran logistics: the institute seminar room for plenaries, two breakout rooms, a lab tour of the cfDNA wet lab with [[elias-moreau]], and the dinner on the second evening. [[grace-tanaka]] wrote a short news item for the institute site. [[maximilian-auer]] and [[henrik-dahl]] set the plenary agenda; Ines set the WP4 day. The meeting fell three weeks after the truth-set shipment, so only pilot-panel data existed; the WP4 day was planned around that, with the commutability problem from [[halo-pilot-panel-results-2026-04]] as the main item.
