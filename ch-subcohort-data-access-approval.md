# CH Subcohort Data Access Approval

## Summary

The Northvale steering group's approval, dated 2025-10-02, for the [[ch-cancer-risk-analysis]]: buffy coat DNA from 6,000 randomly selected participants, baseline questionnaire data, and registry follow-up. It became the October 2025 annex to the [[northvale-collaboration-agreement]].

## Key Details

- Approved question: CH at baseline and incident solid cancer risk. CH and cardiovascular outcomes were explicitly excluded because another approved project holds that question.
- Sample release: up to 500 ng buffy coat DNA per participant; released in two batches by [[paolo-ferrante]] (November and December 2025).
- Return of derived CH calls to the cohort within 12 months of publication.
