# Are infectious diseases risk factors for sarcoidosis or a result of reverse causation? Findings from a population-based nested case-control study.

## Summary

A register-based nested case-control study of about 4,000 incident sarcoidosis cases and 40,000 matched controls, examining infections in the three years before diagnosis. Associations were estimated with conditional logistic regression and tested for robustness under assumptions of reverse causation bias, given that disease onset precedes diagnosis by an uncertain interval.

## Why It Matters

Maja used it in preparing the options for [[northvale-analysis-meeting-2026-06-24]]: it shows a nested case-control analysis that treats the time before diagnosis explicitly as a bias window and checks robustness by varying it, which is what our window and exclusion sensitivity analyses do.

## Caveats

Different disease and exposure; register data rather than biomarkers.
