# 'Potato potahto'? Disentangling de-identification, anonymisation, and pseudonymisation for health research in Africa

Edgcumbe A et al. Journal of Law and the Biosciences, 2025.

## Summary

A legal analysis of how the terms de-identification, anonymisation and pseudonymisation are used in health research, arguing that they are often treated as interchangeable when they carry different legal consequences.

## Why It Matters

[[yara-castellanos]] used it while writing the definitions section of draft 1 of the [[data-access-policy-revision]] in April 2026. The paper's main point, that loose terms lead to wrong legal treatment, is the reason the policy now bans "de-identified" in board papers and defines three categories instead ([[pseudonymisation-vs-anonymisation]]).

## Caveats

Written for a different legal context; we use it for the conceptual distinction, not for any jurisdiction-specific rule.
