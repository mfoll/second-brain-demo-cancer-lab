# Halo Truth Set Draft Plan 2025

Archived. Superseded by [[halo-truth-set-design-document]] (design v2, approved 2026-02-26). Kept because the rejected reasoning still comes up.

## What It Proposed

Drafted by Ines and [[tomasz-wilk]] in October 2025: a contrived-only truth set of 60 samples (mutation dilutions at six VAF levels, methylation fractions, no plasma), cheap to make, unlimited in volume, and free of any data-protection question because it contained no personal data.

## Why It Was Rejected

At the [[halo-steering-committee-2025-11-20]], [[sun-hee-park]] argued that a benchmark without patient plasma could not answer the consortium's clinical question and would favour assays tuned to clean dilutions. The committee agreed five to one. In hindsight the draft would also have hidden the commutability problem for fragmentomics entirely ([[commutability-of-reference-materials]]), and its one attraction, avoiding data-protection questions, simply moved them to later.

## What Survived

The dilution design and VAF levels, reused in the contrived tier of the [[halo-truth-set]].
