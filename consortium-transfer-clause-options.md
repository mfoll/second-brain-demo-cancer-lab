# Consortium Transfer Clause Options

The three wording options considered for the consortium transfer clause (section 5A) of the [[data-access-policy-revision]], written up for [[data-governance-board-2026-05-14]] and kept current since.

## Options

| Option | Mechanism | Who liked it | Problem |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Board approves the consortium agreement once; use within the consortium's stated aims needs no further approval | Halo coordination, most users | Stated aims can be broad; [[victor-lindahl]] could not assess risk per use |
| B | Board approves the agreement once; each use approved by a [[data-access-committee]] function within 20 working days | Ruth, Victor, Ines | Needs a standing DAC and a terms of reference change |
| C | Keep bilateral agreements, add a standard template to speed them up | Two group leaders | Does not allow pooled environments; Halo needs one |

## Choice

Draft 2 carried option A; draft 3 moved to option B after Victor's review and [[eva-novak]]'s note that Halo sites run per-use review anyway ([[halo-data-sharing-agreement-template]]). Option C was rejected at the May board because it does not solve the pooled environment.

## Open Details For Draft 4

- Whether the DAC function can approve a use that includes registry-derived variables, or must refer it to the full board.
- Deletion within 90 days with written confirmation, as the board asked at [[data-governance-board-2026-09-10]].
