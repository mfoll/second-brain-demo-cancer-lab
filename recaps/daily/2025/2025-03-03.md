# Monday 3 March 2025

## Atlas kickoff

The [[colorectal-metastasis-atlas|Colorectal Metastasis Atlas]] started with Amara, Benedikt and Rafael from the sequencing core. Scope is 40 liver metastases with paired pre- and post-chemotherapy tissue; the platform question was deliberately left open and handed to a pilot. Detail and owners are in the [[atlas-kickoff-2025-03-03|kickoff note]].

## Visium HD pilot and its stop rule

The [[visium-hd-pilot|Visium HD Pilot]] began the same day. We wrote the stop rule in the room rather than after the data came in, which felt slow at the time but is the part worth keeping: see [[pilots-need-a-written-stop-rule|Pilots need a written stop rule]]. Standard Visium stays as the fallback.

## Money and sequencer time

Until the [[frontier-starting-grant|Frontier Starting Grant]] decision, only pilot spending. Rafael's warning about autumn competition for the sequencer with cfDNA runs was the main practical point; we agreed to book pilot runs early.
