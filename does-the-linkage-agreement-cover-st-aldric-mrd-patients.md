# Does The Linkage Agreement Cover St Aldric MRD Patients?

## Current Answer

> [!success]
> Yes. The agreement signed on 2025-10-31 names both the Northvale Health Cohort and the St Aldric MRD study as covered populations, and the St Aldric consent form explicitly covers follow-up through national health registers.

Answered on 2025-11-06 after [[samuel-okoye]] asked whether the MRD survival analysis could use registry deaths. Checked against [[registry-linkage-agreement-summary]] and [[st-aldric-consent-wording]], and confirmed by [[helena-strom]].

## Limits

- Coverage is for linkage and analysis inside the institute. Sending registry-derived variables to Halo is a separate question ([[can-we-send-pseudonymised-cfdna-data-to-halo-sites]]).
- Recurrence is not registered. The registry adds deaths and second primaries only; recurrence dates still come from St Aldric records.
- Patients recruited under a future consent version would need to be checked again.

## Where It Was Used

Vital status for all 212 patients in the [[mrd-manuscript]], from the initial St Aldric extract delivered on 2026-01-22.
