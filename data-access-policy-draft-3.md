# Data Access Policy Revision Draft 3

## Summary

The draft discussed at [[data-governance-board-2026-09-10]]. It revises [[corvane-data-access-policy-2023]] and adds a consortium transfer clause (section 5A) using option B from [[consortium-transfer-clause-options]]. Not adopted; draft 4 is due 2026-10-29 for the board on 2026-11-12.

## Why It Matters

Adoption of this clause, in this or a revised form, is what lets Corvane sign the Halo agreement, receive patient-tier data from the [[halo-cross-site-benchmark]] and contribute St Aldric data, and therefore unblocks the external validation for the [[mrd-manuscript]].

## Key Details

- Section 5A.1: the institute may join a consortium agreement that permits transfer of pseudonymised person-level data among named members, in both directions, approved once by the board.
- 5A.2: each use within the consortium needs approval by a [[data-access-committee]] function of the board, target decision within 20 working days.
- 5A.3: minimum dataset per use. No direct identifiers, no exact dates (time from surgery or blood draw in days), no free text.
- 5A.4: hosting in a consortium secure environment is allowed if the host site has been assessed in the DPIA.
- 5A.5: no onward transfer outside the named members; deletion at project end.
- 5A.6: registry-derived variables only with the registry's written consent.
- Section 1 now defines pseudonymised, aggregate and anonymous data ([[pseudonymisation-vs-anonymisation]]).

## Board Requests For Draft 4

1. DPIA section assessing receiving sites ([[consortium-transfer-dpia-draft]]).
2. Written registry position on 5A.6 from [[daniel-asante]].
3. Deletion within 90 days of project end, with written confirmation; physical samples explicitly excluded.
